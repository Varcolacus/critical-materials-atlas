# Antimony chain — research library

Antimony's mine map is diversifying (China's mine share fell from ~84% to ~43% as Tajikistan and Russia grew), yet it is more of a chokepoint than ever, because China dominates refining and antimony trioxide, and its September 2024 export controls turned a flame-retardant and munitions metal into a strategic lever. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Antimony](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-antimony.pdf) — USGS (2026). Latest annual US/world antimony production, reserves, and trade data.
- [BGS, World Mineral Statistics — antimony mine production](https://www2.bgs.ac.uk/mineralsuk/statistics/worldStatistics.html) — British Geological Survey (2024). Country-level mine-production time series used for the chain's history chart.
- [CSIS, China's antimony export restrictions and the impact on U.S. national security](https://www.csis.org/analysis/chinas-antimony-export-restrictions-impact-us-national-security) — CSIS (2024). Analysis of the 2024 export-control episode and its defence-supply implications.
- [CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. Bilateral trade database used for the chain's unwrought antimony and antimony oxide flows.

## Government & agency reports

- [Mineral Commodity Summaries 2024 — Antimony](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2024/mcs2024-antimony.pdf) — USGS (2024). Pre-export-control-year baseline for comparison against 2025/2026 editions.
- [Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 — Antimony](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025-antimony.pdf) — USGS (2025). Covers the year the export controls hit.
- [U.S. Geological Survey Minerals Yearbook World Production Data for Antimony, Bauxite, and Alumina 2010–2022](https://www.usgs.gov/data/us-geological-survey-minerals-yearbook-world-production-data-antimony-bauxite-and-alumina-2010) — USGS Science Data Catalog. ## Academic papers & reviews
- [Global antimony supply risk assessment through the industry chain](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/energy-research/articles/10.3389/fenrg.2022.1007260/full) — Frontiers in Energy Research (2022). Supply-risk assessment across the antimony industry chain, geographic concentration and political-instability factors.
- [Resilience in the antimony supply chain](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0921344922004219) — Resources, Conservation and Recycling / ScienceDirect (2022). Analyses antimony supply disruptions 1913–2018 and resilience/price-feedback mechanisms.
- [Mapping the evolution of global antimony competition pattern: a network-based analysis](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/earth-science/articles/10.3389/feart.2026.1888343/full) — Frontiers in Earth Science (2026). Network analysis of shifting country-level antimony trade/production competition.
- [Rethinking antimony: Supply vulnerability, waste flows, and recovery pathways](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1557/s43578-026-01840-7) — Journal of Materials Research / Springer Nature (2026). Secondary-supply and recycling pathways as a vulnerability mitigant.

## Gaps & good next searches
- No peer-reviewed life-cycle-assessment (LCA) or material-flow-analysis (MFA) study specific to antimony trioxide/flame-retardant end use was surfaced — search "antimony trioxide material flow analysis" or "antimony life cycle assessment flame retardant" in MDPI Minerals/Resources or Journal of Cleaner Production.
- No IEA-specific antimony report or dataset turned up (IEA's critical-minerals coverage tends to focus on energy-transition metals; antimony is more dual-use/defence) — worth checking IEA's Critical Minerals Data Explorer directly for an antimony line item.
- No OECD or NEA antimony-specific publication found — likely thin coverage; antimony's civil-nuclear use (noted in the chain) may be covered obliquely in NEA fuel-cycle or non-proliferation materials work, worth a targeted search.
- Refining-stage data (China's antimony-trioxide production/export share specifically, as opposed to mine share) is asserted qualitatively across sources but a hard quantified figure with a citable primary source was not directly located — worth pulling from USGS MCS's refined-antimony trade tables or a Roskill/Wood Mackenzie/Benchmark Mineral Intelligence antimony report (paywalled, not searched here).
