Research Library · Antimony

Antimony — flame-retardant and munitions metal chain

A curated reference library of public sources for deeper study — gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source and carries an abstract (or factual summary) plus the data it rests on. A research-support layer of the Critical Materials Atlas.

Antimony's mine map is spreading — China's share fell from ~84% to ~43% as Tajikistan and Russia grew — yet it became more of a chokepoint, not less, because refining and the trade in antimony trioxide stayed in China, which in September 2024 turned a flame-retardant and munitions metal into a strategic lever. The mine diversified; the control did not.

Ordered to follow the chain: ① Mine (stibnite — China ↓, Tajikistan / Russia ↑) → ② Refining & antimony trioxide (China-dominated) → ③ End uses (flame retardants + lead-acid batteries + munitions / primers) → ④ the September-2024 export-control lever and the Western response — then the cross-cutting frameworks and trade data that sit across every stage.

③ The September 2024 export-control lever 5

The concentrated refined stage is where China placed the licence — the same playbook as gallium, germanium and graphite.
  • China's antimony export restrictions and the impact on U.S. national security · CSIS (2024) Analysis of the September 2024 export-control episode and its defence-supply implications. Abstract. CSIS analysis of China's 15 Sep 2024 licensing of six antimony products (ore, metals, oxide, gold-antimony smelting tech). China supplies 48% of output and 63% of US imports; even Tajik and Australian ore is refined in China. Prices doubled to a record ~$22,000/t; the US has produced no antimony since 2001. Data. CSIS synthesis of USGS production / import figures, trade data and market-price reporting.
  • China Imposes Its Most Stringent Critical Minerals Export Restrictions Yet · CSIS (2024) Places the antimony controls in the broader gallium / germanium / graphite export-control sequence. Summary. CSIS places the antimony controls in China's retaliation sequence — gallium / germanium (Jul 2023), graphite (Oct 2023), then the Dec 2024 country-specific ban on antimony, gallium and germanium to the US. After Sep 2024, antimony shipments fell ~97% and prices rose ~200%. Data. CSIS analysis of USGS, trade and market-price data.
  • China's Export Controls: Critical Minerals and Strategic Pressure Points · Andersen Institute Broader framing of antimony within China's critical-minerals export-control architecture. Summary. The Andersen Institute maps China's export-control architecture — the 2020 Export Control Law and 2024 dual-use rules, with control lists, temporary controls and catch-all provisions — and traces antimony within a 2020–2026 escalation; antimony exports fell ~97% after Aug 2024 and prices roughly doubled. Data. Synthesis of USGS, Census Bureau, academic and press sources; firm-level evidence noted as limited.
  • Smuggling of antimony: China launches crackdown · Fastmarkets (2025) Market reporting on enforcement and circumvention of the export controls. Summary. Fastmarkets (May 2025): China's Commerce Ministry announced a crackdown on illegal strategic-mineral exports — false documentation and third-country transshipment were used to evade the Sep-2024 antimony controls. Rotterdam grade-II antimony ran from ~$22,461/t (Jul 2024) to ~$57,778/t (Apr 2025). Data. Fastmarkets twice-weekly antimony price assessments (MMTA standard grade II, in-warehouse Rotterdam).
  • Uncertainty lingers over antimony exports following US-China truce · Fastmarkets (2025) Follow-up on the 2025 partial suspension / status of the export ban after the truce. Summary. Fastmarkets (Nov 2025): after the Trump–Xi truce, no official change to antimony export rules; licensing and intensified customs inspections since May 2025 left most exporters suspended and containers held at ports — a resumption of US-bound exports would move prices materially. Data. Fastmarkets reporting and market-participant interviews; policy timeline from Aug 2024 onward.

The EU JRC's Critical Raw Materials 2020 factsheets — the antimony factsheet covers EU import reliance (sourced almost entirely via imports, historically led by Turkey), global production shares, uses and supply risk. The 1MB+ PDF exceeded the fetch size limit on this pass; figures were not quoted directly. Data. EU criticality assessment (production, trade, end-use, substitution and recycling data).
  • China's Antimony Export Controls: Risks and Opportunities for the EU · European Commission JRC (2025) JRC briefing on the EU exposure created by China's 2024 antimony export controls. Summary. JRC (Jun 2025), by Ingoglia & Manfredi: an economic and trade analysis of the Aug-2024 restrictions on a material essential to EU defence (armour-piercing rounds, night vision, IR sensors), semiconductors and batteries — framing the controls as both risk and reshoring opportunity for the EU. Data. JRC in-depth economic and trade-related analysis of China's antimony controls.
  • Global antimony supply risk assessment through the industry chain · Frontiers in Energy Research (2022) Supply-risk assessment across the antimony industry chain and its concentration. Abstract. Builds a Supply-Risk Index for antimony across the industry chain from five factors — market concentration (HHI), governance, social stability, innovation and trade-network betweenness — over 2011–2019 UN Comtrade data for seven antimony products across twelve countries (~80% of trade); finds mining-country instability and China's midstream dominance amplify the concentrated-chain risk. Data. UN Comtrade 2011–2019, seven antimony products; composite index (HHI, WGI, HDI, GII, network centrality).
  • Resilience in the antimony supply chain · Resources, Conservation and Recycling (2022) Analyses antimony supply disruptions and resilience / price-feedback mechanisms. Summary. Resources, Conservation & Recycling (2022): analyses antimony supply disruptions across 1913–2018 and the price-feedback and resilience mechanisms that follow shocks. ScienceDirect full text is paywalled (403) on this pass; the abstract was not retrieved. Data. Long-run (1913–2018) antimony supply-disruption and price series.
  • Mapping the evolution of global antimony competition pattern: a network-based analysis · Frontiers in Earth Science (2026) Network analysis of shifting country-level antimony trade / production competition. Abstract. Network analysis of global antimony competition, 2015–2024 (UN Comtrade, five product categories in antimony-content equivalent). Builds import-competition networks with an improved intensity index; finds China's exports fell 44% while Tajikistan (+108%) and Myanmar (+377%) rose, and China alone faces intensifying competition through >90% ore-import dependence. Data. UN Comtrade 2015–2024, five antimony product categories; weighted directed trade-network model.
  • Rethinking antimony: Supply vulnerability, waste flows, and recovery pathways · Journal of Materials Research (2026) Secondary-supply and recycling pathways as a vulnerability mitigant. Summary. Journal of Materials Research (2026), "Rethinking antimony: supply vulnerability, waste flows and recovery pathways" — frames secondary supply and recycling as a mitigant to antimony supply risk. Springer full text sits behind an authentication redirect; the abstract was not retrieved on this pass. Data. Material-flow / secondary-supply synthesis (per the title and journal listing).
