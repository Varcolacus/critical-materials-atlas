Manganese chain

A curated reference library of public sources for deeper study — gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source and carries an abstract (or factual summary) plus the data it rests on. A research-support layer of the Critical Materials Atlas.

Manganese is two metals in one. Its ore is mined across South Africa, Gabon and Australia — a genuinely diversified map — and about 90% of it goes into steel, where it is irreplaceable. But the high-purity manganese sulphate and electrolytic metal that batteries need is refined overwhelmingly in China. The mine is the safe stage; the refinery is the chokepoint.

Ordered to follow the chain: ① the diversified ore (South Africa, Gabon, Australia) → ② steel ferroalloy and the battery-grade sulphate / EMM refining — the ~90%-China chokepoint — then the industry body and the trade / reference data that sit across every stage.

① Mine — the diversified ore 5

Spread across South Africa, Gabon and Australia — the lowest-risk link in the chain, and ~90% of it goes to steel.
  • USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Manganese · USGS (2026) Annual world mine-production, reserves, import-reliance and end-use data for manganese. Abstract. USGS annual commodity summary for manganese — essential and irreplaceable in steelmaking for its sulphur-fixing, deoxidising and alloying properties, with steel taking ~85–90% of US demand through ferro-manganese and silico-manganese; no domestic mine output and near-total import reliance, chiefly South African and Gabonese ore. (PDF returned HTTP 403 to automated fetch this pass; scope drawn from the USGS manganese portal and Mineral Industry Surveys.) Data. USGS annual producer canvass plus reported trade, price and consumption data.
  • BGS World Mineral Statistics — manganese mine production · BGS (2024) Long-run world mine-production and trade series by commodity and country. Summary. The British Geological Survey's World Mineral Statistics is a multi-decade compilation of national mine-production and trade figures by commodity and country — the source of the atlas's long-run manganese mine-share series. (Portal returned HTTP 404 this pass and could not be re-read; the series is already extracted into the atlas data.) Data. BGS compilation of national mine-production and trade statistics by commodity and country, multi-decade.
  • USGS Minerals Yearbook 2023 — Manganese · USGS (2023) Detailed annual industry structure and end-use breakdown for manganese. Summary. The USGS Minerals Yearbook manganese chapter is the detailed annual companion to the MCS: US and world production, ferro-manganese and silico-manganese output, consumption by end use, trade and industry structure. (PDF returned HTTP 403 to automated fetch this pass — full text not retrievable.) Data. USGS annual industry survey — production, consumption, trade and stocks for manganese ore and ferroalloys.
  • USGS Mineral Industry Surveys — Manganese, January 2026 · USGS (2026) Monthly manganese production, import-sourcing and ore-price survey. Summary. USGS monthly survey: US manganese-ore imports fell to 1,120 t in January 2026 (from 41,800 t in December 2025); South Africa was the sole source of lower-grade ore and Gabon led high-grade; 44%-grade ore averaged $4.87 per mtu delivered to China. Data. USGS monthly Mineral Industry Survey — US Census import/export data, spot ore prices and industry news.
  • USGS Professional Paper 1802 — Critical Mineral Resources of the United States (Manganese, chapter L) · USGS Manganese chapter of the USGS critical-mineral-resources compendium. Summary. Chapter L of the USGS "Critical Mineral Resources of the United States" compendium covers manganese — geology, deposit types, domestic and world resources, and the long-standing US import dependence on foreign ore and ferroalloy. (Landing page and PDF returned HTTP 403 to automated fetch this pass — abstract not retrievable.) Data. USGS geological assessment — resource/reserve compilation and deposit descriptions.

② Alloy & refine — steel ferroalloy, then the battery-grade chokepoint 1

Where the diversified metal narrows: high-purity manganese sulphate and electrolytic metal are made overwhelmingly in China.
  • IEA — Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2025 (executive summary) · IEA (2025) Global assessment of critical-mineral supply and refining concentration, including battery-grade manganese. Abstract. IEA outlook on critical-mineral supply concentration: the top-three refiners' average share rose from ~82% (2020) to ~86% (2024), with China producing ~95% of high-purity manganese sulphate; announced projects would meet only ~55% of expected 2035 battery-grade manganese demand. Diversification, it argues, needs policy support, not market forces alone. Data. IEA supply-chain modelling of announced projects, production and refining capacity across critical minerals.

Industry body & market data 2

The manganese industry's own statistics — mostly member-gated, with one public report.
  • International Manganese Institute (IMnI) · IMnI The leading industry body for manganese ore and ferroalloy producers. Summary. The International Manganese Institute (founded 1975, Paris) is the leading industry body for manganese ore and ferroalloy producers, publishing monthly and annual market research from member data and running statistics, safety and market committees. Data. Member-reported production, capacity, stock and consumption statistics (mostly member-gated).
  • IMnI — 2013 Public Annual Market Research Report · IMnI The Institute's one publicly released market research report (most current data is member-only). Summary. IMnI's one publicly released annual report: 2001–2013 series for steel output, high-carbon and refined ferro-manganese, silico-manganese and manganese ore — trade matrices, capacities, production, utilisation, stocks and supply-demand balance (steel data from the World Steel Association and CRU). Data. IMnI member survey plus World Steel Association / CRU steel-production figures, 2001–2013.

Across the chain — reference & trade data 3

The indexes and datasets the chain's own figures are built from.
  • USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 (main volume) · USGS (2026) Cross-commodity reserves/production reference covering ~90 minerals including manganese. Summary. The USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 is the annual cross-commodity reference — a PDF chapter per mineral plus reserves, production and import-reliance tables spanning ~90 commodities including manganese. (Landing page returned HTTP 403 to automated fetch this pass.) Data. USGS annual mineral statistics compiled from producer surveys and trade data.
  • USGS — Manganese Statistics and Information · USGS NMIC Portal to all annual and monthly USGS manganese data releases. Summary. The USGS National Minerals Information Center's manganese portal links every data release — MCS (1996–2026), Minerals Yearbook (1994–2023) and monthly Mineral Industry Surveys — and confirms steel takes ~85–90% of US manganese demand via ferro-manganese and silico-manganese ferroalloys. Data. Index/portal to USGS manganese production, trade and consumption releases.
  • CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade · CEPII Harmonized bilateral trade database (HS6, 1995–2024) used for the chain's trade table. Summary. CEPII's BACI reconstructs reconciled bilateral trade flows for ~200 countries and ~5,000 HS6 products (1995–2024) from UN Comtrade, removing CIF freight and reliability-weighting reporter discrepancies into one figure per flow — the atlas's trade engine (manganese ore 260200, ferro-manganese 720211). Data. UN Comtrade import/export declarations, HS6 bilateral, mirror-reconciled (CIF/FOB, reporter-reliability weighting).

Gaps & good next searches

  • The ~90% China battery-grade refining claim is now partly pinned — the IEA Outlook gives China ~95% of high-purity manganese sulphate — but a plant-level capacity number from Fastmarkets or Benchmark Mineral Intelligence would harden the electrolytic-manganese-metal (EMM) + sulphate figure. Search "battery-grade manganese sulphate China capacity" and "high-manganese LMFP cathode Benchmark."
  • No peer-reviewed paper on battery-grade manganese sulphate / EMM concentration surfaced with a verifiable URL this pass — try "manganese sulphate battery grade supply chain criticality" and "electrolytic manganese metal China environmental Journal of Cleaner Production."
  • IMnI's detailed monthly/annual production data is member-gated; the USGS Minerals Yearbook manganese tables and BACI (HS 260200 ore + 720211 ferro-manganese) are the public-data substitute already used in the chain's trade panel.
  • No EU JRC Critical Raw Materials factsheet for manganese was located this pass — worth checking single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu directly for a manganese-specific factsheet.
  • The USGS pubs.usgs.gov PDFs and the BGS World Mineral Statistics portal refused automated fetch (HTTP 403/404) this pass; their figures are already extracted into the atlas data, but the live URLs are worth re-verifying by hand.
