# Manganese chain — research library

Manganese's ore is genuinely diversified (South Africa, Gabon, Australia) and irreplaceable in steelmaking, but the high-purity manganese sulphate and electrolytic manganese metal that batteries need is refined overwhelmingly in China. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Manganese](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-manganese.pdf) — USGS (2026). Mine production, reserves, world output by country.
- [IEA, Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2025](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2025/executive-summary) — IEA (2025). Refining-stage concentration context.
- [BGS, World Mineral Statistics — manganese mine production](https://www2.bgs.ac.uk/mineralsuk/statistics/worldStatistics.html) — BGS (2024). Long-run mine-production series by country.
- [CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. Harmonised bilateral trade database used for the atlas's own flows.

## Government & agency reports
- [Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 (main volume)](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/mcs2026) — USGS (2026). Cross-commodity reserves/production reference.
- [USGS Minerals Yearbook 2023 — Manganese](https://pubs.usgs.gov/myb/vol1/2023/myb1-2023-manganese.pdf) — USGS (2023). Detailed annual industry structure and end-use breakdown.
- [USGS — Mineral Industry Surveys (Manganese), Jan 2026](https://d9-wret.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/assets/palladium/production/s3fs-public/media/files/mis-202601-manga.pdf) — USGS. Monthly production/trade survey.
- [USGS Professional Paper 1802 — Critical Mineral Resources of the United States (Manganese chapter)](https://doi.org/10.3133/pp1802L) — USGS. Manganese-specific chapter of the critical-minerals compendium.
