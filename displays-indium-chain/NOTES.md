# Displays/indium interpretation notes

## The claim
The screen chain has a trace-input chokepoint: indium (a zinc by-product, ~70% refined in China,
export-controlled 2025) via ITO. The relief is ITO-scrap recycling (Japan/Korea).

## Claims rejected / limits stated
- **No indium trade series.** Indium sits in the shared 811292 basket with Ga/Ge and cannot be
  isolated; trade is shown as flagged context only, never as an indium measure.
- **No long production series.** Public indium-production-by-country history is thin; the page uses
  current snapshots and the 2025 policy event, and says so (no invented series).
- **"ITO is irreplaceable."** Softened. Nanotube/nanowire/metal-mesh substitutes are researched but
  not adopted at scale.

## Rules reused
Refined-share for the concentrated stage; policy event + trade response for the lever; recycling
stated as the relief; every gap (no series, no clean code) shown rather than papered over.
