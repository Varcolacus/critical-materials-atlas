# Displays / indium evidence ledger

- **Refining** — USGS MCS 2025/26: China ~70% (recent data ~68%) of world refined indium.
- **By-product** — USGS: indium is recovered from zinc-ore (sphalerite) processing residues
  (~1–100 ppm); no primary indium mine — supply is coupled to zinc.
- **Export control** — USGS MCS 2026: China placed indium under export restrictions in Feb 2025;
  unwrought-indium exports fell ~72% YoY, Sep 2024 → Sep 2025.
- **Use** — USGS: indium tin oxide (ITO) for flat-panel displays (LCDs, touchscreens) is the largest
  end use; nanotube / Ag-Cu nanowire substitutes explored but not adopted at scale.
- **Recycling** — USGS: indium is most commonly recovered from ITO manufacturing scrap in Japan and
  the Republic of Korea; general end-use/recycling data are limited.
- **Trade context** — CEPII BACI V202601, HS 811292 (shared Ga/Ge/In basket — indium NOT separable),
  901380 (LCD devices), 2002–2024. Flagged context only.
