# Displays / indium chain — research library

Every screen you touch runs on a metal no one mines on purpose: indium is a trace zinc by-product, refining is ~70% China, and China placed it under export control in February 2025. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Indium](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-indium.pdf) — USGS (2026). Latest annual production, reserves and China-share figures.
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 — Indium](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025-indium.pdf) — USGS (2025). Prior-year comparison; source for the ITO/Japan-Korea recycling line.
- [CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. The trade database behind the chain's flagged HS 811292 basket context.

## Government & agency reports
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2024 — Indium](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2024/mcs2024-indium.pdf) — USGS (2024). Earlier-year baseline for production and price series.
- [USGS — Indium statistics and information](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/indium-statistics-and-information) — USGS National Minerals Information Center. Landing page for historical indium data series.
- [Mineral Commodity Profile: Indium](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2004/1300/2004-1300.pdf) — USGS Open-File Report 2004-1300, Jorgenson & George. Long-form profile of geology, production, uses and history.
- [SCRREEN — Indium CRM 2020 Factsheet](https://scrreen.eu/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/INDIUM_CRM_2020_Factsheets_critical_Final.pdf) — EU SCRREEN project (2020). EU criticality factsheet: supply chain, substitution, recycling.
- [Study on the EU's list of Critical Raw Materials (2020) — Final Report](https://ec.europa.eu/docsroom/documents/42883/attachments/1/translations/en/renditions/native) — European Commission / JRC (2020). EU-wide criticality methodology and indium supply-risk scoring (China ~48% of global supply in this dataset).
- [RMIS — More on Critical Raw Materials](https://rmis.jrc.ec.europa.eu/eu-critical-raw-materials) — EU JRC Raw Materials Information System. Portal for EU CRM criticality factsheets, including indium.
- [Critical Mineral Resources: National Policy and Critical Minerals List](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R47982) — Congressional Research Service (2024/25). US policy framing for critical minerals lists that include indium.
- [Emergency Access to Strategic and Critical Materials: The National Defense Stockpile](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R47833) — Congressional Research Service. Background on the US stockpile mechanism referenced by DLA indium-stockpiling proposals.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [The world's by-product and critical metal resources part III: A global assessment of indium](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0169136816300932) — Werner et al., Ore Geology Reviews / ScienceDirect. Global inventory of indium in mineral deposits (~356,000 t across 1,512 deposits).
- [Looking Down Under for a Circular Economy of Indium](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/abs/10.1021/acs.est.7b05022) — Environmental Science & Technology. Circular-economy / recycling-potential study of indium flows.
- [Modeling Indium Extraction, Supply, Price, Use and Recycling 1930–2200 Using the WORLD7 Model](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11053-023-10296-z) — Natural Resources Research / Springer Nature. Long-run system-dynamics model of indium supply and price.
- [Selecting and prioritizing material resources by criticality assessments](https://www.cell.com/one-earth/fulltext/S2590-3322(21)00111-1) — One Earth / Cell Press. Criticality-methodology review that situates indium among by-product critical metals.
