# Copper-chain interpretation notes

## The claim
Copper's risk is not a single-country chokepoint (mining is spread and diversifying) but a
demand-supply gap over time: ~30% demand growth to 2040 vs a mine pipeline pointing to a ~30%
shortfall by 2035, because mines take 10–20 years and ore grades are falling.

## Claims rejected
- **"A country controls copper."** Rejected at the mine — five countries mine ~62% and the
  leader (Chile) is losing share. The one concentrated STAGE is refining (China), shown separately.
- **"The shortfall is certain."** Softened. The 30% figures are IEA projections (demand and mine
  pipeline), marked as estimates, not measured facts.
- **"Recycling closes the gap."** Rejected as sufficient — recycling grows and helps but cannot
  close a deficit of this size on this timeline.

## Rules reused
Mine share + 2000–2024 history for the (diversifying) base; refining share for the concentrated
stage; IEA demand-vs-pipeline as an explicitly-marked projection; trade as hub availability.
