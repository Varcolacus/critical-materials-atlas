# Copper chain — research library

The copper chokepoint is a clock, not a map: mining is spread across a diversifying set of countries, but electrification demand is set to outrun a mine pipeline that takes 10-20 years to build — a projected ~30% supply shortfall by 2035. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Copper](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-copper.pdf) — USGS (2026). Annual mine production, reserves, US/world shares.
- [IEA, Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2025 — Overview of outlook for key minerals](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2025/overview-of-outlook-for-key-minerals) — IEA (2025). Demand/supply-gap outlook by mineral, incl. copper.
- [IEA, Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2025 — Executive summary](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2025/executive-summary) — IEA (2025). Refining-concentration and shortfall headline figures.
- [CEPII BACI V202601](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII, based on UN Comtrade (2026). Bilateral trade database used for the chain's trade panel.

## Government & agency reports
- [Copper Statistics and Information](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/copper-statistics-and-information) — USGS National Minerals Information Center. World supply/demand/material-flow data; links to "Copper – A Metal for the Ages" (Fact Sheet 2009-3031), "Estimate of Undiscovered Copper Resources of the World, 2013," and World Copper Smelters map/table.
- [Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2025 — Executive summary](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2025/executive-summary) — IEA (2025, already core). States the ~30% supply-shortfall-by-2035 figure the chain leads with, and that refining concentration worsened 2020–2024.

## Databases & data sources
- [International Copper Study Group (ICSG)](https://icsg.org/) — ICSG. Intergovernmental body maintaining the primary copper statistical database; publishes the [World Copper Factbook](https://icsg.org/copper-factbook/), a Copper Statistical Yearbook, a Directory of Copper Mines/Smelters/Refineries, and a Monthly Copper Bulletin (see [publications list](https://icsg.org/publications-list/)).
- [Copper Statistics and Information](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/copper-statistics-and-information) — USGS. Primary US production/reserves/trade data portal.

## Industry & trade associations
- [International Copper Association](https://internationalcopper.org/) — ICA ("the voice of copper"). Global trade association; see its [Copper Demand and Long-Term Availability](https://internationalcopper.org/sustainable-copper/about-copper/cu-demand-long-term-availability/) page and [2025 Annual Report](https://internationalcopper.org/2025annualreport/).
- [International Copper Study Group (ICSG)](https://icsg.org/) — intergovernmental industry/market body (also listed under Databases above).
