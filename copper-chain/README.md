# Copper chain pilot (unpublished)

The metal that electrifies everything. Unlike the other chains, copper's chokepoint is
temporal, not geographic: mining is spread across Chile, DRC, Peru, China and Russia and is
even diversifying, but electrification demand is set to outrun supply — and a new mine takes
10–20 years to build. Isolated on `wip/value-chains`; not linked from the live Atlas.

## The finding
The copper chokepoint is a clock, not a map. Demand rises ~30% by 2040 (grids, EVs, data
centres) while the mine pipeline points to a ~30% shortfall by 2035 (IEA), against falling ore
grades and 10–20-year mine lead times. The one concentrated STAGE is refining (China); the
mine base is diversified.
