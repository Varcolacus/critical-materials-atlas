# Copper evidence ledger

- **Mining** — USGS MCS 2026: world mine production ~23 Mt (2025); top five (Chile, DR Congo,
  Peru, China, Russia) ~62%; Chile the leader but losing share; ore grades declining.
- **Refining** — USGS/IEA: China is the largest producer of refined copper (concentrate → cathode)
  and, via grid build-out, the largest driver of recent demand growth.
- **Balance / outlook** — IEA Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2025: copper demand ~+30% by 2040
  (electrification, EVs, grids, data centres); mine pipeline points to a ~30% shortfall by 2035,
  a deficit potentially >6 Mt/yr in the early 2030s; mine lead times 10–20 years. Projections, not facts.
- **Demand drivers** — IEA: grid expansion is the single largest; a BEV uses ~3–4× the copper of a
  petrol car; data-centre/AI power distribution is a fast-growing new load.
- **History** — BGS/USGS mine series (mined_years.json): copper Chile/DR Congo/Peru 2000–2024.
- **Trade context** — CEPII BACI V202601, HS 260300 (ore), 740311 (cathode), 740811 (wire),
  740400 (scrap), 2002–2024. Availability of the traded form, via hubs; not the mine base.
