# From silicon to chips — research library

The chokepoint moves as silicon becomes a chip: China leads basic silicon, but the scarce electronic-grade purification (US/Germany), blank wafers (Japan) and leading-edge fabrication (Taiwan) sit elsewhere. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [IEA-PVPS, Trends in Photovoltaic Applications 2024](https://iea-pvps.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/IEA-PVPS-Task-1-Trends-Report-2024.pdf) — IEA-PVPS (2024). Global PV/polysilicon volumes; source for the 2023 semiconductor-use polysilicon figure.
- [Due Diligence for Responsible Sand and Silicate Supply Chains](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/due-diligence-for-responsible-sand-and-silicate-supply-chains_7be0093e-en/full-report/component-5.html) — OECD (2026). Silicon-metal production shares and silicate-chain concentration.
- [Mapping the Semiconductor Value Chain](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/mapping-the-semiconductor-value-chain_4154cdbf-en.html) — OECD (2025). Stage-by-stage map of the semiconductor value chain.
- [The Chip Landscape: Geographical Distribution of Wafer Fabrication Capacity](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/the-chip-landscape_02dbd028-en/full-report/component-6.html) — OECD (2025). Fab capacity by economy and chip type; source for the 2025 by-type table.
- [SIA Comments on the Polysilicon Section 232 Investigation](https://www.semiconductors.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Semiconductor-Industry-Association-SIA-Comments-Polysilicon-Section-232-Investigation.pdf) — Semiconductor Industry Association (2025). Electronic-grade polysilicon suppliers, concentration and the solar/semiconductor trade-data caveat.
- [Emerging Resilience in the Semiconductor Supply Chain](https://www.semiconductors.org/emerging-resilience-in-the-semiconductor-supply-chain/) — SIA / Boston Consulting Group (2024). Fab-capacity geography 1990–2032F; source for the long history chart.
- [SEMI Reports 2025 Annual Worldwide Silicon Wafer Shipments and Revenue Results](https://www.semi.org/en/semi-press-release/semi-reports-2025-annual-worldwide-silicon-wafer-shipments-and-revenue-results) — SEMI (2026). Latest annual blank-wafer area and revenue (semiconductor-only).
- [Worldwide Silicon Wafer Shipments and Revenue Start Recovery in Late 2024](https://www.semi.org/en/semi-press-release/worldwide-silicon-wafer-shipments-and-revenue-start-recovery-in-late-2024-semi-reports) — SEMI (2025). Wafer shipment series feeding the 2007–2025 chart.

## Government & agency reports
- [Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2025](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2025) — IEA (2025). Supply/demand and concentration for critical minerals incl. high-purity silicon, gallium and germanium.
- [2023 DOE Critical Materials Assessment](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/ammto/articles/2023-doe-critical-materials-assessment) — US Dept. of Energy (2023). Criticality screening of 38 materials across eight energy technologies; silicon among them.
- [European Chips Act](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/european-chips-act) — European Commission (in force 2023). The EU's semiconductor-sovereignty law: three pillars and >EUR 32bn of approved funding.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [Industrialized preparation of electronic-grade polysilicon: a review of impurity sources and control strategies](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1364032125002424) — Yuan et al., Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews (2025). Review of the modified-Siemens purification step that gates the wafer stage.
- [The Environmental Footprint of IC Production: Review, Analysis, and Lessons From Historical Trends](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9979766) — Pirson, Delhaye et al., IEEE Transactions on Semiconductor Manufacturing (2023). IC/fab environmental footprint versus CMOS node downscaling.
- [Semiconductor supply chain resilience and disruption: insights, mitigation, and future directions](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00207543.2024.2387074) — Xiong et al., International Journal of Production Research (2025). Synthesis of supply-chain disruption, geographic concentration and mitigation research.

## Databases & data sources
- [Silicon Wafer Shipment Statistics](https://www.semi.org/en/products-services/market-data/materials/si-shipment-statistics) — SEMI Silicon Manufacturers Group. Standard quarterly worldwide wafer-shipment series (million square inches).
- [UN Comtrade Plus](https://comtradeplus.un.org/) — UN Statistics Division. HS-level trade queries for ICs (8542), semiconductor devices and silicon products by reporter/partner/year.
- [BACI](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. Harmonized bilateral HS 6-digit trade flows; the workhorse dataset for the chain's trade layer.
- [World IC exports (HS 8542) via WITS](https://wits.worldbank.org/trade/comtrade/en/country/WLD/year/2019/tradeflow/Exports/partner/ALL/product/8542) — World Bank WITS / UN Comtrade. Interface to Comtrade IC trade; change the year parameter for later years.

## Industry & trade associations
- [2025 State of the U.S. Semiconductor Industry](https://www.semiconductors.org/2025-state-of-the-u-s-semiconductor-industry/) — Semiconductor Industry Association (2025). Annual snapshot of US chip manufacturing, CHIPS build-out and global position.
- [Taiwan Semiconductor Industry Association (TSIA)](https://www.tsia.org.tw/en/index) — TSIA. Industry body for the world's leading-edge foundry/design/OSAT/materials cluster.
- [SEMICON Taiwan](https://www.semicontaiwan.org/en) — SEMI Taiwan. Public window on Taiwan's fab, equipment, materials and packaging ecosystem.
- [ASML expects impact of updated export restrictions to fall within outlook for 2025](https://www.asml.com/news/press-releases/2024/asml-expects-impact-of-updated-export-restrictions-to-fall-within-outlook-for-2025) — ASML (2024). Primary statement from the sole EUV lithography supplier on the Dec-2024 US export-control update.

## Analysis, think-tanks & reporting
- [Mapping the Semiconductor Supply Chain: The Critical Role of the Indo-Pacific Region](https://www.csis.org/analysis/mapping-semiconductor-supply-chain-critical-role-indo-pacific-region) — Thadani & Allen, CSIS (2023). Indo-Pacific/Taiwan concentration across design, foundry, OSAT and equipment.
- [The Semiconductor Supply Chain: Assessing National Competitiveness](https://cset.georgetown.edu/publication/the-semiconductor-supply-chain/) — Khan, Peterson & Mann, CSET/Georgetown (2021). Most-cited public chokepoint map of national shares by stage.
- [Preserving the Chokepoints](https://cset.georgetown.edu/publication/preserving-the-chokepoints/) — Barbe & Hunt, CSET (2022). SME chokepoints incl. ASML as sole commercial EUV supplier.
- [The Double-Edged Sword of Semiconductor Export Controls](https://www.csis.org/analysis/double-edged-sword-semiconductor-export-controls) — Whitney, Schleich & Reinsch, CSIS (2024). How chip/SME export controls push China to design out US technology.
- [Mineral Demands for Resilient Semiconductor Supply Chains](https://www.csis.org/analysis/mineral-demands-resilient-semiconductor-supply-chains) — Berg, Ziemer & Polo Anaya, CSIS (2024). Gallium/germanium/fluorine/arsenic dependencies behind chip manufacturing.
- [2Q25 Foundry Revenue Surges 14.6%; TSMC's Market Share Hits 70%](https://www.trendforce.com/presscenter/news/20250901-12691.html) — TrendForce (2025). Current evidence of leading-edge foundry concentration (TSMC 70.2% share, $30.24bn revenue).

## Gaps & good next searches
- **US CHIPS Act primary docs**: no single Commerce/BIS URL captured here — search NIST CHIPS Program Office award pages and the BIS advanced-computing/SME interim final rules (Oct 2022, Oct 2023, Dec 2024) for the export-control text itself.
- **USGS silicon**: the USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries "Silicon" chapter (annual PDF) is the authoritative production/reserves source and should be added once a stable current-year URL is confirmed.
- **Lithography depth**: thin on peer-reviewed EUV/DUV technology and ASML market-structure analysis — search IEEE Xplore and SPIE for EUV source-power/throughput and the DUV immersion supplier field (Nikon/Canon).
- **Wafer-purity science**: only one polysilicon-purity review captured; search for 11N/Siemens-process and Czochralski crystal-growth defect literature (J. Cleaner Production, J. Crystal Growth).
- **Packaging/OSAT & memory**: advanced packaging (CoWoS) and the Korea-led memory segment are under-covered here — search CSET/CSIS packaging briefs and TrendForce DRAM/NAND market data.
- **China domestic build-out**: search SIA/OECD and TrendForce for SMIC and China mature-node capacity to quantify the "China leads several mature categories" claim over time.
