Silicon metal → pure silicon → wafers

One hop past the silicon the Atlas already publishes. The next two customs lines are real and thick. Both mix solar and chips. Who makes the solar half is public. Who makes the chip half is not — and this page does not invent it.

The chain, in three measurable hops

Same grammar as the rest of the Atlas: a public code, who ships it, who makes it if a public factory series exists, and a flag when the code is dirty.

1 · Silicon metal already published

HS 280469 — silicon under 99.99%. Alloys, silicones, and the feedstock for everything downstream. This is current Atlas silicon.

2024 trade matches the live silicon profile (China 40% of exports, $1.55B, ~$2,150/t). Mine / refine shares on that page are USGS / IEA: China ~73% of mining, ~79% of refining. The metal is abundant; the concentration is already genuine, not an accounting trick.

2 · Purer silicon mixed code

HS 280461 — silicon at least 99.99%. The legal cut is 4N. Solar polysilicon and electronic-grade poly both live here. Vietnam as the top importer is solar cells, not a chip plant.

2024: $3.63B, 164 kt, about $22,000/t — nine times the metal’s price. Germany and the US lead exports. China leads making the solar half and uses most of it at home.

Same Atlas thesis as cobalt: the country that makes it is not the country the customs line puts first. More than 98% of 2023 polysilicon went to solar.

3 · Wafers mixed code

HS 381800 — doped discs / wafers for electronics. Silicon wafers and compound wafers; cheap solar wafers and expensive chip wafers.

2024: $17.8B, 114 kt. Japan leads value (28%, ~$533,000/t). China leads tonnes (58% of weight, 22% of value, ~$59,000/t). Buyers are Taiwan, Korea, China — the fab countries.

4 · Chip factories cannot measure

No Atlas-grade country table. USGS silicon covers metal and ferrosilicon. IEA-PVPS and IEA ETP cover solar factories. SEMI and company reports exist for semiconductor wafers and fabs; they are not a public country series like BACI or USGS. Counting plants (Japan has many small lines; Korea has few huge ones) is the wrong unit. This box stays empty until a public production series exists.

Chips remain the first product family this chain is aimed at. That does not license a fake factory layer. Solar can reuse hops 2–3 later, because those codes already contain it.

What this page will not claim

  • That 280461 is electronic-grade (9N–12N) polysilicon.
  • That 381800 is 300 mm semiconductor wafers.
  • Any company share (Wacker, Hemlock, Shin-Etsu, SUMCO, TSMC).
  • That Germany “owns” high-purity silicon — Wacker is real; the code is still mixed.
  • Plant counts from blogs or Wikipedia fab lists.
