Research Library · Silicon chip

Silicon / semiconductor value chain

A curated reference library of public sources for deeper study — gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source and carries an abstract (or factual summary) plus the data it rests on. A research-support layer of the Critical Materials Atlas.

A chip begins as ordinary quartz, but every purification step narrows the field: China smelts most of the world's silicon metal, yet the electronic-grade polysilicon (US / Germany), the blank wafers (Japan) and the leading-edge fabs (Taiwan) sit elsewhere. The chokepoint is not the mine — it is fab capability.

Ordered to follow the chain: ① Quartz → silicon metal (raw material) → ② electronic-grade polysilicon (≥11N purification) → ③ monocrystalline blank wafer → ④ EUV lithography & fabrication (Taiwan / TSMC leading-edge; ASML the sole EUV supplier, Netherlands; US / Japan tools and materials) → ⑤ finished chip — then the cross-cutting frameworks and trade data that sit across every stage.

① Quartz → silicon metal — the raw input 2

The least concentrated stage: quartz is abundant, and China smelts most of the world's silicon metal.
  • USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 — Silicon · USGS (2025) Annual world/US production, trade, price and reserves data for ferrosilicon and silicon metal. Abstract. USGS commodity summary: in 2024 US ferrosilicon and silicon metal came from five facilities (one idled at end-2023), and four companies produced polysilicon. Silicon metal may be further refined to ultra-high-purity semiconductor- or solar-grade polysilicon. China accounted for almost 80% of total global silicon-materials production; total US net import reliance was under 50%. Data. USGS annual producer canvass plus reported import/export trade and price data (much company-specific data withheld).
  • Due Diligence for Responsible Sand and Silicate Supply Chains · OECD (2026) Silicon-metal production shares and concentration along the sand-to-silicate chain. Summary. OECD due-diligence report on sand and silicate chains: quartz (river or extracted rock) is refined into metallurgical-grade silicon (silicon metal), abundant and available in many regions, yet smelting is highly concentrated — China holding roughly 85% of silicon-metal production, the figure the chain uses at the base of the value chain. Data. OECD synthesis of production and trade statistics with due-diligence framing — no new primary dataset.

② Electronic-grade polysilicon — the ≥11N purification gate 3

Basic silicon is common; ≥11N electronic-grade polysilicon is not — a handful of firms in Germany, the US, Japan and Korea.
  • Trends in Photovoltaic Applications 2024 · IEA-PVPS (2024) Annual global PV/polysilicon market and technology survey; source for the 2023 semiconductor-use polysilicon figure. Summary. IEA-PVPS Task 1 annual report tracking global photovoltaic markets, production and technology. Within total polysilicon output it isolates the small semiconductor-use volume — about 38,800 t in 2023 — the number the chain uses to separate chip-grade polysilicon from the far larger solar-grade pool. Data. IEA-PVPS national-survey and industry reporting on PV markets, production capacity and polysilicon volumes.
  • SIA Comments on the Polysilicon Section 232 Investigation · Semiconductor Industry Association (2025) Industry submission on electronic-grade polysilicon supply, concentration and the trade-data caveat. Abstract. SIA §232 submission: semiconductor-grade polysilicon is a distinct, tiny market — about 2.4% of demand, a forecast 33,500 t — supplied mainly by Wacker Chemie (Germany), Hemlock (US), Tokuyama, SUMCO (Japan) and OCI (Korea), with Wacker and Hemlock roughly three-quarters. It warns HS 280461 combines solar and semiconductor grades, so trade data cannot distinguish them. Data. SIA member-supplier figures plus USITC DataWeb trade statistics (HTS 2804.61 / 2804.69, 8541, 8542).
  • Industrialized preparation of electronic-grade polysilicon: a review of impurity sources and control strategies · Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews (2025) Review of the modified-Siemens purification step that gates the wafer stage. Abstract. Review (Yuan et al.) of industrialized electronic-grade polysilicon preparation, surveying impurity sources and the control strategies of the modified-Siemens process that lifts silicon to ≥11N chip purity. Full text is JS/paywall-gated and could not be retrieved; described from its title and the chain's use of it. Data. Literature review of purification chemistry and impurity control — no primary dataset.

③ Monocrystalline blank wafer 3

Blank polished wafers concentrate in Japan (Shin-Etsu, SUMCO): the top two hold at least half, the top six ~92%.
  • SEMI Reports 2025 Annual Worldwide Silicon Wafer Shipments and Revenue Results · SEMI (2026) Latest annual blank-wafer area and revenue, semiconductor applications only. Summary. SEMI year-end release (Feb 2026): worldwide silicon-wafer shipments rose 5.8% in 2025 to 12,973 million square inches amid strong advanced-computing demand, while wafer revenue slipped 1.2% to $11.4 billion — the current endpoint of the chain's semiconductor-only wafer series. Data. SEMI Silicon Manufacturers Group canvass of wafer producers; polished and non-polished area (MSI) and revenue, semiconductor use only.
  • Worldwide Silicon Wafer Shipments and Revenue Start Recovery in Late 2024 · SEMI (2025) Wafer-shipment series through 2024 feeding the multi-year chart. Summary. SEMI release (Feb 2025): worldwide silicon-wafer shipments fell 2.7% in 2024 to 12,266 million square inches as inventory correction weighed on fab utilisation, with recovery expected into 2025. Shipments cover semiconductor applications only and exclude solar — the scope note that keeps the series clean. Data. SEMI Silicon Manufacturers Group annual wafer-shipment area (MSI) and revenue series.
  • Silicon Wafer Shipment Statistics · SEMI Silicon Manufacturers Group The standard quarterly worldwide wafer-shipment series (million square inches). Summary. Landing page for SEMI's Silicon Manufacturers Group quarterly wafer-shipment statistics — the industry-standard area series (million square inches) behind every wafer data point the chain plots. Data. SEMI SMG member canvass — quarterly and annual worldwide silicon-wafer shipment area; program/subscription product.

④ EUV lithography & fabrication 5

The binding stage: Taiwan holds leading-edge fabs, ASML is the sole EUV supplier (Netherlands), and US/Japan hold the tools and materials.
  • Mapping the Semiconductor Value Chain · OECD (2025) Stage-by-stage map of dependencies and vulnerabilities across the chain. Abstract. OECD Science, Technology and Industry policy paper (57 pp, June 2025): maps the semiconductor value chain stage by stage — design, tools, materials, fabrication, assembly/test — to identify dependencies and vulnerabilities as activities disperse internationally, and the policy challenges that dispersion creates. The chain's own stage skeleton follows it. Data. OECD synthesis of industry and trade data and prior literature; policy analysis, no new primary dataset.
  • The Chip Landscape: Geographical Distribution of Wafer Fabrication Capacity · OECD (2025) Fab capacity by economy and by process node / chip type. Abstract. OECD analysis of in-production wafer-fabrication capacity by feature size across the five largest economies — China, Chinese Taipei, Korea, Japan, the US — which together held 87% of global in-production capacity as of September 2025. The ten largest firms account for about half of monthly wafer starts; capacity distribution differs sharply by node. Data. OECD compilation of fab-capacity data by economy, firm and process-node band (8-inch-equivalent wafer starts per month), as of September 2025.
  • Emerging Resilience in the Semiconductor Supply Chain · SIA / Boston Consulting Group (2024) Fab-capacity geography 1990–2032F; the source for the long history chart. Abstract. SIA/BCG report projecting fab-capacity geography to 2032. With CHIPS-Act grants and a 25% tax credit, the US share is projected to rise toward 14% by 2032 (its largest gain, versus slipping to 8% without action), while other incentive programs expand in Taiwan, Korea, Japan, India and China — leading-edge capacity beginning to diversify beyond Taiwan and Korea. Data. BCG modelling of announced fabs and incentive programs plus SIA industry data; regional capacity shares 1990–2032 forecast.
  • ASML expects impact of updated export restrictions to fall within outlook for 2025 · ASML (2024) Primary statement from the sole EUV lithography supplier on the Dec-2024 US export-control update. Summary. ASML announcement (Veldhoven, Netherlands, 2 Dec 2024) on updated US export-control rules — adding restricted technologies including metrology and software, effective immediately with a delayed 31 Dec 2024 compliance date for some changes — stating the impact falls within its 2025 financial outlook. Primary word from the only EUV-lithography maker. Data. Company statement — no dataset; regulatory scope and guidance only.
  • Preserving the Chokepoints · CSET / Georgetown (2022) Semiconductor-manufacturing-equipment chokepoints, incl. ASML as sole commercial EUV supplier. Summary. CSET report (Barbe & Hunt) mapping semiconductor-manufacturing-equipment chokepoints, identifying ASML as the sole commercial EUV-lithography supplier and analysing where equipment concentration gives leverage. Page 403-blocked this pass — described from the published summary, not full text. Data. Analysis of SME market structure and supplier shares by tool category; no primary dataset accessed.

⑤ Finished chip & foundry concentration 6

The most concentrated stage: one foundry (TSMC) and one region (Taiwan) dominate leading-edge logic.
  • 2Q25 Foundry Revenue Surges 14.6%; TSMC's Market Share Hits 70% · TrendForce (2025) Current evidence of leading-edge foundry concentration. Summary. TrendForce research note: global foundry revenue reached a record US$41.7 billion in 2Q25, up 14.6% quarter-on-quarter, with TSMC's market share reaching about 70% — current corroboration of the OECD advanced-logic finding that leading-edge fabrication concentrates in Taiwan. Data. TrendForce proprietary foundry market-share and revenue tracking — no public primary dataset.
  • The Semiconductor Supply Chain: Assessing National Competitiveness · CSET / Georgetown (2021) The most-cited public chokepoint map of national shares by stage. Summary. CSET report (Khan, Peterson & Mann) mapping national shares across every stage — design, tools, materials, fabrication, assembly/test — the most-cited public benchmark for stage-level concentration. Page 403-blocked this pass — not retrievable; noted from its known scope. Data. Compilation of firm/national market shares by supply-chain stage; no primary dataset accessed here.
  • Mapping the Semiconductor Supply Chain: The Critical Role of the Indo-Pacific Region · CSIS (2023) Indo-Pacific / Taiwan concentration across design, foundry, OSAT and equipment. Summary. CSIS analysis (Thadani & Allen): though the semiconductor industry is truly global, the Indo-Pacific is its critical region — Taiwan, Japan, China and South Korea each hold pivotal roles across design, foundry, assembly/test and equipment, concentrating the chain in a single geopolitically exposed region. Data. Think-tank synthesis of industry market-share data by stage and economy; no primary dataset.
  • 2025 State of the U.S. Semiconductor Industry · Semiconductor Industry Association (2025) Annual snapshot of US chip manufacturing, CHIPS build-out and global position. Summary. SIA annual report on the US semiconductor position — design leadership, manufacturing, the CHIPS-Act build-out and global competition — context for the US line in the fab-capacity history and the "where advanced-computing demand is met" framing. Data. SIA compilation of industry revenue, capital-investment, employment and market-share statistics.
  • Taiwan Semiconductor Industry Association (TSIA) · TSIA Industry body for the world's leading-edge foundry / design / OSAT / materials cluster. Summary. Landing page for the TSIA — the industry association representing Taiwan's semiconductor cluster (foundry, design, assembly/test and materials), the leading-edge hub at the centre of the chain's fabrication stage. Provenance and context, not a data source. Data. No primary data — association overview.
  • SEMICON Taiwan · SEMI Taiwan Public window on Taiwan's fab, equipment, materials and packaging ecosystem. Summary. Landing page for SEMICON Taiwan, SEMI's Taiwan event and ecosystem hub covering fabrication, equipment, materials and advanced packaging — context on the depth of the Taiwan cluster behind the leading-edge chokepoint. Data. No primary data — event / ecosystem overview.

Across the chain — criticality frameworks & policy 7

List methodologies, EU/US assessments and analysis that classify the whole chain's inputs and set the policy backdrop.
  • Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2025 · IEA (2025) Supply/demand and concentration for critical minerals incl. high-purity silicon, gallium and germanium. Summary. IEA flagship outlook on critical-mineral supply, demand and concentration, built from a detailed review of announced projects showing how mining- and refining-stage concentration evolves over time. Covers high-purity silicon and the chip-input minerals gallium and germanium — the cross-link to those sibling chains. Data. IEA project-by-project database of announced mining/refining capacity plus demand scenarios.
  • 2023 DOE Critical Materials Assessment · US Dept. of Energy (2023) Criticality screening of materials across energy technologies; silicon among them. Summary. DOE criticality assessment updating prior reports for recent market and technology shifts: it considers 38 materials used by eight major energy technologies, of which 23 are evaluated in detail, placing them on a near-/medium-term criticality matrix. Silicon is among the materials screened. Data. DOE criticality methodology (importance-to-energy × supply-risk) over 38 materials and eight technologies.
  • European Chips Act · European Commission (in force 2023) The EU's semiconductor-sovereignty law: three pillars and approved public-plus-private funding. Summary. EU law to strengthen semiconductor sovereignty and competitiveness through three pillars — the "Chips for Europe" initiative, a security-of-supply and resilience framework, and monitoring/crisis response — anchoring first-of-a-kind facilities that represent over EUR 32 billion of combined public and private investment. The policy backdrop to the fab-share forecast. Data. Policy text plus State-aid decisions listing approved facility investments by member state.
  • The Double-Edged Sword of Semiconductor Export Controls · CSIS (2024) How chip/equipment export controls push China to design out US technology. Summary. CSIS analysis (Whitney, Schleich & Reinsch) on how chip and equipment export controls, while restricting China's access, also incentivise it to design out US technology and build indigenous capacity — the risk that belongs in the decision/breakout layer rather than the descriptive chain. Data. Policy analysis of export-control measures and industry response; no primary dataset.
  • Mineral Demands for Resilient Semiconductor Supply Chains · CSIS (2024) Gallium/germanium/fluorine/arsenic dependencies behind chip manufacturing. Summary. CSIS analysis (Berg, Ziemer & Polo Anaya) on the input minerals behind fabrication: China has leveraged early-stage dominance to restrict gallium, germanium and graphite exports in response to US controls, able to modulate flows via licensing. Maps the gallium/germanium/fluorine/arsenic dependencies — the input cross-links worth building out. Data. Policy synthesis citing trade and export-licensing data; no primary dataset.
  • Semiconductor supply chain resilience and disruption: insights, mitigation, and future directions · International Journal of Production Research (2025) Synthesis of supply-chain disruption, geographic concentration and mitigation research. Abstract. Xiong et al. review synthesising research on semiconductor supply-chain disruptions after recent geopolitical and public-health shocks. It overviews the chain's key characteristics and complexities, analyses vulnerabilities — especially geopolitical and public-health risks — and catalogues resilience strategies, framing the node-vulnerability argument the chain draws on. Data. Systematic literature review — synthesis of prior studies, no primary dataset.
  • The Environmental Footprint of IC Production: Review, Analysis, and Lessons From Historical Trends · IEEE Trans. Semiconductor Manufacturing (2023) IC/fab environmental footprint versus CMOS node downscaling. Abstract. Pirson, Delhaye et al. review the environmental footprint of integrated-circuit production and how it tracks CMOS node downscaling — context on the rising cost of leading-edge fabrication. IEEE Xplore page not retrievable (403) — described at title level only. Data. Review and analysis of historical IC-production footprint data; no primary dataset accessed.

Across the chain — trade & commodity data 3

The datasets the chain's own trade figures are built from — kept as context, since mixed customs codes never isolate chip grade.
  • UN Comtrade Plus · UN Statistics Division HS-level trade queries for ICs (8542), semiconductor devices and silicon products. Summary. UN Statistics Division's trade-query platform: bilateral import/export values and quantities by reporter, partner, year and HS code — used to query IC (8542), semiconductor-device and silicon-product flows as a check on the chain's BACI extract. Data. Reported UN Comtrade import/export declarations at HS level by reporter/partner/year.
  • CEPII BACI, based on UN Comtrade · CEPII Harmonized bilateral HS 6-digit trade flows; the workhorse dataset for the chain's trade layer. Summary. CEPII database: BACI provides reconciled bilateral trade flows for ~200 countries across ~5,000 HS6 products, 1995–2024, derived from UN Comtrade by removing CIF costs and reliability-weighting reporter discrepancies. Powers the chain's silicon-stage trade overlay, kept as context because mixed codes cannot isolate chip grade. Data. UN Comtrade import/export declarations, HS6 bilateral, mirror-reconciled (CIF/FOB, reporter-reliability weighting).
  • World IC exports (HS 8542) via WITS · World Bank WITS / UN Comtrade Interface to Comtrade IC trade; change the year parameter for later years. Summary. World Bank WITS front-end onto UN Comtrade for integrated-circuit trade (HS 8542): world and bilateral export/import flows by year, a quick public cross-check on the chip-trade layer. Change the year parameter in the URL for later years. Data. UN Comtrade IC (HS 8542) export/import flows served through the World Bank WITS interface.

Gaps & good next searches

  • No mixed customs code isolates chip-grade silicon: HS 280461 combines solar- and semiconductor-grade polysilicon and HS 381800 mixes doped wafers with other compounds, so trade data cannot verify the production read — the chain deliberately keeps trade as flagged context, not evidence.
  • US CHIPS Act primary docs: no single Commerce/BIS URL is captured here — next, the NIST CHIPS Program Office award pages and the BIS advanced-computing/SME interim final rules (Oct 2022, Oct 2023, Dec 2024) for the export-control text itself.
  • Lithography depth is thin: only ASML's own statement and the CSET SME summary. Next, IEEE Xplore and SPIE for EUV source-power/throughput and the DUV immersion field (Nikon / Canon).
  • Wafer-purity science: only one polysilicon-purity review captured (and unretrievable in full). Next, ≥11N / Siemens-process and Czochralski crystal-growth defect literature (J. Cleaner Production, J. Crystal Growth).
  • Packaging / OSAT & memory are under-covered: advanced packaging (CoWoS) and the Korea-led memory segment. Next, CSET/CSIS packaging briefs and TrendForce DRAM/NAND data.
  • China domestic build-out: quantify the "China leads several mature categories" claim over time via SIA/OECD and TrendForce on SMIC and mature-node capacity.
