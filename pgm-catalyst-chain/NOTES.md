# PGM-chain interpretation notes

## The claim
PGMs invert the usual read twice: the chokepoint is geological (two countries), not a
refining stage; and the dominant risk is demand destruction (EVs) plus a recycling loop
that cushions supply — not a supply cut.

## Claims rejected
- **"Exporter shares show the mine base."** Rejected. Refined PGM trade routes through UK,
  Swiss and German refining/trading hubs; exporter shares are hubs, not mines. Trade is
  shown as context only.
- **"A supply cut is the main risk."** Softened. A two-country base is real, but recycling
  returns a large secondary supply and the structural threat is EV-driven demand loss.
- **Autocatalyst value from HS 711510.** Not used as a demand measure — most autocatalyst
  value is coated ceramic, not the platinum-wire heading.

## Rules reused
Reserves/production for the mine base; end-use split for demand; secondary-supply share
for the recycling loop; trade as hub availability, not mine geography.
