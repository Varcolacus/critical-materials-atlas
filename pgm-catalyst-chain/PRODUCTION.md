# PGM evidence ledger

- **Reserves & mine base** — USGS MCS 2025 (PGMs): South Africa's Bushveld holds ~90% of
  world reserves and leads platinum mine supply; Russia (Norilsk) and South Africa co-lead
  palladium (US palladium imports ~32% Russia, ~32% South Africa); rhodium is a small,
  concentrated South-African by-product.
- **Uses** — USGS: autocatalysts are the dominant use of Pt/Pd/Rh; platinum also in fuel
  cells; chemical/refining catalysts, glass, electronics, jewellery.
- **Recycling** — USGS: recycled catalytic converters, jewellery and electronics provide a
  significant proportion of total supply.
- **Demand outlook** — USGS World Minerals Outlook (SIR 2025-5021): palladium and platinum
  demand through 2029; EV adoption reduces autocatalyst demand.
- **Trade context** — CEPII BACI V202601, HS 711011/711021/711031/711510, 2002–2024.
  Exporters are refining/trading hubs, not the mine base.
