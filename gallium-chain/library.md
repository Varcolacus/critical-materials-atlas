# Gallium chain — research library

China makes almost all the gallium — then made it a lever: ~98% of world primary gallium is a by-product of alumina/zinc refining, and in July 2023 China placed it under export licensing. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Gallium](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-gallium.pdf) — USGS (2026). Latest U.S./world production, use and price data.
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 — Gallium](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025-gallium.pdf) — USGS (2025). Prior-year baseline.
- [CSIS, China's gallium and germanium export controls](https://www.csis.org/analysis/chinas-gallium-and-germanium-export-controls) — CSIS (2023). Explainer on the July 2023 licensing measures.
- [CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. Harmonised bilateral trade data used for the trade panel.

## Gaps & good next searches
- No dedicated gallium-specific industry association (e.g. a "Gallium Producers Association") turned up — the closest is downstream semiconductor bodies (SEMI) and aluminium trade press. Worth checking the Minor Metals Trade Association (MMTA) directly for a gallium page.
- Quantified Western recovery-capacity restart plans (US DOE, EU) are thin in these results — search "gallium recovery circuit restart Europe United States 2025 2026" and DOE critical-minerals grant announcements.
- Price-series data (e.g. Fastmarkets, Argus, USGS historical price tables) wasn't directly retrieved — worth a targeted search for gallium price history post-2023 controls.
