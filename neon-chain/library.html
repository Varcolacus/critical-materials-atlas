Research Library · Neon

Neon / semiconductor specialty-gas chain

A curated reference library of public sources for deeper study — gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source and carries an abstract (or factual summary) plus the data it rests on. A research-support layer of the Critical Materials Atlas.

Chip lithography runs on neon — a trace gas skimmed from the air-separation units bolted to large steel plants. Before 2022, roughly half of the semiconductor-grade supply came from Ukraine. The dependency was real, sat under the most advanced step in electronics, and customs data could not even see it.

Ordered to follow the chain: ① air separation (steel-plant by-product) → ② crude neon & the Ukrainian purifiers → ③ semiconductor grade & the 2022 DUV-lithography shock — then the cross-cutting analysis and trade data that sit across every stage. Neon shares HS 280429 with helium, krypton and xenon, so customs statistics cannot isolate it.

① Air separation — neon captured as a by-product 1

Neon is not mined and not made: it is skimmed from the air-separation units that supply oxygen to steelworks.
  • USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Helium and Rare Gases · USGS (2026) Annual US helium and rare-gas (neon, argon, krypton, xenon) production, use and trade data — the closest thing to an official neon series. Abstract. USGS commodity summary for helium and rare gases. Rare gases are produced by fractional distillation in air-separation units. In 2025 US neon sales were ~110 million litres, used for lamps (53%), semiconductors (32%) and plasma displays (12%); US net import reliance for neon rose from 33% (2021) to 52% (2025). Neon is not tracked as a standalone mined commodity. Data. USGS annual producer canvass; rare gases folded into the helium chapter; neon volumes in million litres, import reliance as % of apparent consumption.

③ Semiconductor grade & the 2022 lithography shock 3

Purified to laser grade, neon becomes the buffer gas in the DUV excimer lasers that pattern chips — and in 2022 that link snapped.
  • CSIS — The semiconductor supply chain and Russia's invasion of Ukraine · CSIS (2022) The chain's anchor analysis of how the 2022 invasion exposed semiconductor reliance on Ukrainian neon. Summary. CSIS analysis arguing that Russia's 2022 invasion exposed the chip supply chain's reliance on Ukrainian neon — the source behind the chain's "roughly half from Ukraine" concentration and the price-spike / diversification story. Across the chain — trade & commodity data 1

The dataset the chain's trade figures are built from — and the reason neon is invisible in customs.
  • CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade · CEPII Harmonized bilateral trade database (HS6, 2002–2024); neon appears only inside the pooled HS 280429 "rare gases" line. Summary. CEPII database: BACI provides reconciled bilateral trade flows for ~200 countries across ~5,000 HS6 products, derived from UN Comtrade by removing CIF costs and reliability-weighting reporter discrepancies. Neon is not separable — it is pooled with helium, krypton and xenon under HS 280429, so semiconductor-grade neon cannot be isolated in customs data. Data. UN Comtrade import/export declarations, HS6 bilateral, mirror-reconciled (CIF/FOB, reporter-reliability weighting); neon read via HS 280429.

Gaps & good next searches

  • Neon has no clean HS6 trade code — it is pooled with helium, krypton and xenon under HS 280429, so BACI cannot verify any neon-specific volume. A dedicated criticality study modelling rare-gas supply risk with source metadata (Cheng SSRN, once full text is available) would strengthen the "invisible in customs" argument.
  • The chain's CSIS anchor URL now 404s and the IEA citation does not actually cover neon — both should be replaced. The USITC partial-equilibrium paper and the Reuters/Ars Technica/CNBC reporting are the natural, independently-confirmed substitutes.
  • No post-2022 diversification figure is pinned to a public report here — the chain asserts US/Korea/China air-separation and purification expansion. Next: "neon production China Korea capacity expansion 2023 2024" and steelmaker neon-recovery retrofits.
  • No government/agency review treats neon as a standalone tracked commodity (USGS folds it into "rare gases"). Next: "US Department of Commerce neon semiconductor supply-chain review" and "South Korea / Taiwan neon stockpile policy 2022."
  • Krypton and xenon (same air-separation by-product family, also gated at semiconductor purity) would benefit from the same library treatment as a natural extension of this chain.
