# Neon / specialty-gas chain — research library

Semiconductor-grade neon is captured as a by-product of steel-plant air separation, and roughly half the world's pre-2022 supply came from Ukraine — the 2022 invasion spiked prices many-fold and exposed a dependency invisible in customs data. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Helium (rare-gas context)](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-helium.pdf) — USGS (2026). Rare-gas production context; neon is not separately tracked by USGS.
- [CSIS, "Semiconductors and the Ukraine war — neon supply"](https://www.csis.org/analysis/semiconductor-supply-chain-and-russias-invasion-ukraine) — CSIS (2022). Named as the chain's core CSIS citation (URL could not be re-verified live this pass; see Gaps).
- [IEA / industry analyses of semiconductor specialty gases](https://www.iea.org/reports/the-state-of-clean-technology-manufacturing) — IEA (2023). Note: on re-fetch, this specific IEA report ("The State of Clean Technology Manufacturing") covers solar/wind/battery/electrolyser/heat-pump manufacturing and does not itself discuss neon — flagged as a citation to double-check.
- [CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. Harmonised bilateral trade database; neon is only visible inside the pooled HS 280429 "rare gases" line.

## Gaps & good next searches
- The chain's own core CSIS and IEA source URLs could not be re-verified live this pass (the CSIS URL 404'd on refetch and the IEA URL points to a report that, on inspection, does not itself discuss neon) — worth confirming both citations still resolve, or replacing them with the Reuters/Ars Technica/CNBC sources gathered here, which were independently confirmed via Wikipedia's own reference list.
- No USGS or IEA report treats neon as a standalone tracked commodity (it is folded into "rare gases" or omitted entirely) — this is itself a finding worth stating explicitly in the chain, not just a research gap.
- Post-2022 diversification (US/Korea/China air-separation and purification capacity expansion) is asserted in the chain record but not pinned to a specific public report here — search "neon production China Korea expansion 2023 2024" for current-state verification.
- Krypton and xenon (same by-product family, also gated at semiconductor purity) would benefit from the same research-library treatment as a natural extension.
