There is no single electric-vehicle recipe

Electric-car sales grew from thousands to millions, but the product changed while it scaled. Battery chemistry, pack size, motor topology, vehicle mass and voltage architecture decide which material and manufacturing bottleneck matters.

The battery recipe changed during the expansion

LFP displaced part of the nickel-based chemistry market. That reduces nickel and cobalt exposure, but it does not remove lithium, graphite, cell manufacturing or cathode-processing dependencies.

Share of EV battery deployment by capacity. Published anchors, not an interpolated annual series.

The motor can move the bottleneck

Permanent-magnet machines offer high power density, but EV traction is not technically locked to rare-earth magnets. Induction, reluctance and wound-field machines exchange magnet exposure for different copper, steel, control and efficiency requirements.

Power electronics became a material layer

Higher voltage is a system design. SiC or GaN devices can reduce losses and support higher voltage, but fast charging also depends on cell quality, cooling, cables and charger power. A semiconductor change alone does not deliver the claimed charging rate.

Customs can identify EVs only from 2017

The clean BEV, PHEV and lithium-ion codes begin with HS17. Earlier “other vehicle” classifications are too broad to splice into an electric-car series, so the page shows them as unavailable.
