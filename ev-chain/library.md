# Electric-vehicle chain — research library

An EV is a battery with a car around it: the real chokepoint is the battery pack (its own mine-to-cell supply chain), the magnet motor is substitutable, and China assembles ~70% of the world's electric cars. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [IEA, Global EV Outlook 2026 — Trends in electric cars](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/trends-in-electric-cars) — IEA (2026). Headline EV production/sales trends, China's ~70-75% assembly share.
- [IEA, Global EV Outlook 2026 — Manufacturing and trade](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/manufacturing-and-trade) — IEA (2026). Where EVs are built vs traded; localisation of assembly.
- [IEA, Global EV Outlook 2026 — Electric vehicle batteries](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/electric-vehicle-batteries) — IEA (2026). Battery chemistry mix, LFP vs NMC, cell manufacturing geography.
- [US DOE, Electric Motors Research and Development](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/vehicles/electric-motors-research-and-development) — US DOE (2026). Federal R&D on traction motors incl. rare-earth-free designs.
- [CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. The bilateral trade dataset used for the chain's trade table (HS 870380, 850760).

## Government & agency reports
- [IEA, Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2025 — Beyond NMC batteries: supply chain issues for emerging battery technologies](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2025/beyond-nmc-batteries-supply-chain-issues-for-emerging-battery-technologies) — IEA (2025). LFP's cathode/cell concentration in China vs NMC.
- [IEA, Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2025 — Executive summary](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2025/executive-summary) — IEA (2025). Processing concentration for cobalt, nickel, lithium, rare earths.
- [IEA, Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2026 — Market overview](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2026/market-overview) — IEA (2026). Updated battery-metal demand and pricing outlook.
- [IEA, Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2026 — Executive summary](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2026/executive-summary) — IEA (2026). Top-line concentration and supply-risk findings.
- [IEA, Global EV Outlook 2025 — Executive summary](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2025/executive-summary) — IEA (2025). Prior-year baseline for EV sales/production trends.
- [IEA, Global Supply Chains of EV Batteries](https://iea.blob.core.windows.net/assets/4eb8c252-76b1-4710-8f5e-867e751c8dda/GlobalSupplyChainsofEVBatteries.pdf) — IEA. Dedicated report mapping the mine-to-cell battery supply chain stage by stage.
- [JRC, deep dive on critical raw materials for strategic technologies](https://publications.jrc.ec.europa.eu/repository/bitstream/JRC141715/jrc141715_01.pdf) — European Commission JRC. Foresight study underpinning the EU Critical Raw Materials Act, incl. Li-ion cells.
- [European Commission, Critical Raw Materials Act](https://commission.europa.eu/topics/competitiveness/green-deal-industrial-plan/european-critical-raw-materials-act_en) — EU Commission. The CRMA's 10/40/15% domestic mining/refining/recycling targets.
- [USITC, The Supply Chain for Electric Vehicle Batteries](https://www.usitc.gov/publications/332/journals/the_supply_chain_for_electric_vehicle_batteries.pdf) — US International Trade Commission. US-focused mapping of the EV battery supply chain and trade flows.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [Climate impacts of critical mineral supply chain bottlenecks for electric vehicle deployment](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-51152-9) — Nature Communications (2024). Models how mineral-supply bottlenecks slow EV deployment and its climate benefit.
- [Self-sufficiency of the European Union in critical raw materials for E-mobility](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0921344924006001) — ScienceDirect (2024). Quantifies the EU's import dependence for EV-relevant raw materials.
- [Electric vehicle traction motors without rare earth magnets](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2214993715000032) — ScienceDirect. Engineering review of induction and other rare-earth-free EV motor designs.
- [Geopolitical risk and the global supply of rare earth permanent magnets: insights from China's export trends](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0140988325003202) — Resources Policy / ScienceDirect (2025). China's magnet export trends as a geopolitical-risk signal.
- [Electric vehicle battery chemistry affects supply chain disruption vulnerabilities](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10923860/) — PMC/NCBI. Compares NMC vs LFP exposure to supply disruption.
- [Unconventional Critical Mineral Resources for Growing Electric Vehicle Industry: Sustainability SWOT Analysis](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s42461-026-01492-5) — Mining, Metallurgy & Exploration / Springer (2026). SWOT on alternative (non-traditional) mineral sources for EVs.
- [A Hybrid Rotor Design with Reduced Rare Earth Magnets for Traction Motors](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11837-022-05594-5) — JOM / Springer (2022). DOE Critical Materials Institute-linked motor redesign to cut rare-earth content.
- [Emerging magnetic materials for electric vehicle drive motors](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1557/s43577-024-00743-4) — MRS Bulletin / Springer (2024). Review of Sm2Fe17N3, Fe16N2 and other rare-earth-free magnet candidates.
