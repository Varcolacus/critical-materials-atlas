# Research notes

## Narrative correction

An electric vehicle has no permanent material recipe. BEV and PHEV battery sizes differ; LFP removes nickel and cobalt; motor topologies can use, reduce or avoid rare-earth magnets; silicon carbide becomes relevant as voltage and power rise; and larger cars generally require more material.

## Interpretation rules

- Keep annual sales separate from the cumulative vehicle stock.
- Never add the IEA `EV` aggregate to BEV, PHEV or FCEV components.
- Chemistry share is measured by deployed battery capacity, not vehicle count.
- Treat motor types as an architecture menu, not measured market shares.
- Keep factory location, OEM headquarters, sales market and mineral origin separate.
- Do not splice pre-2017 broad HS 870390 trade into the clean post-2017 BEV code.

## Customs boundary

HS17 introduced clean codes for battery-electric cars (870380) and plug-in hybrids (870360/870370). The 2002–2016 record is retained only for all passenger cars and broad static converters. Lithium-ion batteries under 850760 include non-vehicle uses.
