# Arsenic chain — research library

The poison that's also a semiconductor: arsenic is not mined for itself but captured as a toxic by-product of copper and gold smelting, and the small high-purity fraction makes gallium arsenide (GaAs) chips for radar, RF and satellite links. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Arsenic](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-arsenic.pdf) — USGS (2026). Latest annual production/reserves/uses summary for arsenic.
- [BGS, World Mineral Statistics — arsenic](https://www2.bgs.ac.uk/mineralsuk/statistics/worldStatistics.html) — British Geological Survey (2024). UK/world mine production statistics portal.
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Gallium](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-gallium.pdf) — USGS (2026). Gallium/GaAs semiconductor context, paired chain.
- [CEPII BACI V202601](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII, based on UN Comtrade (2026). Harmonised bilateral trade database used for the chain's trade view.

- [Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 — Arsenic (data release)](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/mineral-commodity-summaries-2025-arsenic-data-release) — USGS/Data.gov (2025). Prior-year arsenic dataset for year-over-year comparison.

Downloadable production-by-country dataset (1970–2024) covering 70+ commodities including arsenic.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [Direct, Embedded, and Embodied Trade of Arsenic: 1990–2019](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acs.est.4c04715) — Environmental Science & Technology. Material-flow-analysis study of embedded/embodied arsenic trade across 18 arsenic-containing products and 244 countries.
- [A review of arsenic reaction behavior in copper smelting process and its disposal techniques](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s11771-023-5392-7.pdf?pdf=inline+link) — Journal of Central South University / Springer Nature (2023). Technical review of how arsenic partitions into slag/flue dust during copper smelting and disposal options.
- [Arsenic flows in the environment of the European Union: a synoptic review](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/chapter/bookseries/abs/pii/S1875112106090201) — ScienceDirect (book series chapter). EU-focused arsenic environmental flow review.
- [Accurate Electronic, Transport, and Bulk Properties of Gallium Arsenide (GaAs)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1601.05300) — arXiv preprint. Materials-physics reference for GaAs semiconductor properties underlying its device uses.
