# Zinc chain — research library

Zinc is the invisible host that gates half the atlas's by-product metals — gallium, germanium, indium and cadmium are recovered at zinc smelters. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Zinc](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-zinc.pdf) — USGS (2026). World mine and refined-zinc production, reserves and uses.
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Gallium](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-gallium.pdf) — USGS (2026). Gallium recovered from alumina and zinc processing — the host-coupling evidence.
- [International Lead and Zinc Study Group (ILZSG)](https://www.ilzsg.org/) — ILZSG (2025). Intergovernmental supply, demand and stock statistics for zinc.
- [CEPII BACI V202601](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. Bilateral HS6 trade (zinc ore 260800, unwrought zinc 790111).

## Government & agency reports
- [USGS — Zinc Statistics and Information](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/zinc-statistics-and-information) — USGS. Annual and historical zinc production/trade statistics.
- [USGS — Gallium Statistics and Information](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/gallium-statistics-and-information) — USGS. Documents gallium recovery from zinc residues (the by-product link).
- [European Commission — Critical Raw Materials](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/raw-materials/areas-specific-interest/critical-raw-materials_en) — European Commission (2023). CRM assessment; zinc is the host for several listed by-product CRMs.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [Lead, zinc and their minor elements: Enablers of a circular economy](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Markus-Reuter/publication/277711917) — Reuter, Matusewicz & van Schaik, World of Metallurgy — Erzmetall (2015). How zinc/lead smelting enables recovery of minor by-product metals.
- [From Zinc Ore to Refined Indium: Recovery Investment, Supply-Chain Bottlenecks, and Commodity-Market Availability](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7292595) — Guzman (SSRN working paper). Directly models indium as a zinc by-product and its recovery bottlenecks.
- [Review on advances in mineral processing technologies for critical metal recovery from mining and processing wastes](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666790822000568) — Whitworth et al., Cleaner Engineering and Technology (2022). Recovery routes for critical by-product metals from zinc/base-metal residues.
- [Modeling Indium Extraction, Supply, Price, Use and Recycling 1930–2200 Using the WORLD7 Model](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11053-023-10296-z) — Sverdrup, van Allen & Haraldsson, Natural Resources Research (2024). Long-horizon dynamic model of indium (a zinc by-product) supply.
- [Critical metals in strategic energy technologies (supply-chain bottlenecks)](https://inis.iaea.org/records/dmprd-yxe90) — Moss, Tzimas, Kara et al., JRC (2011). Early EU assessment of by-product/minor-metal bottlenecks.

## Databases & data sources
- [ILZSG statistics](https://www.ilzsg.org/) — ILZSG. Monthly/annual zinc supply-demand balances.
- [BGS World Mineral Statistics](https://www2.bgs.ac.uk/mineralsuk/statistics/worldStatistics.html) — BGS. Long-run zinc mine-production series.
- [UN Comtrade](https://comtradeplus.un.org/) — UN. Bilateral zinc ore and unwrought-zinc trade by HS code.
- [CEPII BACI](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. Mirror-reconciled HS6 trade flows.

## Industry & trade associations

- [International Lead and Zinc Study Group (ILZSG)](https://www.ilzsg.org/) — ILZSG. Intergovernmental statistics and forecasts.
