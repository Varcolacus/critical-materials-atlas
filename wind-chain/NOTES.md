# Interpretation notes

## The narrative

Wind deployment grew roughly 76-fold between 2000 and 2025 and shifted geographically toward China. That does not by itself establish manufacturing dependency: installed capacity is an asset stock, while supplier installations, factory capacity and trade each describe different flows or potential output.

The design question is equally important. Direct drive eliminates the gearbox, but direct drive can use permanent magnets or electrical excitation. Conversely, geared and hybrid turbines may use permanent-magnet generators. “Direct-drive share” must therefore not be relabelled as “rare-earth turbine share.”

## Three 2025 clocks

- IRENA: about 158.7 GW net increase in year-end operating capacity.
- GWEC: 165 GW commissioned.
- GWEC: 178 GW mechanically installed.

Retirements, commissioning dates, dataset revisions and status definitions explain why the figures differ.

## Exclusions

- No announced factory is treated as observed production.
- No drivetrain observations after 2012 are invented from model announcements.
- JRC material intensities are not multiplied by all installed capacity to create false demand precision.
- Broad component trade codes are not allocated to wind using unit values.
- Technical recyclability is not reported as an actual recycling rate.
