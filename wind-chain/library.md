# Wind-turbine chain — research library

The wind-turbine bottleneck is scale, not scarcity — a turbine is ~85–90% steel and concrete by mass, and its real chokepoint is OEM concentration (China-HQ manufacturers supplied ~67% of new units in 2025), not a scarce mineral (rare-earth magnets appear only in some direct-drive designs). A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [GWEC, Wind turbine installations and suppliers in 2025](https://www.gwec.net/news/gwec-records-sharp-rise-in-wind-turbine-installations-as-five-oems-exceed-100-gw) — Global Wind Energy Council (2026). Source of the ~67% China-HQ OEM supplier-share figure.
- [European Commission JRC, Material requirements for wind (JRC139701)](https://publications.jrc.ec.europa.eu/repository/handle/JRC139701) — JRC (2024). Bill-of-materials study behind the "bulk commodities, not critical minerals" finding.
- [IEA, The State of Clean Technology Manufacturing](https://www.iea.org/reports/the-state-of-clean-technology-manufacturing/analysis) — IEA (2023). Manufacturing-capacity analysis across clean-tech supply chains including wind.
- [US DOE, Wind Turbine Recycling](https://www.energy.gov/cmei/systems/wind-turbine-recycling) — US Department of Energy (2023). Basis for the blade end-of-life / composite-recycling panel.
- [CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII (2026). Trade dataset behind the chain's component-trade extract.

## Government & agency reports
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Rare Earths](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-rare-earths.pdf) — USGS (2026). Annual production/reserve/import-reliance data for the rare earths used in some turbine magnets.
- [IEA, Rare Earth Elements: Pathways to secure and diversified supply chains](https://www.iea.org/reports/rare-earth-elements) — IEA (April 2026). Dedicated rare-earth supply-chain report covering wind and EV magnet demand.
- [IEA, Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2026](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2026) — IEA (July 2026). Latest annual outlook on minerals demand for clean-energy technologies, including wind.
- [IEA, Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2025](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2025) — IEA (May 2025). Prior-year edition, useful for year-over-year comparison.
- [IEA, Renewables 2025](https://www.iea.org/reports/renewables-2025) — IEA (2025). Market forecasts and capacity additions for wind through 2030.
- [IEA, Offshore Wind Geospatial Analysis](https://www.iea.org/data-and-statistics/data-tools/offshore-wind-geospatial-analysis) — IEA. Global offshore wind resource/siting data tool, relevant to the logistics-chokepoint framing.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [Global rare earth supply, life cycle assessment, and wind energy](https://doi.org/10.1016/b978-0-12-809451-8.00024-2) — Weng et al., in *Wind Energy Engineering* (2017). Direct look at rare-earth supply constraints on wind-turbine LCA.
- [Winds of Change: Supply Chain Analysis of a Wind Turbine](https://doi.org/10.36399/degrowth.003.01.03) — Ayer, *Degrowth Journal* (2025). Component-level supply-chain breakdown of a wind turbine.
- [Prospects for global market expansion of China's wind turbine manufacturing industry](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.enpol.2013.12.055) — Gosens, *Energy Policy* (2014). Foundational academic analysis of how Chinese OEMs built global market share — the mechanism behind today's ~67% figure.

## Databases & data sources
- [IEA, Wind — energy system overview](https://www.iea.org/energy-system/renewables-and-low-emissions-fuels/wind) — IEA. Wind generation and capacity data, 2015–2024.
- [UN Comtrade Plus](https://comtradeplus.un.org/) — United Nations. Underlying international trade database that feeds BACI.

## Industry & trade associations
- [GWEC, Global Wind Report 2026](https://www.gwec.net/reports/globalwindreport) — Global Wind Energy Council (2026). Annual flagship report on installations, growth pathways and supplier landscape.
- [GWEC, Global Offshore Wind Report 2026](https://www.gwec.net/reports/globaloffshorewindreport) — Global Wind Energy Council (2026). Offshore-specific market and supply-chain guide.
- [GWEC, Global Wind Workforce Outlook 2025–2030](https://www.gwec.net/reports/global-wind-workforce-outlook/2025-2030) — Global Wind Energy Council (2025). Technician/labour requirements across the manufacturing and installation chain.
- [WindEurope, Offshore Wind Energy 2026 Mid-Year Statistics](https://windeurope.org/data/products/offshore-wind-energy-2026-mid-year-statistics) — WindEurope (August 2026). H1 2026 European offshore connections and capacity data.
- [American Clean Power Association, US Wind Energy Monitor Q2 2026](https://cleanpower.org/resources/us-wind-energy-monitor-q2-2026/) — ACP, with Wood Mackenzie (2026). Quarterly US wind market analysis.
