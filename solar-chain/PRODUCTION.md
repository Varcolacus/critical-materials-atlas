# Solar evidence ledger

The canonical machine-readable ledger is `out/solar_chain.json`. Each block
points to a source identifier with title, year, URL, and licence where stated.

## Long module-production geography, 1990–2025

Fraunhofer ISE's *Photovoltaics Report* publishes annual regional shares of PV
module production from 1990 through 2025. The pilot records eight rounded
readings to show the structural transition without inventing precision from a
chart. The source itself changes data provider: Navigant for 2000–2009, IHS
Markit for 2010–2021, and estimates based on IEA and other sources from 2022.

Selected readings:

| Year | Asia | Europe | North America | Rest of world |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1990 | 43% | 21% | 36% | 0% |
| 2000 | 47% | 23% | 30% | 0% |
| 2010 | 82% | 13% | 2% | 3% |
| 2020 | 92% | 1% | 4% | 3% |
| 2025 | 92% | 1% | 6% | 1% |

## Comparable manufacturing capacity, 2010 and 2021

IEA publishes matching CC BY 4.0 charts with the same stages and regions. The
Chinese shares moved as follows:

| Stage | 2010 | 2021 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Polysilicon | 28.6% | 79.4% |
| Wafers | 78.3% | 96.8% |
| Cells | 57.9% | 85.1% |
| Modules | 55.7% | 74.7% |
| Global PV demand | 3.5% | 36.4% |

These figures measure nameplate manufacturing capacity, not output. The demand
row is included to show that the factory became substantially more concentrated
than the market it served.

## Physical production, 2023

IEA-PVPS reports:

| Stage | World | China | China share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Polysilicon, including 38.8 kt semiconductor use | 1.608 Mt | 1.470 Mt | 92% |
| Solar wafers | 682 GW | 668 GW | 98% |
| Solar cells | 644 GW | 591 GW | 91.8% |
| PV modules | 612 GW | 510 GW | 84.6% |

The polysilicon country shares are reported for total polysilicon; more than 97%
of the volume went to PV. The pilot does not silently change the denominator.

## Current capacity and material intensity

- IEA's *Energy Technology Perspectives 2026* puts China's weighted share of
  solar supply-chain manufacturing capacity at about 85% in 2024 and its wafer
  capacity share at 95%.
- Fraunhofer reports silicon intensity falling from about 16 g/Wp in 2004 to
  2 g/Wp in 2024 through higher efficiency, thinner wafers, diamond-wire sawing,
  and larger ingots.

## Trade context, 2002–2024

The shared BACI extraction adds HS 854140 to HS 280461. It is intentionally
labelled a broad customs basket. The 2024 HS17 line totals roughly USD 78
billion and gives China about 47% of export value, but it also includes LEDs
and other photosensitive devices and therefore cannot replace factory data.
