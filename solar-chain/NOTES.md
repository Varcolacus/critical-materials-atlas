# Solar-chain interpretation notes

## The narrative

Solar manufacturing did not simply scale up in place. Module production was
distributed among North America, Europe, and Asia in 1990. Asia held more than
90% by the 2020s. Within the manufacturing chain, China then became most
dominant in wafers, followed by cells and polysilicon; module assembly remained
somewhat easier to distribute.

The same period also contains a material-efficiency story. Silicon use fell
from about 16 g/Wp in 2004 to 2 g/Wp in 2024. Growing solar deployment therefore
does not translate mechanically into eight times the silicon requirement per
unit of output.

## Why the page uses several clocks

- The 1990–2025 Fraunhofer chart measures regional module production.
- The IEA 2010/2021 matrices measure manufacturing capacity.
- IEA-PVPS measures actual 2023 production.
- IEA's 2024 concentration estimate again measures capacity.
- BACI measures international trade in broad customs categories.

Joining these as if they were one continuous series would create false changes
at every source boundary. The page instead uses them to answer different
questions.

## Customs limitations

- HS 280461 combines solar- and electronic-grade polysilicon.
- HS 854140 under the HS02/HS17 nomenclatures combines photovoltaic cells,
  assembled modules/panels, LEDs, and other photosensitive semiconductor
  devices.
- Neither unit prices nor exporter names can reliably allocate these baskets to
  a specific solar stage.
- The HS2022 split into more specific photovoltaic codes is too recent to
  reconstruct the long history used here and is not present in the stitched
  HS02/HS17 BACI series.

## Claims deliberately excluded

- Installed PV capacity is not described as manufacturing capacity.
- China-headquartered production outside China is not assigned to China unless
  a source measures ownership rather than factory location.
- Nameplate capacity is not called production.
- The rounded Fraunhofer chart readings are not presented with false decimals.
- Broad code 854140 is not called “solar-module trade.”
