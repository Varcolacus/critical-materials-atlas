# Solar-PV chain — research library

China makes 85-98% of every stage of the panel, from polysilicon to lamination, even as silicon-per-watt fell about eight-fold in twenty years. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [Trends in Photovoltaic Applications 2024](https://iea-pvps.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/IEA-PVPS-Task-1-Trends-Report-2024.pdf) — IEA-PVPS (2024). Annual global PV deployment and market-trend report.
- [Solar PV Global Supply Chains](https://www.iea.org/reports/solar-pv-global-supply-chains) — IEA (2022). The reference IEA study on manufacturing concentration across the PV supply chain.
- [Energy Technology Perspectives 2026 — supply-chain risks](https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-technology-perspectives-2026/supply-chain-risks-and-industrial-competitiveness) — IEA (2026). Cross-technology assessment of supply-chain and industrial-competitiveness risk.
- [Photovoltaics Report](https://www.ise.fraunhofer.de/content/dam/ise/de/documents/publications/studies/Photovoltaics-Report.pdf) — Fraunhofer ISE (2026). Technical reference on cell/module technology, efficiency and material intensity trends.
- [CEPII BACI V202601](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII, based on UN Comtrade (2026). Harmonised bilateral trade-flow database used for the trade layer.

## Government & agency reports
- [Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2026](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2026) — IEA (2026). Annual outlook covering the metals (silicon, silver, copper) that feed PV manufacturing.
- [Study on Critical Raw Materials for the EU 2023 – Final Report](https://op.europa.eu/en/publication-detail/-/publication/57318397-fdd4-11ed-a05c-01aa75ed71a1) — European Commission (2023). The EU's fifth CRM assessment; covers silicon metal, relevant to polysilicon supply.
- [Critical Raw Materials Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=OJ:L_202401252) — European Union, Official Journal (2024). Legal text of the EU's policy response to raw-material supply concentration.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [Solar-grade silicon in the energy transition: A strategic commodity for the global photovoltaic market](https://www.mdpi.com/2813-2432/4/3/18) — Ramírez-Márquez, Commodities (2025). Reviews polysilicon as a strategic, concentrated commodity.
- [A UK foresight study of materials in decarbonisation technologies: the case of photovoltaic cells](https://nora.nerc.ac.uk/id/eprint/539379/) — Petavratzi, Josso, Shaw, Horn & Singh (2024). UK government-affiliated foresight study on PV cell material demand.
- [Diversifying the solar photovoltaic supply chain to secure Europe's energy and climate roadmap and sovereignty](https://www.cell.com/iscience/fulltext/S2589-0042(25)01012-0) — Evans, Grand & Dupuis, iScience (2025). Analyses options for reducing China-concentration risk in EU PV supply.
- [Role of anthropogenic mineral circularity in addressing dual challenges of resource supply and waste management in global photovoltaic development](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-025-64145-z) — Yuan, Song, Liu, Huang & Wang, Nature Communications (2025). Global PV material-circularity/recycling modelling.
- [Drivers of photovoltaics cost evolution](https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/115605) — Kavlak, MIT (2018). Decomposes the historical fall in PV cost and material intensity.

## Databases & data sources
- [UN Comtrade Plus](https://comtradeplus.un.org/) — United Nations. Official international merchandise trade statistics platform underlying BACI.

## Industry & trade associations
- [Global Solar Market Outlook 2026-2030](https://www.solarpowereurope.org/insights/outlooks/global-solar-market-outlook-2026-2030) — SolarPower Europe (2026). Flagship annual global demand/supply outlook for the solar industry.
- [Solar & Storage for EU Energy Security](https://www.solarpowereurope.org/insights/thematic-reports/solar-and-storage-for-eu-energy-security) — SolarPower Europe. Thematic report linking PV/storage deployment to import-dependence reduction.
