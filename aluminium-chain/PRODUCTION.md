# Aluminium evidence ledger

- **Stage concentration** — USGS: China ~21% of world bauxite MINING but ~59% of aluminium
  SMELTING (and ~58% of alumina refining). The chokepoint is the smelter.
- **Bauxite** — USGS MCS 2026: Australia, Guinea and China each ~25% of mine output; Guinea holds
  the largest reserves (~7.4 bn t) and rising exports to China; Indonesia banned raw-bauxite exports.
- **Energy** — USGS: primary smelting takes ~12 MWh/tonne (newer plants) to ~15 MWh/tonne (older);
  power is ~40% of cost, so smelters locate where energy is cheap. Recycling needs ~5% of the energy.
- **Carbon** — industry/IEA: aluminium's CO2 intensity depends on the smelter's power (coal vs
  hydro); relevant to 'green aluminium' premiums and EU CBAM.
- **History** — BGS/USGS mine series (mined_years.json): bauxite Australia/Guinea/China 2019–2024.
- **Trade context** — CEPII BACI V202601, HS 260600 (bauxite), 281820 (alumina), 760110/760120
  (unwrought aluminium), 2002–2024. Availability of the traded form, not the smelter map.
