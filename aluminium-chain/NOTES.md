# Aluminium-chain interpretation notes

## The claim
The bauxite mine is spread (Australia, Guinea, China ~25% each); the concentration is built in
the smelter (China ~59%) because smelting is electricity. Ore geography and smelter geography
are two different maps.

## Claims rejected
- **"Bauxite mining shows the aluminium chokepoint."** Rejected. Mining is diversified; smelting
  is where China dominates. Shares are shown by stage (mine vs smelter), not conflated.
- **"Aluminium trade = smelting map."** Rejected. Unwrought-aluminium trade routes through metal
  hubs and misses captive/integrated output; shown as context only.
- **"All aluminium has the same footprint."** Rejected. Carbon intensity tracks the smelter's
  power source (coal vs hydro), so 'green aluminium' is a distinct product.

## Rules reused
Production/smelter share by stage; MWh/tonne to explain the driver; 2019–2024 bauxite history for
durability; trade as availability; the recycling relief valve stated explicitly.
