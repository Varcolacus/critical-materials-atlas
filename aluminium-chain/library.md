# Aluminium chain — research library

Aluminium is congealed electricity: bauxite mining is a diversified three-way split (Australia, Guinea, China, ~25% each), but smelting is ~40% electricity by cost, so China's share jumps from ~21% at the mine to ~59% at the smelter — the chokepoint is the powerline, not the pit. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Aluminum](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-aluminum.pdf) — USGS (2026). Annual production, reserves, trade and price data for aluminium metal.
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Bauxite and Alumina](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-bauxite-alumina.pdf) — USGS (2026). Annual bauxite/alumina production, reserves and trade statistics.
- [USGS Global Maps of Critical Mineral Production in 2023 (FS 2025-3038)](https://pubs.usgs.gov/fs/2025/3038/fs20253038.pdf) — USGS (2025). Country-level mine-to-processing shares for critical minerals, including aluminium's chain.
- [CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. Harmonised bilateral trade database used for the chain's bauxite/alumina/aluminium trade figures.

## Government & agency reports
- [Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 (full volume)](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026.pdf) — USGS (2026). Full-volume MCS covering >90 commodities; 2025 tariff actions and Indonesian smelter developments noted for aluminum.
- [Bauxite and Alumina Statistics and Information](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/bauxite-and-alumina-statistics-and-information) — USGS. Portal to historical bauxite/alumina statistics, yearbooks and commodity summaries.
- [Sustainability aspects of Bauxite and Aluminium (JRC125390)](https://rmis.jrc.ec.europa.eu/uploads/library/jrc125390_sustainability_profile_bauxite__aluminium_online.pdf) — European Commission JRC. Climate, environmental and social sustainability profile of the bauxite-aluminium value chain.
- [Raw Materials Study on the EU's list of Critical Raw Materials (2020)](https://rmis.jrc.ec.europa.eu/uploads/CRM_2020_Report_Final.pdf) — European Commission JRC (2020). Methodology and criticality assessment that added bauxite/aluminium to the EU CRM list.
- [JRC deep-dive on critical raw materials (JRC141715)](https://publications.jrc.ec.europa.eu/repository/bitstream/JRC141715/jrc141715_01.pdf) — European Commission JRC. Foresight-style assessment of CRM demand, including aluminium, under EU climate scenarios.
- [Special Report 04/2026 — Critical raw materials for the energy transition](https://www.eca.europa.eu/ECAPublications/SR-2026-04/SR-2026-04_EN.pdf) — European Court of Auditors (2026). EU audit of critical-raw-materials policy, including supply-security measures relevant to bauxite/aluminium.
- [ALUMINIUM AND BAUXITE — CRM 2020 factsheet](https://scrreen.eu/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/ALUMINIUM-AND-BAUXITE_CRM_2020_Factsheets_critical_Final.pdf) — SCRREEN / EU-funded consortium. Two-page criticality factsheet covering supply chain, uses and substitution for aluminium/bauxite.
- [Critical Mineral Insights — Bauxite](https://www.afdb.org/sites/default/files/documents/publications/bauxite_factsheet_final_nov_21.pdf) — African Development Bank (2021). Occurrence, production and by-production factsheet on bauxite, with Guinea-focused context.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [Social risks assessment of the supply chain of an aluminium semi-finished profile for window](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11367-024-02334-6) — The International Journal of Life Cycle Assessment (2024). Social-LCA of an aluminium product supply chain.
- [An LCA study of a primary aluminum supply chain](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0959652604000083) — Journal of Cleaner Production. Life-cycle assessment of the primary aluminium production chain.
- [Towards carbon-negative primary aluminium production: Integrating biomass resources and renewable electricity](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0959652625023510) — Journal of Cleaner Production (2025). Pathways to decarbonise Hall–Héroult smelting via renewable power and biomass anodes.
- [Decarbonizing the aluminium industry: A comprehensive review of pathways and process integration perspectives](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2211467X25002160) — ScienceDirect (2025). Review of technical routes to cut aluminium-sector emissions.
- [A Novel Aluminium Alloys Design Strategy for Low Usage of Critical Raw Materials and High Casting Processibility for Automotive Applications](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40962-024-01502-6) — International Journal of Metalcasting (2024). Alloy design to reduce reliance on critical raw materials in automotive aluminium.
- [Co-Extraction of Aluminum and Silicon and Kinetics Analysis in Carbochlorination Process of Low-Grade Bauxite](https://www.mdpi.com/1996-1944/17/14/3613) — Minerals (MDPI, 2024). Alternative processing route for low-grade bauxite ores unsuitable for the Bayer process.
- [A comparative review of the mineralogical and chemical composition of African major bauxite deposits](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10450972/) — PMC / academic review. Comparative geology of Guinea and other African bauxite deposits.
