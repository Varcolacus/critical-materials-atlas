# Beryllium chain — research library

The rare critical mineral where the US, not China, is the chokepoint — a single Utah deposit and largely one company (Materion) hold ~55% of world beryllium, guarded by a serious toxicity barrier. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Beryllium](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-beryllium.pdf) — USGS (2026). Annual mine production, reserves, US/world shares.
- [BGS World Mineral Statistics — beryllium](https://www2.bgs.ac.uk/mineralsuk/statistics/worldStatistics.html) — British Geological Survey (2024). Long-run mine-production series by country.
- [USGS Historical Statistics for Mineral and Material Commodities](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/historical-statistics-mineral-and-material-commodities) — USGS (2024). Long-run US/world production history.
- [CEPII BACI V202601](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII, based on UN Comtrade (2026). Bilateral trade database used for the chain's trade panel.

## Government & agency reports
- [Beryllium: Economic Geology, Material Flow, and Global Importance of a Key Critical Mineral](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/geology-energy-and-minerals-science-center/science/beryllium-economic-geology-material-flow) — USGS. Deposit geology, material-flow analysis, why beryllium is on the critical-minerals list.
- [Beryllium Statistics and Information](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/beryllium-statistics-and-information) — USGS National Minerals Information Center. Production, reserves, uses, salient statistics.
- [Beryllium — A critical mineral commodity: Resources, production, and supply chain (Fact Sheet 2016-3081)](https://pubs.usgs.gov/fs/2016/3081/fs20163081.pdf) — USGS (2016). Dedicated supply-chain fact sheet on why beryllium is single-source-critical.
- [Beryllium, USGS Minerals Yearbook 2022](https://pubs.usgs.gov/myb/vol1/2022/myb1-2022-beryllium.pdf) — USGS (2022). Detailed annual chapter: production, trade, prices, world review including China/Kazakhstan.
- [Critical Mineral Resources: National Policy and Critical Minerals List (R47982)](https://www.congress.gov/crs_external_products/R/PDF/R47982/R47982.7.pdf) — Congressional Research Service. How beryllium made the US critical-minerals list (single point of failure).
- [Raw materials in the European defence industry](https://setis.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2021-02/raw_materials_in_the_european_defence_industry.pdf) — Claudiu C. Pavel, EU JRC/SETIS (2021). Beryllium and other CRMs in EU defence equipment.
- [Beryllium — OSHA overview](https://www.osha.gov/beryllium) — US Occupational Safety and Health Administration. Regulatory framework for the toxicity that gates the industry.
- [Utah Geological Survey Circular C-130](https://ugspub.nr.utah.gov/publications/circular/c-130.pdf) — Utah Geological Survey (Mills, Rupke, Vanden Berg, Boden). State-level geology of the Spor Mountain beryllium district.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [Epidemiologic and Clinical Studies of Beryllium Sensitization and Chronic Beryllium Disease](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK214767/) — NCBI Bookshelf, *Managing Health Effects of Beryllium Exposure*. Review of CBD epidemiology — the toxicity moat, in depth.
- [Beryllium copper alloy (2%) causes chronic beryllium disease](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10224597/) — PubMed. Clinical case evidence that even low-beryllium Cu-Be alloy causes CBD.
- [Beryllium Copper — an overview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/pharmacology-toxicology-and-pharmaceutical-science/beryllium-copper) — ScienceDirect Topics. Reference overview of copper-beryllium alloy properties and hazards.
- [Beryllium Toxicity](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK585042/) — StatPearls, NCBI Bookshelf. Clinical reference on beryllium toxicology and CBD management.
- [From strategic critical metal to hazardous solid waste: integrated management of beryllium-containing wastes and environmental risk assessment](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1383586626024950) — ScienceDirect. Frames beryllium at the intersection of criticality and hazardous-waste management.
- [Metallogeny and exploration strategy for volcanic rocks hosting world-class Be-U-F mineralization at Spor Mountain, Utah, U.S.A.](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0375674223000018) — ScienceDirect (Ore Geology Reviews). Deposit-scale academic geology of the Spor Mountain source.
