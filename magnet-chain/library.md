# Rare-earth magnet chain — research library

The rare-earth mine is the least concentrated step (~60% China); separation (~91%) and sintered-magnet manufacturing (~94%) are where China's grip actually tightens. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [IEA, Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2025 — Executive summary (rare earths & magnets)](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2025/executive-summary) — IEA (2025). Stage-by-stage China share: mine/separation/magnet.
- [BGS, World Mineral Statistics — rare-earth mine production](https://www2.bgs.ac.uk/mineralsuk/statistics/worldStatistics.html) — BGS (2024). Long-run mine-production series by country.
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Rare Earths](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-rare-earths.pdf) — USGS (2026). Reserves, production, US import reliance on China.
- [CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. Harmonised bilateral trade database used for the atlas's own flows.

## Government & agency reports
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Rare Earths (Heavy)](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-rare-earths-heavy.pdf) — USGS (2026). Separate heavy-rare-earth (Dy, Tb, Lu…) commodity sheet; notes the Oct–Nov 2025 export-control episode.
- [USGS — Rare Earths Statistics and Information](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/rare-earths-statistics-and-information) — USGS National Minerals Information Center. Portal to all rare-earth data releases.
- [USGS — "China's Rare-Earth Industry" (Open-File Report 2011-1042)](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2011/1042/) — USGS (2011). Foundational account of how China built its rare-earth industrial base.
- [USGS Professional Paper 1802 — Critical Mineral Resources of the United States](https://doi.org/10.3133/pp1802) — USGS. Rare-earth-elements chapter within the broader critical-minerals compendium.
- [USGS Scientific Investigations Report 2010-5220 — Principal Rare Earth Elements Deposits of the United States](https://pubs.usgs.gov/sir/2010/5220/) — USGS (2010). Deposit-level summary of the (thin) US resource base.
- [USGS Scientific Investigations Report 2011-5094 — Rare Earth Elements: End Use and Recyclability](https://pubs.usgs.gov/sir/2011/5094/) — USGS (2011). Where the separated oxides actually go, incl. magnets.
- [US DOE — Critical Materials Assessment](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2023-07/doe-critical-material-assessment_07312023.pdf) — US Department of Energy (2023). Federal assessment covering rare-earth magnet materials for the energy transition.
