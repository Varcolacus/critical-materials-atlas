# Interpretation notes

## The narrative

The strategic vulnerability is not simply “China mines rare earths.” Mining concentration rose sharply in the 1990s, peaked around the 2000s, and later loosened. The processing and magnet-manufacturing stages remained tighter. In IEA's 2024 magnet-specific view, China held about 60% of mining, 91% of separation/refining and 94% of sintered permanent-magnet production.

## Why several China shares coexist

- BGS's 75% is 2024 mine production for **all rare earths**, in oxide equivalent.
- IEA's 60/91/94% figures follow **magnet rare earths** through physical stages.
- BACI's 63% is China's share of 2024 **export value** for broad HS 850511 metallic magnets.
- Comext's 92.7% is China's **origin share** of EU extra-EU imports under the clean CN 85051110 rare-earth-magnet code.

They have different products, geographies, measures and denominators. They must not be averaged or subtracted.

## Exclusions

- No company-capacity database was found with a long, public and consistent time series.
- No annual series is interpolated between the IEA's 2005 and 2024 magnet-production anchors.
- HS 850511 is not relabelled as NdFeB trade.
- The provisional 2025 Comext year is visible but is not the headline year.
- The pilot does not overwrite or merge the existing live magnet profile.
