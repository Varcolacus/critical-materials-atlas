Critical-materials trade database

DuckDB-WASM over one Parquet file, SQL client-side, no server, no key. One canonical table — every source normalized into the same shape, so wide, deep and (later) mirror layers coexist:
  flows(source, freq, period, reporter, partner, flow, hs6, native_code, code_level, material, value_usd, qty_kg, is_mirror)
Seven sources: baci (world · HS-6 · annual · reconciled) · eurostat (EU · CN-8) · comexstat (Brazil · NCM-8 · Jun 2026) · hmrc (UK · CN-8) · uscensus (US · HS-6) · comtrade (world · HS-6 · rotating) · mirror (China/Russia/DRC via partners). Monthly, USD at real ECB rates. Adding a country = one more adapter.
Three views: flows = raw layers · flows_best = each flow once (de-duplicated) · flows_reconciled = monthly mirror check: where the exporter's FOB and importer's CIF agree we reconcile them; where they conflict we don't fabricate a number — both sides + the range are exposed and flagged. For critical materials those conflicts are mostly HS ambiguity / re-exports / confidentiality, not freight — surfacing them is the point. (reconciled values use an equal-weight geometric mean of the two customs sides — an ablation vs BACI showed reporter-reliability weighting did not improve accuracy, so the reliability/bias metrics are kept only as exposed diagnostics; every flow carries via_entrepot, confidential and disagree_reason flags; benchmarked against BACI. A single global freight markup remains a known, bounded limitation.)
