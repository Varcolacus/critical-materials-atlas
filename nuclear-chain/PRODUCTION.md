# Publication checklist

This pilot is not ready for the live Atlas by default.

Before publication:

1. Recheck IAEA PRIS and status values against the edition being cited.
2. Confirm Red Book table transcription and retain its units and footnotes.
3. Review the NEA conversion-capacity and DOE enrichment estimates for denominator changes.
4. Treat BACI results as contextual and test any confidentiality-related discontinuities.
5. Obtain subject-matter review of reactor-family, HALEU and back-end wording.
6. Decide whether to add a licensed facility-level source; do not infer facilities from trade leadership.
7. Remove `noindex,nofollow` and link from navigation only after review.
