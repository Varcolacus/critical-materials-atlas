# Research notes and boundaries

The core narrative is capability separation. Mine output in tU, conversion capacity, enrichment work, fabricated assemblies, reactor capacity and electricity cannot be collapsed into one “nuclear supply” number.

- IAEA PRIS 2006–2025 values are annual production-reporting measures. The end-2024 IAEA status is a separate fixed fleet snapshot.
- The Red Book gives a clean comparable world mine-production series for 2020–2023 in the current table. The pilot does not manufacture a longer annual series from cumulative figures.
- OECD NEA conversion shares are a 2022 capacity snapshot. The US DOE enrichment share is a separate 2024 estimate with another denominator.
- BACI headings are monetary customs records. HS 284420 combines enriched uranium and plutonium products; services and confidential flows are not captured cleanly.
- USGS zircon, boron and graphite histories cover all uses and gross mineral weight. They do not measure nuclear-grade output. Hafnium is discussed but omitted from the chart because the local workbook has no world series.
- Reactor architecture matters: graphite and boron are design-specific; natural-uranium heavy-water systems have a different enrichment exposure; only some advanced/SMR designs require HALEU.
- Spent-fuel storage, reprocessing and disposal are alternative or sequential back-end arrangements, not one universally observed chain.
