# Nuclear fuel chain — research library

The uranium mine is the easy part — the chokepoint is a service (conversion, enrichment, licensed fabrication), not a rock; enrichment is Russia-led. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [Uranium 2024: Resources, Production and Demand](https://cms.oecd-nea.org/jcms/pl_103179/uranium-2024-resources-production-and-demand) — OECD NEA & IAEA (2025). The "Red Book" — the definitive biennial survey of global uranium resources, production and demand.
- [High-Assay Low-Enriched Uranium: Drivers, Implications and Security of Supply](https://oecd-nea.org/jcms/pl_96126/high-assay-low-enriched-uranium-drivers-implications-and-security-of-supply) — OECD NEA (2024). Why advanced/SMR reactors need HALEU and why supply is nearly all Russian today.
- [Uranium Enrichment Explained](https://www.energy.gov/ne/articles/uranium-enrichment-explained) — US DOE (2024). Plain-language explainer of the enrichment step and why it is the chokepoint.
- [PRIS — World trend in electrical production](https://pris.iaea.org/PRIS/WorldStatistics/WorldTrendinElectricalProduction.aspx) — IAEA Power Reactor Information System (2026). Reactor fleet and nuclear-generation data.
- [BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII (2026). Harmonised bilateral trade data used for the chain's uranium ore/compound trade extract.

Press release framing the 2024 Red Book's headline findings.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [Nuclear Fuel Revival: Uranium Markets, SMRs, and Global Energy Security](https://doi.org/10.3390/commodities5010007) — MDPI *Commodities* (2026). Review of uranium as a strategic resource amid SMR-driven demand growth.
- [Uranium at the nexus of energy security and sustainable development in a renuclearised world](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s44329-025-00045-3) — Springer, *BMC Environmental Science* (2025). Demand trajectories vs. mining/conversion/enrichment/HALEU expansion capacity.
- [Critical raw materials, circular economy, sustainable development: EU policy reflections for future research and innovation](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0921344924006505) — *Journal of Cleaner Production* (2024). EU CRM policy context relevant to why uranium sits outside the official CRM list.
- [Merits and Viability of Different Nuclear Fuel Cycles and Technology Options and the Waste Aspects of Advanced Nuclear Reactors](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/26500/chapter/7) — National Academies Press. Peer-reviewed committee assessment of fuel-cycle technology options.
