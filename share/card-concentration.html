Critical minerals concentrate two ways
The “critical minerals are concentrating” average is these two: remove lithium & cobalt and it falls to −0.002. Freeze the definition to the first EU list (2011) and those materials mostly diversified (median −0.095).
Cobalt is the clean case — its HHI tripled while producers rose 14→20. Lithium’s producers fell (10→7), so it is shown, not leaned on. HHI of national production shares; production only — no trade, no model.
