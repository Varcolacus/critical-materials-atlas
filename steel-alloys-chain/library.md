# Steel / structural-alloys chain — research library

Steel itself is abundant, but the alloying metals that give it strength — niobium, vanadium, chromium, manganese, molybdenum — come from a handful of concentrated sources. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Niobium (Columbium)](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-niobium.pdf) — USGS (2026). Annual production/reserves data; Brazil's ~92% share of mine production.
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 — Vanadium](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025-vanadium.pdf) — USGS (2025). Annual production/reserves data; China's dominant share.
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 — Chromium](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025-chromium.pdf) — USGS (2025). Annual production/reserves data; South Africa mine / China ferrochrome split.
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 — Manganese](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025-manganese.pdf) — USGS (2025). Annual production/reserves data.
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 — Molybdenum](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025-molybdenum.pdf) — USGS (2025). Annual production/reserves data.
- [CEPII BACI V202601](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII, based on UN Comtrade (2026). Harmonised bilateral trade-flow database used for the trade layer.

## Government & agency reports
- [Draft critical mineral list—Summary of methodology and background information](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/ofr20181021) — Fortier, Nassar, Lederer & Brainard, USGS Open-File Report 2018-1021 (2018). Methodology behind the US critical-minerals screening that includes these alloying metals.
- [Investigation of US foreign reliance on critical minerals](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/ofr20201127) — Nassar, Alonso & Brainard, USGS Open-File Report 2020-1127 (2020). Quantifies US import reliance by metal.
- [Methodology and technical input for the 2021 review and revision of the US Critical Minerals List](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/ofr20211045) — Nassar & Fortier, USGS Open-File Report 2021-1045 (2021). Updated criticality screening methodology.
- [United States and Puerto Rico—mineral resource assessment (antimony, barite, beryllium, chromium…)](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/ofr20191023D/full) — Brennan, Day, Drenth, Foley, Hall et al., USGS Open-File Report 2019-1023-D (2022). Domestic resource assessment covering chromium among other metals.
- [Domestic sources of critical minerals in Alaska](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/ofr20191023E) — Kreiner, Jones & Case, USGS Open-File Report 2019-1023-E (2022). Domestic-supply assessment relevant to diversification away from concentrated sources.
- [Study on Critical Raw Materials for the EU 2023 – Final Report](https://op.europa.eu/en/publication-detail/-/publication/57318397-fdd4-11ed-a05c-01aa75ed71a1) — European Commission (2023). The EU's fifth CRM assessment; niobium, vanadium, manganese and chromium (via coking coal-adjacent chain) all feature.
- [Critical Raw Materials Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=OJ:L_202401252) — European Union, Official Journal (2024). Legal text of the EU's policy response to raw-material supply concentration.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [Manganese materials flow patterns](https://pubs.usgs.gov/usbmic/ic-9399/9399.pdf) — Jones, USGS Information Circular 9399 (1994). Classic material-flow study of manganese from mine to steel.
- [The security evaluation of nickel industrial and supply chains](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0196890425011446) — Sun, Yuan, Wang, Chen & Zha, Resources Policy (2025). Supply-chain security methodology directly transferable to ferroalloy metals.

## Databases & data sources
- [UN Comtrade Plus](https://comtradeplus.un.org/) — United Nations. Official international merchandise trade statistics platform underlying BACI.

## Industry & trade associations
- [Niobium Tech (CBMM) — products, applications and sustainability reports](https://www.niobium.tech/) — CBMM (Companhia Brasileira de Metalurgia e Mineração). The dominant Brazilian niobium producer's technical and sustainability publications.
- [The Use of Vanadium in High Strength Low Alloy Steels](https://vanitec.org/images/papers/051.pdf) — Crowther & Li, Vanitec technical library. Technical paper on vanadium's role in steel alloying.
- [Vanadium set for "disruptive" demand growth as battery energy storage boom gains momentum](https://vanitec.org/latest-from-vanitec/article/vanadium-set-for-disruptive-demand-growth-as-battery-energy-storage-boom-gains-momentum-vanitec/) — Vanitec. Industry commentary on vanadium demand beyond steel (VRFBs).
- [Manganese market research (public reports, monthly and annual reports)](https://www.manganese.org/en/market-research/) — International Manganese Institute (IMnI). Industry body's market-data and report hub.
- [ICDA market insights](https://www.icdacr.com/market-insights/) — International Chromium Development Association. Industry body covering chromite/ferrochrome/stainless-steel market data.
- [IMOA Annual Review and MolyReview](https://www.imoa.info/) — International Molybdenum Association. Industry body's annual review, MolyReview newsletter, and Mo4Steel conference proceedings.
