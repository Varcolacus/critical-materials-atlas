# Steel-alloys evidence ledger

- **Niobium** — USGS MCS 2026: Brazil ~92% of world production (largely CBMM, Araxá),
  Canada ~7%; ~77% into steel, ~21% superalloys; Brazil exports ~49% to China.
- **Vanadium** — USGS MCS 2025: China the top producer, much from steelmaking slag;
  demand tied to the Chinese steel industry; also vanadium-flow grid batteries.
- **Chromium** — USGS MCS 2025: South Africa leads chromite-ore mining; China leads
  ferrochromium and stainless-steel production and chromium consumption.
- **Manganese** — USGS MCS 2025: mine spread (South Africa, Gabon, Australia); ferroalloy
  processing concentrated in China (see the atlas's manganese profile).
- **Molybdenum** — USGS MCS 2025: China, Chile, US, Peru (largely a copper by-product).
- **History** — BGS/USGS mine series (mined_years.json): niobium (Brazil ~90%, 2019–2023),
  vanadium (China ~64%, 2000–2024).
- **Trade context** — CEPII BACI V202601, HS 720293/720292/720241/720211 (ferroalloys),
  2002–2024. The alloy addition as traded, not steel.
