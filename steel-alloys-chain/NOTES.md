# Steel-alloys interpretation notes

## The claim
Steel supply is not the chokepoint; the concentrated alloying additions are. Niobium
(~92% Brazil) is the extreme; chromium and vanadium concentrate at the ferroalloy stage
(China). The correct risk read is "which grades can be made", not "will steel run out".

## Claims rejected
- **"Ferroalloy trade = steel manufacturing."** Rejected. Ferroalloys are the alloy
  addition, not crude steel (a far larger, separate trade). Trade is shown as context.
- **"Niobium is irreplaceable."** Softened. USGS: Mn, Mo, Nb, Ti, W are partly
  interchangeable with V — substitution is possible but changes recipe, cost and timeline.
- **Mixing mine and ferroalloy shares silently.** Shares are labelled by the binding
  stage per metal (mine for Nb/V/Mo, ferroalloy/processing for Cr/Mn).

## Rules reused
Production/ferroalloy share for "who controls it"; 2000–2024 mine history for durability;
trade as availability of the alloy addition; state the substitution hedge honestly.
