# Steel / structural-alloys chain pilot (unpublished)

The material the built world is made of. Steel (iron ore + coking coal) is abundant
and not a chokepoint — but the alloying metals that give it strength, corrosion
resistance and heat tolerance are highly concentrated: niobium (~92% Brazil, one
company), chromium (South African ore → Chinese ferrochrome), vanadium (~64% China),
manganese and molybdenum. Isolated on `wip/value-chains`; not linked from the live Atlas.

## The finding
The risk is the recipe, not the metal. A few hundred grams of niobium per tonne of
steel — from essentially one Brazilian mine — decide whether a category of light,
strong steel can be made. Steel won't run out; the grades an economy depends on can.
