# Lithium chain — research library

The lithium mine diversified to Australia's hard-rock spodumene, but turning spodumene or brine into battery-grade chemical is still concentrated in China — the chokepoint is the refinery, not the deposit. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Lithium](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-lithium.pdf) — USGS (2026). Mine production, reserves, world output by country.
- [IEA, Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2025 — Executive summary](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2025/executive-summary) — IEA (2025). Refining concentration, China's conversion share.
- [BGS, World Mineral Statistics — lithium mine production](https://www2.bgs.ac.uk/mineralsuk/statistics/worldStatistics.html) — BGS (2024). Long-run mine-production series by country.
- [CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. Harmonised bilateral trade database used for the atlas's own flows.

## Government & agency reports
- [Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 (main volume)](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/mcs2026) — USGS (2026). Reserves reached ~26 Mt; global production up 31% y/y.
- [USGS interactive critical-minerals data visualization](https://apps.usgs.gov/critical-minerals/mineral-commodities-2026.html) — USGS. Country-level production/reserve explorer, 2026 vintage.
- [IEA, Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2026 — Executive summary](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2026/executive-summary) — IEA (2026). Refining concentration hit new records in 2025; lithium projected into a 2030s deficit.
- [IEA, Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2026 — Outlook](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2026/outlook) — IEA (2026). $800bn mining/refining investment needed by 2040 for net-zero pathways.
- [EU Commission, Critical Raw Materials Act](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/raw-materials/areas-specific-interest/critical-raw-materials/critical-raw-materials-act_en) — European Commission. Lithium listed as both critical and strategic; EU demand seen 12x by 2030.
- [BGS, "Responsible extraction in South America's Lithium Triangle"](https://www.bgs.ac.uk/news/responsible-extraction-in-south-americas-lithium-triangle/) — British Geological Survey. Brine reserves vs. output gap in Chile/Argentina/Bolivia.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [Geography of control: a deep dive assessment on criticality and lithium supply chain](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13563-023-00414-x) — Mineral Economics / Springer Nature (2023). Criticality analysis across the lithium chain's control points.
- [Critical risks in an industry chain-based global lithium supply networks: Static structure and dynamic propagation](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0957582025004045) — ScienceDirect (2025). Network-propagation model of lithium supply risk.
- [Technological risks disrupting trade stability in the global lithium supply chain network](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12270698/) — PMC (2025). Multi-layer trade-network analysis of upstream-to-battery lithium flows.
- [Managing Geological Uncertainty in Critical Mineral Supply Chains: A POMDP Approach with Application to U.S. Lithium Resources](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.05690) — arXiv (2025). Decision-theoretic model applied to US lithium resource development.
- [Revising the 'Economic importance' dimension: The European framework for critical raw materials, completed and illustrated using lithium](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0301420724008201) — Resources Policy / ScienceDirect (2024). Methodology paper using lithium as the worked example.
- [Securing European lithium: assessing future demand, primary supply, trade and the role of recycling](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44296-026-00114-x) — npj Materials Sustainability / Nature (2026). EU demand/supply/recycling balance.
- [Self-sufficiency of the European Union in critical raw materials for E-mobility](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0921344924006001) — ScienceDirect (2024). EU import dependence quantified for e-mobility materials incl. lithium.
- [Bringing the state back in the lithium triangle: An institutional analysis of resource nationalism in Chile, Argentina, and Bolivia](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2214790X24001308) — ScienceDirect (2024). Political-economy account of the three Lithium Triangle states.

## Gaps & good next searches
- No IMOA-style single-commodity trade association dedicated to conversion/refining specifically (ILiA covers the whole chain) — search "China lithium carbonate hydroxide conversion capacity" for refiner-level detail.
- Direct-lithium-extraction (DLE) technology and cost data is thin here — the IDTechEx "Direct Lithium Extraction 2025-2035" report was referenced in search snippets but no stable public URL was found; search IDTechEx or S&P Global Commodity Insights directly.
- No peer-reviewed life-cycle-assessment (LCA) paper on spodumene-vs-brine carbon intensity was captured — worth a dedicated search ("lithium LCA spodumene brine comparison").
- Bolivia's YLB brine project status is mentioned only in passing; search "Bolivia lithium YLB Uyuni 2026" for a current-state check.
