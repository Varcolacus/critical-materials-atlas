# Sulfur / sulfuric-acid chain — research library

The highest-volume industrial chemical is now almost entirely a fossil-fuel by-product — and decarbonization threatens to drain the very supply that fertilizer and battery-metal leaching depend on. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Sulfur](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-sulfur.pdf) — USGS (2026). Annual recovered-sulfur production, the by-product share, and end uses.
- [Maslin et al., "The future of the sulfur supply" (Geographical Journal)](https://rgs-ibg.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/geoj.12475) — Royal Geographical Society / Wiley (2022). The core academic source for the "decarbonization shrinks sulfur supply" thesis.
- [USGS, Historical Statistics for Mineral and Material Commodities in the United States](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/historical-statistics-mineral-and-material-commodities) — USGS (2024). Long-run US sulfur production/recovery series.
- [CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII (2026). Bilateral trade database used for the chain's trade-flow layer.

## Government & agency reports
- [USGS National Minerals Information Center — Sulfur Statistics and Information](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/sulfur-statistics-and-information) — USGS. Hub page indexing all sulfur Mineral Commodity Summaries, Minerals Yearbook chapters and special publications.
- [USGS, Fertilizers — Sustaining Global Food Supplies (Fact Sheet FS-155-99)](https://pubs.usgs.gov/factsheet/fs155-99/) — USGS (1999). Direct USGS statement of the sulfur/phosphate-fertilizer/food-security link the chain's narrative rests on.
- [USGS, Materials Flow of Sulfur (Open-File Report 02-298)](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2002/of02-298/) — USGS (2002). A full material-flow study of the US sulfur economy — mine-to-use, exactly the kind of analysis the chain's "1 · Recovery → 2 · Acid → 3 · Fertilizer/metals" hop structure implies.
- [USGS, U.S. Geological Survey Releases 2022 List of Critical Minerals (news release)](https://www.usgs.gov/news/national-news-release/us-geological-survey-releases-2022-list-critical-minerals) — USGS (2022). Confirms sulfur is *not* on the US critical-minerals list at all — despite being the highest-volume industrial chemical, reinforcing the chain's "almost no one tracks it" framing.

## Industry & trade associations
- [The Sulphur Institute](https://www.sulphurinstitute.org/) — TSI. The global association for sulphur and sulphuric-acid professionals (Washington DC); runs the Sulphur World Symposium and sulphur-in-agriculture programs.
- [International Fertilizer Association (IFA)](https://www.fertilizer.org/) — IFA. Global fertilizer-industry association covering the phosphate/nitrogen/potash supply chain that sulfuric acid feeds into.
