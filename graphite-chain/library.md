# Graphite / battery-anode chain — research library

Every lithium battery has a graphite anode — and China makes almost all of it: China mines ~74% of natural graphite but makes close to 100% of the spherical, purified anode-grade material, export-licensed since December 2023. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Graphite (Natural)](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-graphite.pdf) — USGS (2026). Latest production/trade/tariff data.
- [IEA, Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2025 — Executive summary](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2025/executive-summary) — IEA (2025). Graphite demand and processing-concentration analysis.
- [BGS, World Mineral Statistics — graphite mine production](https://www2.bgs.ac.uk/mineralsuk/statistics/worldStatistics.html) — British Geological Survey (2024). Historical mine-production series (China/Madagascar/Brazil).
- [CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. Trade data underlying the atlas.

## Gaps & good next searches
- Quantitative Western/spheronising-capacity build-out data (US IRA-backed plants, EU projects) is present qualitatively but not with hard tonnage figures — search "spherical graphite plant capacity United States Europe 2025 2026 announced".
- HS-code-level customs detail on spherical/coated graphite specifically (vs. the flake/artificial basket already flagged as a limit) — worth checking national customs schedules (e.g. China's own export-control HS codes) directly.
- Academic material-flow-analysis papers specific to anode-grade graphite (parallel to the gallium/indium MFA papers found) were not directly retrieved — search "material flow analysis battery anode graphite China".
