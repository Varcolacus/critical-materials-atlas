# Fibre-chain interpretation notes

## The claim
Fibre optics has a chokepoint at each end: a China-dominated, export-controlled trace
dopant (germanium) upstream, and a four-firm submarine-cable oligopoly downstream. The
West holds three of four cable builders but imports the dopant and faces the low-cost
Chinese challenger.

## Claims rejected
- **"Cable trade shows who builds the internet."** Rejected. Optical-fibre and cable
  customs headings miss preform capability, germanium content and who lays a subsea system;
  trade routes through integrators. Shown as context only.
- **A germanium-in-fibre tonnage series.** Not attempted — germanium sits in the shared
  811292 basket and its fibre share is a snapshot, not a separable trade series.
- **Precise subsea supplier shares.** Given as an oligopoly (four firms; ASN+NEC+SubCom
  >60%) from secondary analyses, not exact percentages.

## Rules reused
Refining share for the dopant; company capability for preform/fibre; supplier market share
for subsea systems; trade as availability; show the shared-code and integrator gaps.
