# Fibre-optics / telecom chain — research library

The internet's backbone is a trace of germanium, drawn by a handful of firms, then laid as submarine cable by just four companies — two chokepoints, one country (China) at both ends. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 — Germanium](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025-germanium.pdf) — USGS (2025). China's refined-germanium share and germanium's use as the fibre dopant.
- [CSIS, Safeguarding Subsea Cables](https://csis-website-prod.s3.amazonaws.com/s3fs-public/2024-08/240816_Runde_Subsea_Cables.pdf) — CSIS (2024). Security analysis of submarine cable vulnerability and policy response.
- [Carnegie Endowment, Securing Europe's Subsea Data Cables](https://carnegieendowment.org/research/2024/12/securing-europes-subsea-data-cables) — Carnegie Endowment (2024). Europe-focused subsea cable security and resilience analysis.
- [Mordor Intelligence, Submarine Cabling Systems Market](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/submarine-cabling-systems-market) — Mordor Intelligence (2024). Market-share data for the wet-plant/cable-laying industry.
- [Corning, Hemlock (Michigan) preform-capacity expansion announcement](https://www.corning.com/worldwide/en/about-us/news-events.html) — Corning (2024). Corning's own preform-capacity investment as evidence of stage concentration.
- [CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. The bilateral trade dataset used for the chain's optical-fibre/cable trade table.

## Government & agency reports
- [USGS, Quantifying potential effects of China's gallium and germanium export restrictions on the U.S. economy](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/ofr20241057/full) — USGS Open-File Report (2024). Economic modelling of the 2023 germanium export-licensing shock.
- [USITC, Germanium and Gallium: U.S. Trade and Chinese Export Controls](https://www.usitc.gov/publications/332/executive_briefings/ebot_germanium_and_gallium.pdf) — US International Trade Commission. Executive briefing on US trade exposure to China's germanium/gallium controls.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [Fiber Preforms — fabrication, MCVD/OVD/VAD/AVD methods](https://www.rp-photonics.com/fiber_preforms.html) — RP Photonics Encyclopedia. Technical reference (not peer-reviewed) on the preform-fabrication methods that define this chain's capability chokepoint.
- [IEEE Milestone: Vapor-phase Axial Deposition Method for Mass Production of High-quality Optical Fiber, 1977–1983](https://ethw.org/Milestones:Vapor-phase_Axial_Deposition_Method_for_Mass_Production_of_High-quality_Optical_Fiber,_1977-1983) — Engineering and Technology History Wiki / IEEE. History of the VAD process (NTT + Sumitomo/Furukawa/Fujikura) now used for >50% of telecom fibre.
