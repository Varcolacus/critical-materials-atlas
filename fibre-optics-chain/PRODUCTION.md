# Fibre-optics evidence ledger

- **Germanium (dopant)** — USGS MCS 2025: China ~60% of refined germanium; fibre optics a
  major end use; germanium under Chinese export licensing from 2023.
- **Preform / fibre** — company disclosures & market analyses: low-loss subsea-grade fibre
  is concentrated among a few firms; Corning (US) leads and supplies most submarine
  consortia (2024 Hemlock, Michigan preform-capacity expansion); Prysmian, YOFC (China),
  Sumitomo among others.
- **Submarine cable systems** — CSIS (2024); Carnegie (2024); market analyses: four builders
  — ASN (France/Nokia, 2024 leader), SubCom (US), NEC (Japan), HMN Tech (China);
  ASN+NEC+SubCom >60% of 2024 wet-plant revenue. ~99% of intercontinental data on subsea fibre.
- **Trade context** — CEPII BACI V202601, HS 900110 (bare fibre), 854470 (sheathed cable),
  811292 (germanium basket), 2002–2024. Availability of the traded good, not maker structure.
