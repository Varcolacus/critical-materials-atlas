# Fibre-optics / telecom chain pilot (unpublished)

Follows the physical internet from a trace dopant to the ocean floor: germanium →
germanium-doped silica preform → drawn fibre → submarine cable systems. Two
chokepoints — germanium upstream (China ~60%, export-controlled) and just four
subsea-cable makers downstream. Isolated on `wip/value-chains`; not linked from the
live Atlas.

## The finding
- Optical fibre is silica doped with germanium; fibre optics is a major germanium use,
  and germanium has been under Chinese export licensing since 2023.
- The lowest-loss subsea-grade fibre/preform is made by a handful of firms (Corning leads).
- Four companies build essentially all submarine cables — ASN (France), SubCom (US),
  NEC (Japan), HMN Tech (China); ASN+NEC+SubCom were >60% of 2024 wet-plant revenue.
- ~99% of intercontinental data crosses on subsea fibre. China sits at both chokepoints.
