# Phosphate-chain interpretation notes

## The claim
Unlike the tech chains, the phosphorus bottleneck does not move downstream. There is
no synthetic substitute and no alternative ore, so the reserve base is the constraint —
and it is ~70% in one country. The risk is a resource-and-efficiency question, not a
refining one.

## Claims rejected
- **"China controls phosphorus because it's the top producer."** Softened. China leads
  current output but restricts exports for domestic supply; the long-run ceiling is
  reserves, ~70% Moroccan. Production and reserves are shown separately.
- **"Fertilizer trade shows the resource base."** Rejected. Export restrictions and
  captive use make trade a poor proxy for reserves; trade is shown as availability only.
- **A single N-P-K concentration number.** Not attempted — each nutrient has a different
  geography (P Morocco, K Canada/Russia/Belarus, N gas-linked); they are shown separately.

## Rules reused
Reserves as the ceiling where no substitute exists; production distinct from reserves;
per-nutrient concentration shown separately; trade as availability; show the recycling
gap rather than imply a loop that does not exist at scale.
