Research Library · Phosphate & Food

Phosphate → fertilizer → food chain

A curated reference library of public sources for deeper study — gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source and carries an abstract (or factual summary) plus the data it rests on. A research-support layer of the Critical Materials Atlas.

Phosphate rock becomes phosphoric acid, then DAP/MAP fertilizer, then food — and unlike the tech chains, the supply does not get narrower downstream. It is already narrow at the source: about 68% of the world's phosphate-rock reserves sit in Morocco, and phosphorus has no substitute in agriculture. Here the mine is the chokepoint — for phosphorus there is no plan B.

Ordered to follow the chain: ① Phosphate rock (Morocco holds ~68% of reserves) → ② acid & fertilizer (OCP, the downstream processor) → ③ the three nutrients N-P-K → ④ food — then the cross-cutting frameworks and trade data that sit across every stage.

① Phosphate rock — Morocco reserves, the only source 4

The raw input, and the one stage that binds: reserves ~68% Morocco, no substitute for phosphorus.
  • USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Phosphate Rock · USGS (2026) Annual world phosphate-rock production and reserves; Morocco holds the dominant share of reserves. Abstract. USGS commodity summary: in 2025 five firms mined ~20 Mt of marketable phosphate rock at 10 US mines; over 95% fed wet-process phosphoric acid for ammonium-phosphate fertilizers. China, Morocco, the US and Russia led output, but Morocco holds 50 of ~74 billion t of world reserves. World fertilizer P₂O₅ use reached 47.8 Mt, rising toward 51.5 Mt by 2029. Data. USGS annual producer canvass plus reported trade, price and world reserve estimates.
  • USGS — Phosphate Rock Statistics and Information · USGS Annual and historical phosphate-rock statistics from the USGS National Minerals Information Center. Summary. USGS portal for phosphate rock: monthly marketable-rock surveys, annual Mineral Commodity Summaries (1996–2026) and Minerals Yearbook chapters, crop-year phosphate/potash reports, the World Phosphate Mines dataset and historical statistics. Data. USGS statistical series — monthly and annual production, trade and reserve compilations.
  • Phosphate rock production and depletion: regional disaggregated modeling and global implications · Resources, Conservation and Recycling (2014) Depletion and production-peak modelling of phosphate rock (Walan, Davidsson, Johansson & Höök). Summary. Peer-reviewed study modelling phosphate-rock production and depletion with regional disaggregation to project global supply peaks and their implications. Full text paywalled (ScienceDirect 403); title, authors and journal verified via Crossref (DOI 10.1016/j.resconrec.2014.10.011) — abstract text not retrievable. Data. Regional disaggregated production/depletion modelling of phosphate-rock reserves.
  • Phosphate rock costs, prices and resources interaction · Science of the Total Environment (2016) Cost-curve and resource economics of phosphate rock (M.C. Mew). Summary. Journal article examining how phosphate-rock costs, prices and resource/reserve estimates interact — the cost-curve and resource economics of the rock. Full text paywalled (403); metadata verified via Crossref (DOI 10.1016/j.scitotenv.2015.08.045) — abstract not retrievable. Data. Cost-curve and resource-economics analysis of phosphate rock.

② Acid & fertilizer — OCP & processing 3

Morocco's OCP moved downstream into phosphoric acid and finished fertilizer — concentration at two stages at once.
  • OCP Group · OCP (Morocco) The dominant phosphate producer's corporate and reserve disclosures. Summary. OCP Group is Morocco's state-owned phosphate company — the vehicle through which Morocco's dominant reserve base is mined and processed into phosphoric acid and DAP/MAP fertilizers, holding both the resource and a large share of processing. Corporate site returned HTTP 403; described from established fact, corroborated by USGS reserve data. Data. Corporate disclosures (not retrievable this pass); Morocco's reserve/processing position corroborated by USGS MCS 2026.
  • Development of WEF-P Nexus based on product-supply chain: phosphorous fertilizer industry in Morocco · Science of the Total Environment (2023) Water-energy-food-phosphorus nexus, focused on Morocco (OCP) — Lee, Assi, Mohtar et al. Summary. Develops a water-energy-food-phosphorus (WEF-P) nexus model on the product-supply chain, applied as a case study to Morocco's phosphorous-fertilizer industry (OCP). Full text paywalled (403); title and authors verified via Crossref (DOI 10.1016/j.scitotenv.2022.159520) — abstract not retrievable. Data. WEF-P nexus modelling of Morocco's phosphate-fertilizer supply chain.
  • Phosphate price peaks and negotiations – Part 2: the 2008 peak and implications for the future · Resources Policy (2023) Price-formation and market-power analysis (Mew, Steiner, Haneklaus & Geissler). Summary. Analyses phosphate price peaks and negotiations, focusing on the 2008 price spike and its implications for future market power and supply security. Full text paywalled (403); metadata verified via Crossref (DOI 10.1016/j.resourpol.2023.103588) — abstract not retrievable. Data. Price-formation and market-power analysis of the 2008 phosphate peak.

③ The three nutrients (N-P-K) 2

Each fertilizer nutrient has its own concentration: P (Morocco), K (Canada / Russia / Belarus), N (natural gas).
  • USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 — Potash · USGS (2025) The K in NPK fertilizer — companion nutrient supply. Abstract. USGS commodity summary: US potash output (~420 kt K₂O, from New Mexico and Utah) met little demand — net import reliance ran ~93%, sourced 79% Canada, 11% Russia, 4% Belarus. About 85% of potash sales went to fertilizer; potash is the K in NPK, with no US substitute for the imported supply. Data. USGS producer survey plus import-source and price data (K₂O-equivalent basis).
  • USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 — Nitrogen (Fixed)–Ammonia · USGS (2025) The N in NPK — links to the ammonia chain; nitrogen fertilizer tracks natural-gas price. Abstract. USGS commodity summary: 18 companies made ammonia at 37 US plants in 2024 (~14 Mt N); ~55% of capacity sits in Louisiana, Oklahoma and Texas for their natural-gas feedstock. About 88% of ammonia went to fertilizer. Ammonia — the N in NPK — tracks the price and availability of natural gas. Data. USGS producer survey plus natural-gas (Henry Hub) and Gulf-Coast ammonia price data.

④ Food & phosphorus security 3

The end use that cannot be switched off — non-substitutable, finite, and poorly recycled.
  • Phosphorus, food and 'messy' problems: a systemic inquiry into the management of a critical global resource · Cordell, UTS (2008) The seminal peak-phosphorus / phosphorus-security thesis. Abstract. Doctoral thesis applying Soft Systems Methodology, stakeholder interviews and substance-flow analysis to phosphorus security. Finds phosphate rock finite and concentrated in China, Morocco and the US, with peak production plausible within decades and no international body governing phosphorus sustainability for food systems — an institutional gap that blocks corrective feedback. Data. Soft Systems Methodology with stakeholder interviews, substance-flow and institutional analysis (UTS doctoral thesis).
  • Phosphorus vulnerability: a qualitative framework for assessing food-system vulnerability · Global Environmental Change (2014) Framework for national/regional phosphorus supply vulnerability (Cordell & Neset). Summary. Proposes a qualitative framework for assessing the vulnerability of national and regional food systems to the multi-dimensional stressors of phosphorus scarcity. Full text paywalled (403); title and authors verified via Crossref (DOI 10.1016/j.gloenvcha.2013.11.005) — abstract not retrievable. Data. Qualitative national/regional phosphorus-vulnerability framework.
  • FAO — World fertilizer trends and outlook · FAO Global fertilizer supply/demand outlook and food-security framing. Summary. FAO outlook on world fertilizer supply, demand and balances for the three nutrients (N, P₂O₅, K₂O), with a food-security framing. FAO repository served a JavaScript shell — full text not retrievable; described from the report title and Crossref metadata (publisher FAO). Data. FAO nutrient-balance projections (supply, demand, trade) — publisher FAO.

Across the chain — criticality frameworks & policy 4

List methodologies, EU/US assessments and industry bodies that classify and track the whole chain.
  • European Commission — Critical Raw Materials · European Commission (2023) Phosphate rock and phosphorus are listed EU critical raw materials. Summary. European Commission page on the EU Critical Raw Materials framework: the 2023 list (fifth assessment, 34 CRMs) scores 70 candidate materials on Economic Importance and Supply Risk. Phosphate rock and phosphorus are both listed as critical raw materials. Data. EU criticality methodology (Economic Importance × Supply Risk) over 70 candidate materials.
  • CSIS — Critical Minerals Security Program · CSIS Analysis of mineral supply security including fertilizer minerals. Summary. Landing page for the CSIS Critical Minerals Security Program — analysis, reports, podcasts and events on building resilient US mineral supply chains, including fertilizer minerals, via domestic production and allied partnerships. Data. No primary data — program description and policy commentary.
  • IFDC — fertilizer & food-security research · IFDC Applied research and reporting on fertilizer supply and access. Summary. IFDC is an independent nonprofit focused on soil health and fertilizer access for food security — fertilizer/soil innovation, farmer training and market/policy development, mainly across Africa and other developing regions. Data. No primary dataset on the page — program description and impact figures.
  • International Fertilizer Association (IFA) · IFA Global fertilizer industry statistics, outlooks and market intelligence. Summary. The International Fertilizer Association — the global fertilizer industry body (~500 members across 80 countries). Provides market intelligence and the IFASTAT statistics platform, and notes that about half of world food production relies on mineral fertilizers. Data. IFA / IFASTAT industry statistics (production, trade, capacity) — membership-sourced.

Across the chain — trade & commodity data 4

The datasets the chain's own figures are built from.
  • CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade · CEPII Harmonized bilateral trade in phosphate rock and fertilizers (HS6, 2002–2024). Summary. CEPII database: BACI provides reconciled bilateral trade flows for ~200 countries across ~5,000 HS6 products, 1995–2024, derived from UN Comtrade by removing CIF costs and reliability-weighting reporter discrepancies (version 202601). Data. UN Comtrade import/export declarations, HS6 bilateral, mirror-reconciled (CIF/FOB, reporter-reliability weighting).
  • USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries series · USGS Annual phosphate/potash/nitrogen production, reserves and trade data. Summary. USGS index for Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — downloadable PDF chapters per commodity (phosphate rock, potash, nitrogen and every other mineral) plus the full report and appendixes. USGS's annual reference on production, reserves, trade and use. Data. USGS annual mineral-commodity statistics series (production, imports, reserves, use).
  • FAOSTAT — fertilizers by nutrient · FAO Fertilizer production, use and trade by nutrient and country. Summary. FAOSTAT's Fertilizers by Nutrient domain: country-level production, agricultural use and trade of the three nutrients (N, P₂O₅, K₂O), compiled by FAO — the nutrient-basis complement to USGS mineral figures. Data. FAO-compiled national fertilizer statistics by nutrient (production, use, import/export).
  • UN Comtrade · United Nations Bilateral trade in phosphate rock and fertilizer products. Summary. UN Comtrade Plus — the United Nations' repository of bilateral merchandise trade statistics reported by national customs, queried at HS6 for phosphate rock and fertilizer products. The primary source that BACI reconciles. Data. National customs import/export declarations, HS-classified (UN Statistics Division).

Gaps & good next searches

  • Phosphorus recovery / recycling — struvite from wastewater, manure and sewage — is the substitution side of a geological chokepoint but is under-covered here. Next: "struvite phosphorus recovery wastewater scale-up" and "manure phosphorus recycling agronomic efficiency."
  • Cadmium in phosphate fertilizer is a quality/health dimension not yet in the chain — the EU has set cadmium limits on P fertilizers. Try "cadmium phosphate fertilizer EU regulation limit."
  • The 2008 and 2022 price spikes and OCP's reserve disclosures need primary retrieval — the four ScienceDirect papers and the OCP corporate site were paywalled or blocked this pass; a library / DOI-proxy route would unlock the abstracts and any figures.
  • No clean single HS6 code separates unbeneficiated phosphate rock from fertilizer product, and export restrictions (China, Russia) plus captive domestic use mean BACI trade shares understate where the resource actually sits — reserves, not trade, are the right lens.
  • Morocco's reserve figure includes Western Sahara; the attribution is a data caveat worth a dedicated note when quoting the ~68% share.
