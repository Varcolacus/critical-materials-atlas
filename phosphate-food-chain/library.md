# Phosphate / food chain — research library

Phosphate is a geological chokepoint — ~70% of world reserves are in Morocco, and there is no substitute for phosphorus in agriculture. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Phosphate Rock](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-phosphate.pdf) — USGS (2026). World phosphate-rock production and reserves; Morocco holds the dominant share of reserves.
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 — Potash](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025-potash.pdf) — USGS (2025). The K in NPK fertilizer — companion nutrient supply.
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 — Nitrogen (Fixed)–Ammonia](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025-nitrogen.pdf) — USGS (2025). The N in NPK — links to the ammonia chain.
- [CEPII BACI V202601](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. Bilateral HS6 trade in phosphate rock and fertilizers.

## Government & agency reports
- [USGS — Phosphate Rock Statistics and Information](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/phosphate-rock-statistics-and-information) — USGS. Annual and historical phosphate-rock statistics.
- [European Commission — Critical Raw Materials](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/raw-materials/areas-specific-interest/critical-raw-materials_en) — European Commission (2023). Phosphate rock and phosphorus are listed EU critical raw materials.
- [FAO — World fertilizer trends and outlook](https://www.fao.org/documents/card/en/c/cc0088en) — FAO (2022). Global fertilizer supply/demand outlook and food-security framing.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [Phosphorus, food and 'messy' problems: a systemic inquiry into the management of a critical global resource](https://opus.lib.uts.edu.au/handle/10453/16086) — Cordell, UTS (2008). The seminal peak-phosphorus / phosphorus-security thesis.
- [Phosphorus vulnerability: a qualitative framework for assessing food-system vulnerability](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0959378013001970) — Cordell & Neset, Global Environmental Change (2014). Framework for national/regional phosphorus supply vulnerability.
- [Phosphate rock production and depletion: regional disaggregated modeling and global implications](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0921344914002298) — Walan et al., Resources, Conservation and Recycling (2014). Depletion and production-peak modelling.
- [Phosphate price peaks and negotiations – Part 2: the 2008 peak and implications](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0301420723002994) — Mew, Steiner, Haneklaus & Geissler, Resources Policy (2023). Price-formation and market-power analysis.
- [Phosphate rock costs, prices and resources interaction](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0048969715305489) — Mew, Science of the Total Environment (2016). Cost-curve and resource economics of phosphate rock.
- [WEF-P Nexus based on product-supply chain: phosphorous fertilizer industry in Morocco](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0048969722066190) — Lee, Assi, Mohtar et al., Science of the Total Environment (2023). Water-energy-food-phosphorus nexus, focused on Morocco (OCP).

## Databases & data sources
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries series](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/) — USGS. Annual phosphate/potash/nitrogen data.
- [FAOSTAT — fertilizers](https://www.fao.org/faostat/en/#data/RFN) — FAO. Fertilizer production, use and trade by nutrient and country.
- [UN Comtrade](https://comtradeplus.un.org/) — UN. Bilateral trade in phosphate rock and fertilizer products.
- [CEPII BACI](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. Mirror-reconciled HS6 trade flows.

## Industry & trade associations
- [International Fertilizer Association (IFA)](https://www.fertilizer.org/) — IFA. Global fertilizer industry statistics, outlooks and market intelligence.
- [OCP Group](https://www.ocpgroup.ma/) — OCP (Morocco). The dominant phosphate producer's corporate/reserve disclosures.

## Analysis, think-tanks & reporting
- [CSIS — Critical Minerals Security Program](https://www.csis.org/programs/critical-minerals-security-program) — CSIS. Analysis of mineral supply security including fertilizer minerals.
- [IFDC — fertilizer & food-security research](https://ifdc.org/) — IFDC. Applied research and reporting on fertilizer supply and access.
