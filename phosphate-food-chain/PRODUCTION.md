# Phosphate / food evidence ledger

- **Reserves** — USGS MCS 2025/26: Morocco (incl. Western Sahara) ~50 bn t, ~70% of
  world phosphate-rock reserves.
- **Production** — USGS: China largest, then Morocco, United States, Russia. Several
  producers restrict exports to protect domestic fertilizer supply.
- **Non-substitutability** — USGS: phosphate rock minerals are the only significant
  global resource of phosphorus; most is consumed as N-P-K fertilizer on food crops.
- **Demand** — USGS: world P₂O₅ in fertilizer ~47.8 Mt (2025), projected ~51.8 Mt by 2028.
- **Potash (K)** — USGS Potash: Canada, Russia and Belarus dominate; Russia+Belarus
  supply disrupted after 2022 sanctions.
- **Nitrogen (N)** — USGS Nitrogen/Ammonia: fixed by Haber-Bosch, powered by natural gas.
- **Trade context** — CEPII BACI V202601, HS 251010 (rock), 310530 (DAP), 310540 (MAP),
  310420 (potash), 280470 (phosphorus), 2002–2024. Availability, not reserves.
