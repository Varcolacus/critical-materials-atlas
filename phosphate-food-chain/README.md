# Phosphate → fertilizer → food chain pilot (unpublished)

The one chain where the atlas's usual finding inverts. For phosphorus there is no
substitute and no alternative resource to phosphate rock, and ~70% of reserves are
in Morocco — so the reserve base itself is the chokepoint. Isolated on
`wip/value-chains`; not linked from the live Atlas.

## Reading rule
Reserves, production, per-nutrient concentration and trade are separate measures.
Reserves (the long-run ceiling) are the right lens when there is no substitute;
they are not the same as current output, which is often export-restricted.

## The finding
- Morocco holds ~70% of world phosphate-rock reserves (~50 bn t); China is the
  largest current producer, then Morocco, US, Russia.
- Phosphorus has no substitute in agriculture; phosphate rock is its only real source.
- The other two fertilizer nutrients are concentrated too: potash (K) in Canada,
  Russia and Belarus; nitrogen (N) fixed via Haber-Bosch but powered by natural gas.
- World P₂O₅ fertilizer use ~47.8 Mt/yr, rising toward ~52 Mt by 2028.
