# Tantalum / capacitor chain — research library

The capacitor metal inside phones, cars, aircraft and implants comes about 70% from the DR Congo and Rwanda, making tantalum a governance chokepoint (3TG conflict mineral) rather than a geological one. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Tantalum](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-tantalum.pdf) — USGS (2026). Annual mine production, reserves, US import sources and events for tantalum.
- [BGS, World Mineral Statistics — tantalum mine production](https://www2.bgs.ac.uk/mineralsuk/statistics/worldStatistics.html) — British Geological Survey (2024). Long-run world mine production series used for the atlas's history chart.
- [OECD Due Diligence Guidance for Responsible Mineral Supply Chains](https://www.oecd.org/corporate/mne/mining.htm) — OECD (2016). The governance framework behind 3TG conflict-mineral due diligence, cited directly in the chain's "traceability, not tonnage" argument.
- [CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII (2026). Bilateral trade database used for the chain's trade-flow layer.

## Government & agency reports
- [USGS National Minerals Information Center — Niobium and Tantalum Statistics and Information](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/niobium-and-tantalum-statistics-and-information) — USGS. Hub page indexing every niobium/tantalum Mineral Commodity Summary, Minerals Yearbook chapter, and special publication.
- [USGS Open-File Report 2013-1239, "An Exploration in Mineral Supply Chain Mapping Using Tantalum as an Example"](https://pubs.usgs.gov/of/2013/1239/) — USGS (2013). A full mine-to-capacitor supply-chain mapping exercise built specifically around tantalum.
- [USGS Fact Sheet 2014-3122, "Conflict Minerals from the Democratic Republic of the Congo"](https://doi.org/10.3133/fs20143122) — USGS (2014). USGS's own framing of the DRC 3TG conflict-mineral problem.
- [USGS Fact Sheet 2014-3054, "Niobium and Tantalum — Indispensable Twins"](https://doi.org/10.3133/fs20143054) — USGS (2014). Short primer on the linked niobium/tantalum mineralogy and supply chain — bridges to the atlas's steel-alloys chain.
- [USGS Fact Sheet 2015-3079, "Shift in Global Tantalum Mine Production, 2000 to 2014"](https://pubs.er.usgs.gov/publication/fs20153079) — USGS (2015). Documents exactly the DRC/Rwanda-ward shift the chain's history chart shows.
- [USGS Professional Paper 1802, "Critical Mineral Resources of the United States"](https://doi.org/10.3133/pp1802) — USGS. Chapter-length geological/economic profile of tantalum as a US critical mineral.
- [USGS Circular 1196, "Flow Studies for Recycling Metal Commodities in the United States"](https://pubs.usgs.gov/circ/circ1196/) — USGS. Covers tantalum recycling from electronic scrap, relevant to the chain's "processing sits downstream" section.
- [USGS, U.S. Geological Survey Releases 2022 List of Critical Minerals (news release)](https://www.usgs.gov/news/national-news-release/us-geological-survey-releases-2022-list-critical-minerals) — USGS (2022). Confirms tantalum's continued inclusion on the US critical-minerals list, used in capacitors and superalloys.

## Databases & data sources
- [USGS Critical Minerals Review 2021](https://apps.usgs.gov/minerals-information-archives/articles/usgs-critical-minerals-review-2021.pdf) — USGS (2021). Annual review covering tantalum supply/demand/price trends alongside other critical minerals.
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries archive](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/) — USGS. Annual tantalum data 1996–2026.

## Industry & trade associations
- [Tantalum-Niobium International Study Center (T.I.C.)](https://www.tanb.org/) — T.I.C. The dedicated global industry association for tantalum and niobium producers/processors — statistics, standards and market data.
- [Responsible Minerals Initiative (RMI)](https://www.responsiblemineralsinitiative.org/) — RMI. 500+ member industry association running the Conflict Minerals Reporting Template ([CMRT](https://www.responsiblemineralsinitiative.org/reporting-templates/cmrt/)) and smelter/refiner audits ([RMAP](https://www.responsiblemineralsinitiative.org/responsible-minerals-assurance-process/)) that gate legal tantalum sourcing.
- [iTSCi](https://www.itsci.org/) — ITSCi. The tin/tantalum/tungsten (3T) bag-and-tag traceability and due-diligence program covering ~3,000 artisanal mines in the DRC/Rwanda/Burundi/Uganda Great Lakes region — the specific mechanism the chain calls "a governance fix rather than a geological one."

## Analysis, think-tanks & reporting
