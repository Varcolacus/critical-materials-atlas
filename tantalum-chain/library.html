Research Library · Tantalum

Tantalum / capacitor chain

A curated reference library of public sources for deeper study — gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source and carries an abstract (or factual summary) plus the data it rests on. A research-support layer of the Critical Materials Atlas.

The tiny, ultra-reliable capacitor inside phones, cars, aircraft and implants runs on tantalum — and roughly 70% of it is mined in the DR Congo and Rwanda. The chokepoint here is not a unique deposit but a governance one: a 3TG conflict mineral whose scarce part is documented, responsible supply.

Ordered to follow the chain: ① Coltan / mine (~70% DR Congo + Rwanda, artisanal, 3TG) → due-diligence & traceability (the governance response) → ② processing to tantalum powder (China-led) + the niobium twin → ③ capacitors & end use — then the cross-cutting frameworks and trade data that sit across every stage.

① Coltan — the conflict-mineral mine 5

Where it comes out of the ground: ~70% DR Congo and Rwanda, much of it artisanal — a governance chokepoint, not a geological monopoly.
  • USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Tantalum · USGS (2026) Annual world tantalum mine production, reserves, US import reliance and leading uses. Abstract. USGS annual commodity summary for tantalum: world mine production, reserves, US net import reliance and sources, and leading uses (capacitors, superalloys). The atlas reads its mine shares — roughly 70% DR Congo and Rwanda — from this USGS/BGS series. The 2026 PDF chapter would not render this pass (repeated HTTP 403); the capacitors-leading-use and full-import-reliance framing was corroborated against the USGS Niobium & Tantalum hub (#15). Data. USGS annual producer canvass plus reported trade and import-reliance data.

The British Geological Survey's World Mineral Statistics — the long-run world mine-production database by commodity and country, and the source of the atlas's tantalum history series (DR Congo, Rwanda, Brazil, 2019–2023). The interactive query page returned HTTP 404/403 this pass — not directly retrievable; the tantalum series is used via the atlas's own extract. Data. BGS compilation of national mine-production returns — long-run annual series.

USGS fact sheet framing the DR Congo conflict-mineral problem for the 3TG minerals (tin, tantalum, tungsten, gold) and the Dodd-Frank Section 1502 due-diligence regime. pubs.usgs.gov HTTP 403 on retry — scope taken from the title only. Due diligence & traceability — the 3TG governance fix 5

The response to this chokepoint is unusual: not a new mine but documented, validated supply — the scarce thing that shapes who manufacturers can legally buy from.
  • OECD Due Diligence Guidance for Responsible Mineral Supply Chains · OECD (2016) The governance framework behind 3TG conflict-mineral due diligence. Summary. The OECD's five-step framework for responsible sourcing of minerals from conflict-affected and high-risk areas — the governance standard behind 3TG (tin, tantalum, tungsten, gold) conflict-mineral due diligence, implemented downstream by iTSCi (#7) and the RMI (#8). The OECD landing page returned HTTP 403 this pass — described from its established, widely-cited structure, corroborated by the iTSCi and RMI pages that implement it. Data. OECD consensus due-diligence standard — guidance, not a dataset.
  • iTSCi — the ITSCI Programme · ITSCI Bag-and-tag traceability for 3T minerals across the Great Lakes region. Summary. The ITSCI programme runs bag-and-tag traceability and OECD-aligned due diligence for 3T minerals (tin, tantalum, tungsten) across roughly 3,000 artisanal and semi-industrial mine sites in the DR Congo, Rwanda, Burundi and Uganda, supporting responsible export of 23,000+ tonnes a year. Data. ITSCI field-team mine-site monitoring and export-tagging records across the Great Lakes region.
  • Responsible Minerals Initiative (RMI) — CMRT & RMAP · RMI Industry conflict-minerals reporting template and smelter/refiner audit program. Summary. The Responsible Minerals Initiative — 500+ member companies — runs the Conflict Minerals Reporting Template (CMRT) supply-chain survey and the Responsible Minerals Assurance Process (RMAP), the flagship third-party smelter/refiner audit that validates conformant tantalum processors. Data. RMAP audited-facility conformance lists and CMRT company disclosures.
  • Enough Project — Progress and Challenges on Conflict Minerals: Facts on Dodd-Frank 1502 · Enough Project (2018) Assessment of conflict-mineral due-diligence progress in the DRC, with 3T mine-validation data. Abstract. Enough Project assessment of Dodd-Frank Section 1502 conflict-mineral due diligence in the DR Congo. Documents progress toward certified conflict-free 3T (tin, tantalum, tungsten) supply chains and RMI smelter auditing, while flagging that artisanal mining and informal trading networks remain hard to monitor and validate. Data. Review of RMI / ITSCI mine-validation and smelter-conformance data plus field reporting.
  • Enough Project — A Comprehensive Approach to Congo's Conflict Minerals · Enough Project Strategy paper on conflict-minerals policy in the DRC. Abstract. Strategy paper on Congo's conflict minerals (tin, tantalum, tungsten, gold), estimating armed groups earned about $185 million from the trade in 2008. Proposes four responses: verifiable mine-to-market chain of custody, securing key mines, transitional mining governance in the Kivus, and artisanal-miner livelihoods. Data. Enough Project field analysis of eastern-DRC mine control and mineral-revenue estimates.

USGS open-file report mapping the full tantalum supply chain — mine, concentrate, processing, powder and end use — as a worked example of mineral supply-chain mapping. pubs.usgs.gov HTTP 403 on retry — scope taken from the title; the mapped node figures not extracted this pass. Data. USGS supply-chain mapping of tantalum from mine to capacitor.
  • USGS Fact Sheet 2014-3054 — Niobium and Tantalum: Indispensable Twins · USGS (2014) Primer on the linked niobium/tantalum mineralogy and supply chain. Summary. USGS primer on the linked niobium/tantalum mineralogy and supply chain (niobium into steel alloys; tantalum into capacitors and superalloys) — the bridge to the atlas's steel-alloys chain. pubs.usgs.gov HTTP 403 on retry — scope taken from the title. Data. USGS descriptive primer — no primary dataset extracted this pass.
  • USGS Circular 1196 — Flow Studies for Recycling Metal Commodities in the United States · USGS Covers tantalum recycling from electronic scrap. Abstract. USGS material-flow studies for recycling US metal commodities, including tantalum recovery from electronic and capacitor scrap — relevant to the chain's "processing sits downstream" section. pubs.usgs.gov HTTP 403 on retry — scope taken from the title; recovery rates not extracted this pass. Data. USGS material-flow accounting of scrap and recycled metal.
  • Tantalum-Niobium International Study Center (T.I.C.) · T.I.C. The dedicated global industry association for tantalum and niobium producers/processors. Summary. The Tantalum-Niobium International Study Center — the global industry association (founded 1974, 90+ members across 30 countries) for tantalum and niobium producers and processors; provides technical archives, supply-chain portals (mining, processing, manufacturing) and market data. ③ Capacitors & end use 0

The dominant use — and, like blade casting in the aerospace chain, the thinnest evidence base here.
No public, country-level source maps tantalum-capacitor manufacturing: the concentration among a handful of capacitor makers is undocumented in open trade data (BACI cannot isolate capacitor-grade powder or the tantalum inside finished electronics), and no supply-chain study of the stage surfaced this pass. That absence is itself the finding — see Gaps & good next searches for the leads worth chasing.

The list methodologies and agency indexes that classify tantalum across the whole chain.

USGS National Minerals Information Center hub indexing all niobium/tantalum publications — Mineral Commodity Summaries (1996–2026) and Minerals Yearbook (2015–2023).
