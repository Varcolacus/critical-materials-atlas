# Primary / green-steel chain — research library

Iron ore is genuinely abundant; the real chokepoints are metallurgical coking coal and, for low-carbon "green steel," clean hydrogen plus a scarce high-grade ore. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Iron Ore](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-iron-ore.pdf) — USGS (2026). Annual world production/reserves data; Australia and Brazil dominance.
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Iron and Steel](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-iron-steel.pdf) — USGS (2026). Annual finished-steel production and trade data.
- [Iron and Steel Technology Roadmap](https://www.iea.org/reports/iron-and-steel-technology-roadmap) — IEA (2020). The reference roadmap for steel-sector decarbonisation pathways, including hydrogen-DRI.
- [World Steel in Figures 2025](https://worldsteel.org/data/world-steel-in-figures/) — worldsteel (2025). The industry's standard annual statistical reference.
- [CEPII BACI V202601](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII, based on UN Comtrade (2026). Harmonised bilateral trade-flow database used for the trade layer.

## Government & agency reports
- [Green Iron Opportunities in Australia](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/green-iron-opportunities-in-australia_bbd1e2b8-en.html) — OECD (2025). Assesses Australia's potential to export green iron rather than raw ore, addressing the high-grade-ore chokepoint directly.
- [Commodity Markets Outlook, April 2026](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/bitstreams/a384bcad-abc5-4b9b-9c1d-35f374581024/download) — World Bank (2026). Cross-commodity price outlook including iron ore and coal.
- [Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2026](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2026) — IEA (2026). Context on metallurgical-coal and iron-ore-adjacent supply dynamics within the broader minerals outlook.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [Australia's path to green iron](https://ieefa.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/BN_Australias%20path%20to%20green%20iron_Sep25.pdf) — Basirat & Steel, IEEFA briefing note (2025). Analyses the shift from raw ore exports to green-iron/DRI exports.
- [A Review of the Thermodynamic, Kinetic, and Economic Aspects of H2-based Direct Reduced Iron (DRI) Process](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40831-026-01573-1) — Pande, Choudhury, Gupta et al., Journal of Sustainable Metallurgy (2026). Technical review of hydrogen-DRI, the core green-steel process route.
- [The case for green hydrogen steel in China: A strategic analysis](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0360319926035019) — Boretti, International Journal of Hydrogen Energy (2026). Strategic analysis of hydrogen-steel adoption in the world's largest steel producer.
- [Resource and energy conservation in co-production of steel and chemicals](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0196890425011446) — Ji, Zhang, Shen, Zhang & Gao, Energy Conversion and Management (2026). Resource-efficiency angle on integrated steel production.
- [Decarbonizing the steel industry: a case study in Brazil utilizing nuclear-renewable hybrid energy systems](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10098-025-03286-7) — Santos & Velasquez, Clean Technologies and Environmental Policy (2025). Energy-supply case study for low-carbon steelmaking.

## Databases & data sources

- [Commodity Markets Outlook data files](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/bitstreams/76ad7738-f039-45cc-971a-2d5c6cf3a356/download) — World Bank (2026). Underlying price/production data behind the Outlook report, including iron ore and coal series.
