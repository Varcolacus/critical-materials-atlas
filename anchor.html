Method · the reconciliation, improved

The production-constrained anchor

A trade reconciliation should obey physics: a country can’t export more of a material than it produces minus what it consumes. Feed the reconciliation a consumption estimate built without trade, and that becomes a testable constraint — one that repairs the origin gap, where customs credit the refiner and re-exporter, not the mine.

Research / method layer. This demonstrates a technique on top of the atlas’s reconciled trade. The consumption input is an estimate (activity × calibrated intensity), and the blend weight is a documented default (w = 0.5). The direction and magnitude of the corrections are robust; treat the exact corrected percentages as indicative.
How it works. Three independent inputs: production (World Mining Data, per country) · consumption (trade-independent bottom-up = national activity × a calibrated material intensity) · observed exports (the atlas’s reconciled mirror trade). For each producer, exportable supply = max(0, production − consumption). Two levers: a hard guardrail (no origin can export more than it can supply) and a soft prior (pull a deleted origin back toward what production says it must be). We assert only the safe direction — a mine leader credited far less than it can supply (classic refiner-fronting) — and flag the rest. Reproducible: build_anchor.py → anchor.json.

Why this is the moat

Mirror-trade reconciliation (matching what exporters and importers each report) is well-trodden. Constraining it with physical production and an independent consumption estimate is not — and it is exactly what fixes the atlas’s hardest problem: the origin gap, where a material’s true source disappears from trade because it is refined and re-exported by someone else (DR Congo’s cobalt fronted by Chinese refiners; Indonesia’s nickel; South Africa’s platinum). The anchor puts the origin back, not by assertion but by a mass balance the data has to respect. The honest boundary is stage matching: the constraint holds only where the mined form is the traded form — lithium (spodumene → carbonate) and graphite (flake → processed) need the two-stage version, which is the next build.
