Nickel chain — laterite ore to battery cell

A curated reference library of public sources for deeper study — gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source and carries an abstract (or factual summary) plus the data it rests on. A research-support layer of the Critical Materials Atlas.

Nickel makes stainless steel and, increasingly, high-energy battery cathodes — and Indonesia rewrote its whole geography with one rule. Banning raw-ore exports forced smelting at home and pulled its share of world mine output from ~8% to ~61% in little over a decade. This chokepoint was built by policy, not laid down by a deposit.

Ordered to follow the chain: ① Laterite ore & Indonesia's export ban → ② smelting (RKEF/NPI class 2, HPAL → MHP class 1) → ③ the environmental bill → ④ stainless & battery cell — then the cross-cutting frameworks and trade data that sit across every stage.

② Smelting & refining — RKEF/NPI (class 2), HPAL → MHP (class 1) 4

Where laterite becomes metal: rotary-kiln electric furnaces make ferronickel/NPI for stainless (class 2); high-pressure acid leaching makes battery-grade intermediate (class 1).
  • The rise and rise of Indonesian HPAL — can it continue? · Wood Mackenzie Market-analyst view on the sustainability of Indonesia's HPAL boom. Summary. Wood Mackenzie: Indonesia's HPAL boom is cheap (under $1.5bn per plant vs $2–6bn in the West) and Chinese-backed, but every tonne of HPAL nickel makes about 1.4–1.6 t of waste — so tailings disposal, not technology, may cap growth. Data. Wood Mackenzie market analysis citing capex, capacity and waste-intensity figures.
  • Cradle to gate life-cycle assessment of battery grade nickel sulphate production through high-pressure acid leaching · Science of the Total Environment (2024) Process-based LCA of the Indonesian laterite-to-battery-nickel HPAL route. Abstract. Cradle-to-gate life-cycle assessment of battery-grade nickel sulphate made via high-pressure acid leaching of Indonesian laterite (the MHP route), quantifying the carbon and wider environmental burden of the dominant new supply. (ScienceDirect returned 403 this pass — scope from the title; figures not retrieved.) Data. Process-based LCA of the HPAL → MHP → nickel-sulphate route.
  • Improving process granularity of life cycle inventories for battery grade nickel · npj Materials Sustainability (2025) Argues generic ecoinvent nickel-sulfate datasets understate climate impact. Abstract. Argues that generic ecoinvent nickel-sulphate datasets can materially understate the climate impact of battery-grade nickel, and that finer process granularity in the inventory is needed to represent HPAL-route supply honestly. Life-cycle study of nickel sulphate for batteries, comparing mass- versus economic-allocation choices and their large effect on the reported global-warming potential of HPAL-route nickel — a methodological driver of how "dirty" battery nickel looks. (ScienceDirect abstract returned 403 this pass — scope from the title; figures not retrieved.) Data. Comparative LCA (mass vs economic allocation) of HPAL nickel-sulphate GWP.

③ The environmental bill — coal power & tailings 3

The speed of the build-out has a footprint: coal-fired smelting and large tailings volumes shape how clean the batteries built on this nickel really are.
  • Indonesia's electric vehicle batteries dream has a dirty nickel problem · Brookings On the environmental cost behind the battery-nickel buildout. Summary. Brookings: making battery-grade (class-1) nickel from Indonesian laterite emits roughly two-to-six times the CO₂ of sulphide routes, on a grid about 60% coal; nickel-processing parks already take ~15% of Indonesia's coal — the EV-battery paradox. Data. Brookings commentary citing carbon-intensity multiples, grid mix and investment figures.
  • New Report Details High Risk of Indonesia's Nickel Boom · Earthworks Advocacy-sector report on environmental and social risk in the buildout. Summary. Earthworks: Indonesia's nickel output rose from 130,000 to 2.31 Mt (2015–2024) ahead of safeguards; HPAL generates large volumes of toxic tailings, with dam failures, groundwater contamination (boron, chromium-6, nickel) and more than 40 industrial-park deaths reported. Data. Earthworks report using satellite imagery, incident records and tailings-facility technical assessment.
  • Indonesia halts deep-sea mining waste disposal · MINING.com Reporting on Indonesia's tailings-disposal policy shift under environmental pressure. Summary. MINING.com: under environmental pressure Indonesia halted deep-sea tailings disposal for nickel HPAL, pushing plants toward costlier land-based storage in a seismic, high-rainfall region. (Article returned 403 this pass — noted from the headline.) Data. Trade-press reporting — no primary dataset.

④ Stainless & battery cell — end use 0

Where the two nickels split for good: class-2 into stainless steel (~two-thirds of demand), class-1 into EV cathodes.
No dedicated, country-level public source maps nickel at the finished stainless-mill or battery-cell stage: stainless-demand structure (still ~two-thirds of nickel use) and cell-level nickel are under-covered in what this pass surfaced, and there is no clean production series to cite here. The split itself is carried in the smelting sources (RKEF/NPI vs HPAL/MHP) and the price signal in Fastmarkets. See Gaps & good next searches for the World Stainless Association and cathode-chemistry leads worth chasing.

Across the chain — criticality frameworks & policy 7

The outlooks, audits and assessments that classify nickel's supply risk and frame the policy response.
  • Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2025 — Executive summary · IEA (2025) Annual outlook covering nickel supply concentration, demand growth and prices. Summary. IEA: refining stays highly concentrated — for nickel about 90% of 2020–24 supply growth came from the single top supplier (Indonesia), lifting the top-three refiners to ~86%; nickel prices fell 10–20% in 2024 as output surged. Underpins the chain's concentration framing. Data. IEA analysis on company production data, S&P Global market data, Bloomberg prices and announced project pipelines.
  • The Role of Critical Minerals in Clean Energy Transitions — Reliable supply of minerals · IEA Foundational IEA critical-minerals report; supply-concentration framing. Summary. IEA foundational report: geographic concentration means disruption in one producer cascades globally. Indonesia and the Philippines hold ~45% of nickel output, with Indonesia driving roughly half of growth; battery-grade class-1 nickel is the binding quality constraint, most new supply being laterite. Data. IEA scenario modelling with S&P Global project-pipeline and lead-time data.
  • Special Report 04/2026: Critical raw materials for the energy transition · European Court of Auditors (2026) EU audit of critical-raw-materials policy; nickel among the strategic list. Summary. European Court of Auditors audit of EU critical-raw-materials policy for the energy transition, with nickel among the strategic materials assessed — the EU-demand-and-governance side of the chain. (The 3.8 MB PDF was not machine-readable this pass — scope from the title and metadata; findings not retrieved.) Data. ECA audit evidence: EU policy review, interviews and Commission data.
  • European Critical Raw Materials Act · European Commission Policy page; battery-grade nickel is on the EU's strategic raw materials list. Summary. European Commission page: the Critical Raw Materials Act sets 2030 EU benchmarks — 10% extraction, 40% processing and 25% recycling of annual need, and no more than 65% of any strategic material from a single third country; battery nickel is on the strategic list. Data. EU policy document — benchmark targets, no primary dataset.
  • System dynamics modeling of the global nickel supply system at a mine-level resolution · Journal of Industrial Ecology (2025) Prospective dynamic-criticality and resilience modelling of nickel supply. Abstract. Prospective system-dynamics model of global nickel supply at mine-level resolution, used to test dynamic criticality and the resilience of the nickel system under demand and disruption scenarios. (The journal host was blocked by network policy this pass — findings not retrieved.) Data. Mine-level system-dynamics simulation of the global nickel supply system.
  • The impact of country risk on nickel supply chain: complex network and panel regression analysis · Frontiers in Earth Science (2024) Network analysis of how political/economic country risk reshapes nickel trade. Abstract. Builds a 2000–2022 global nickel trade network and a panel regression linking political and economic country risk to trade structure. Finds trade increasingly concentrated and network stability weak; economic risk pushes importers to diversify supply, while political risk depresses exports. Data. Complex-network metrics plus panel regression on 2000–2022 nickel trade (ores, ferronickel, stainless).
  • Global supply chain analysis of nickel: importance and possibility of controlling the resource logistics · Metallurgical Research & Technology Supply-chain and resource-logistics framing of nickel criticality. Abstract. Material-flow analysis of global nickel, with remote sensing of mine land-use and a metallurgical-thermodynamic recycling lens. Stresses end-of-life recovery from vehicles and construction and better scrap sorting to cut primary nickel demand and CO₂ emissions. Data. Material-flow analysis plus remote sensing and metallurgical-thermodynamic recycling analysis.
