# Nickel chain — research library

Indonesia rewrote the nickel map with a single policy: banning raw-ore exports pushed its share of world mine output from ~8% to ~61% in little over a decade, dragging Chinese-financed smelting and, via HPAL, battery-grade nickel with it. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Nickel](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-nickel.pdf) — USGS (2026). Two-page nickel synopsis: production, reserves, salient statistics.
- [IEA, Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2025 — Executive summary](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2025/executive-summary) — IEA (2025). Annual outlook covering nickel supply concentration and demand growth.
- [BGS, World Mineral Statistics — nickel mine production](https://www2.bgs.ac.uk/mineralsuk/statistics/worldStatistics.html) — British Geological Survey (2024). Country-level mine production time series used for the atlas's own history chart.
- [CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII (2026). Reconciled bilateral trade database used for the chain's trade figures.

## Government & agency reports
- [USGS, Nickel Statistics and Information](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/nickel-statistics-and-information) — USGS National Minerals Information Center. Ongoing nickel data hub (historical statistics, commodity summaries archive).
- [Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 (full report)](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026.pdf) — USGS (2026). Full 90-plus-commodity volume nickel sits inside.
- [USITC, Indonesia's Export Ban of Nickel](https://www.usitc.gov/publications/332/working_papers/ermm_indonesia_export_ban_of_nickel.pdf) — US International Trade Commission working paper. Economic analysis of the ore-export ban's supply-chain effects.
- [USITC, Executive Briefing on Trade — Nickel and Indonesia, Part 2](https://www.usitc.gov/sites/default/files/publications/332/executive_briefings/ebot_nickel_and_indonesia_part_2.pdf) — USITC (May 2021). Briefing on Indonesia's rising role in global nickel supply.
- [IEA, The Role of Critical Minerals in Clean Energy Transitions — Reliable supply of minerals](https://www.iea.org/reports/the-role-of-critical-minerals-in-clean-energy-transitions/reliable-supply-of-minerals) — IEA. Foundational IEA critical-minerals report, supply-concentration framing.
- [European Court of Auditors, Special Report 04/2026: Critical raw materials for the energy transition](https://www.eca.europa.eu/ECAPublications/SR-2026-04/SR-2026-04_EN.pdf) — ECA (2026). EU audit of critical-raw-materials policy, nickel among the strategic list.
- [European Commission, European Critical Raw Materials Act](https://commission.europa.eu/topics/competitiveness/green-deal-industrial-plan/european-critical-raw-materials-act_en) — European Commission. Policy page; battery-grade nickel is on the EU's strategic raw materials list.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [Improving process granularity of life cycle inventories for battery grade nickel](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44296-025-00059-7) — npj Materials Sustainability (2025). Shows generic ecoinvent nickel-sulfate datasets can understate climate impact by ~120 kg CO2-eq/kg Ni.
- [Cradle to gate life-cycle assessment of battery grade nickel sulphate production through high-pressure acid leaching](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0048969724060583) — Science of the Total Environment (ScienceDirect, 2024). HPAL/MHP route LCA for Indonesian laterite-to-battery-nickel.
- [Assessing the environmental burden of nickel sulfate for batteries: A life cycle perspective](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0921344925000096) — ScienceDirect (2025). Mass vs economic allocation for HPAL-route nickel sulfate GWP.
- [Nationalist enclaves: Industrialising the critical mineral boom in Indonesia](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2214790X24001606) — ScienceDirect. Political-economy study of Indonesia's downstreaming policy and its smelter enclaves.
- [System dynamics modeling of the global nickel supply system at a mine-level resolution](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jiec.70072?af=R) — Journal of Industrial Ecology (2025). Prospective dynamic-criticality and resilience modelling of nickel supply.
- [The impact of country risk on nickel supply chain: complex network and panel regression analysis](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/earth-science/articles/10.3389/feart.2024.1487521/full) — Frontiers in Earth Science (2024). Network analysis of how political/economic country risk reshapes nickel trade flows.
- [Global supply chain analysis of nickel: importance and possibility of controlling the resource logistics](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/metallurgical-research-and-technology/article/abs/global-supply-chain-analysis-of-nickel-importance-and-possibility-of-controlling-the-resource-logistics/514C79AF8EE2E88B3C4174152A3DA31A) — Metallurgical Research & Technology (Cambridge Core). Supply-chain and resource-logistics framing of nickel criticality.
