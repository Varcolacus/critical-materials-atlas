# Fluorine (fluorspar → HF) chain — research library

One obscure mineral quietly gates chips, batteries and cooling at once: fluorspar → hydrogen fluoride is the shared gateway chemical behind semiconductor etch gases, the LiPF6/PVDF battery electrolyte and refrigerants, and China mines ~65% of it. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Fluorspar](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-fluorspar.pdf) — USGS (2026). Annual US and world fluorspar production, reserves and salient statistics.
- [IEA, Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2025](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2025/executive-summary) — IEA (2025). Flagship annual critical-minerals demand/supply outlook.
- [BGS World Mineral Statistics — fluorspar mine production](https://www2.bgs.ac.uk/mineralsuk/statistics/worldStatistics.html) — British Geological Survey (2024). Historical world mine-production database used for the chain's China-share history chart.
- [CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII (2026). Harmonised bilateral trade database used for the atlas's own reconciliation.

## Gaps & good next searches
- No single authoritative public dataset separates HF production or fluorochemical output (LiPF6, NF3/WF6, HFC/HFO) by country the way USGS/BGS does for raw fluorspar — the chain's own trade_intro flags this same limitation in BACI/HS codes. Good next search: "hydrogen fluoride production capacity by country" or IEA/OECD chemical-industry statistics.
- Coverage of the semiconductor-gas branch (NF3, WF6) specifically is thin in this pass — worth a dedicated search: "NF3 tungsten hexafluoride semiconductor etch gas supply chain China".
- Coverage of HFC/HFO refrigerant-specific fluorspar demand and the Kigali Amendment's effect on fluorochemical demand was not directly searched — worth: "Kigali Amendment HFO refrigerant fluorspar demand forecast".
- No Zenodo/DOI-style dataset or peer-reviewed criticality paper specific to fluorspar (vs. general criticality-methodology papers) was found in this pass beyond the Iran case study and the China MFA study — a targeted Resources Policy / Journal of Cleaner Production search on "fluorspar criticality China 2024 2025" could surface more recent work.
- US revival project details (ARES Strategic Mining Lost Sheep, Utah processing facility) came from a secondary aggregator search result, not a primary source — worth verifying against a company/SEC filing or USGS confirmation before citing as fact.
