# Interpretation notes

## The narrative

Battery minerals are older than the EV market. The structural change is that batteries rapidly became a much larger source of lithium, cobalt and nickel demand, while refining and electrochemical component production concentrated in a different geography from mining.

The chain is also dynamic. LFP removes nickel and cobalt from the cathode and increased from just under 30% of EV battery deployment in 2022 to nearly half in 2024. That chemistry shift reduced some mineral exposure but introduced a highly concentrated LFP-material and cell supply chain.

## Measures that remain separate

- Total mineral production includes every end use.
- Battery-grade refining is narrower than general metal or chemical refining.
- Active-material production is physical output, while factory capacity is potential output.
- Cell production is measured in energy terms; customs data are primarily values and masses.
- HS 850760 combines cells, modules and packs.
- A plant's location and its owner's headquarters are different geographies.

## Exclusions

- Lithium gross-weight observations before 2000 are not joined to lithium-content output.
- No annual battery-price series is reconstructed from the IEA's 90% two-point claim.
- No values are interpolated between the 2017/2022 demand or 2022/2024 chemistry anchors.
- Announced factories are not treated as operating capacity.
- Recycling is shown as a chain loop but not assigned a current primary-supply displacement share.
