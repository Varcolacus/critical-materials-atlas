# Lithium-ion battery chain — research library

The battery metals are mined all over — the battery is made in China: lithium, cobalt and nickel come out of the ground in Australia, the DR Congo and Indonesia, but battery-grade refining, cathode/anode production and cell assembly each run ~80-90% through China. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [IEA, Global EV Outlook 2025 — Electric vehicle batteries](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2025/electric-vehicle-batteries) — IEA (2025). Battery demand, chemistry mix and manufacturing capacity by country.
- [IEA, Global EV Outlook 2026 — Manufacturing and trade](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/manufacturing-and-trade) — IEA (2026). Latest cell/cathode/anode manufacturing and trade shares.
- [IEA, Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2025 — Beyond NMC batteries: supply chain issues for emerging battery technologies](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2025/beyond-nmc-batteries-supply-chain-issues-for-emerging-battery-technologies) — IEA (2025). China's cathode/anode/cell shares and the LFP/sodium-ion transition.
- [IEA, Batteries and Secure Energy Transitions — Status of battery demand and supply](https://www.iea.org/reports/batteries-and-secure-energy-transitions/status-of-battery-demand-and-supply) — IEA (2024). Full-chain demand/supply status report.
- [IEA, Global EV Outlook 2023 — Trends in batteries](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2023/trends-in-batteries) — IEA (2023). Earlier baseline for chemistry and cost trends.
- [CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII (2026). Harmonised bilateral trade data used for the chain's trade panel.

## Government & agency reports
- [USGS, Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Lithium](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-lithium.pdf) — USGS (2026). Global lithium production/consumption; battery demand driving growth.
- [USGS, Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Cobalt](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-cobalt.pdf) — USGS (2026). China as leading refined-cobalt producer/consumer; cobalt-free LFP substitution.
- [USGS, Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 (full volume)](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026.pdf) — USGS (2026). All 90 nonfuel commodities, published Feb 2026.
- [British Geological Survey, World Mineral Statistics](https://www.bgs.ac.uk/mineralsuk/statistics/world-mineral-statistics/) — BGS. UK/world mine-production statistics dataset since 1913, covers lithium and cobalt.
- [JRC, deep dive on critical raw materials (foresight study underpinning the EU CRMA)](https://publications.jrc.ec.europa.eu/repository/bitstream/JRC141715/jrc141715_01.pdf) — European Commission Joint Research Centre. EU cathode/anode production vs demand gap (e.g. EU produced ~1 GWh of cathodes against ~68 GWh demand).
- [Li-Bridge / DOE, Building a Robust and Resilient U.S. Lithium Battery Supply Chain (Feb 2023)](https://netl.doe.gov/sites/default/files/2023-03/Li-Bridge%20-%20Building%20a%20Robust%20and%20Resilient%20U.S.%20Lithium%20Battery%20Supply%20Chain.pdf) — DOE / Argonne National Laboratory (2023). U.S. federal action plan and 2030 domestic-supply-chain targets.
- [EIA, China dominates global trade of battery minerals](https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=65305) — U.S. Energy Information Administration. China's share of interregional battery-mineral trade (imports/exports).

## Academic papers & reviews
- [Sun et al., "Supply risks of lithium-ion battery materials: An entire supply chain estimation"](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S2468606919302035) — ScienceDirect (2019). Extends criticality analysis beyond the mine stage to downstream processing.
- [Olivetti et al., "Lithium-Ion Battery Supply Chain Considerations: Analysis of Potential Bottlenecks in Critical Metals"](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2542435117300442) — Joule / ScienceDirect (2017). Foundational bottleneck analysis for Li, Co, Ni, graphite.
- ["Global Competition in the Lithium-Ion Battery Supply Chain: A Novel Perspective for Criticality Analysis"](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acs.est.1c03376) — Environmental Science & Technology (2021). Trade-based criticality lens on lithium hydroxide/carbonate competition.
- ["Insights into the Critical Materials Supply Chain of the Battery Market for Enhanced Energy Security"](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsenergylett.4c01300) — ACS Energy Letters (2024). Material-flow view plus end-of-life/recycling as a supply lever.

## Gaps & good next searches
- No dedicated OECD or NEA report on batteries surfaced directly — worth a targeted search for "OECD battery raw materials trade" or "OECD critical minerals policy tracker."
- Recycling/second-life battery material flows are only lightly covered here (ACS Energy Letters 2024 touches it) — a deeper search on "battery recycling material flow analysis EU/US 2026" would strengthen the circularity angle the chain's "engineered down" thread gestures at.
- Sodium-ion and solid-state battery supply-chain implications are mentioned in IEA 2025 but not independently sourced — search "sodium-ion battery supply chain China CATL 2026" for a dedicated reference.
- Graphite/anode-specific chokepoint detail (China's ~90%+ share) is asserted across several sources here but would benefit from the dedicated graphite-chain library rather than duplicating primary sources in this one.
- Company-level (CATL, BYD, LG Energy Solution, Panasonic) capacity and investment data was not directly pulled — Benchmark Mineral Intelligence and company IR filings would be the next stop for that granularity.
