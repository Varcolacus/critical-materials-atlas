A battery is a changing recipe, not a single mineral

The raw materials have century-scale industrial histories, but electric vehicles rapidly changed what they are used for. The tightest battery bottlenecks now sit in battery-grade refining, cathode and anode materials, and cell manufacturing—and the chemistry itself keeps moving.

Mine ≠ battery-grade refining ≠ active materials ≠ cells ≠ capacity ≠ customs trade

The correction. “Lithium supply” is not the battery supply chain. A working cell also needs anode material, a cathode chemistry, separators, electrolyte and manufacturing equipment. Different chemistries change the mineral basket.

The chain in one glance

The minerals came before the battery boom

USGS histories establish the long industrial baseline. Select a mineral to see total world output across every end use. The terminal years differ, and lithium’s comparable content series begins only in 2000.

Then batteries changed mineral demand

Between 2017 and 2022, the EV-battery share of demand quadrupled for lithium, tripled for cobalt and increased fivefold for nickel. These are demand shares—not mine-production shares.

Share of mineral demand used in EV batteries

2017 and 2022 IEA anchors. Other batteries and non-battery applications are outside this measure.

Where ore comes from is not where battery chemicals come from

The 2023 physical chain changes geography between mining and refining. Graphite is the exception where the same country dominates both, and battery-grade processing is more concentrated than extraction.

The downstream chain is tighter still

Current IEA production measures show concentration increasing from vehicle assembly back toward electrochemical components.

Capacity is not production

Global cell capacity exceeded 3 TWh/year in 2024—about three times EV and storage demand. China held roughly 85% of nameplate capacity but 80% of actual cell production. Factory announcements therefore cannot be read as realised supply.

Costs transformed the market

Average battery costs fell about 90% from 2010 to 2023. This is a two-point indexed result, not a fabricated annual price series. Scale, chemistry and manufacturing learning all matter.

The recipe changed

LFP avoids nickel and cobalt, trading some energy density for lower cost and other advantages. Its share of the global EV battery market rose from just under 30% in 2022 to nearly half in 2024.

The diversification of chemistry did not diversify production: China produced over 98% of LFP cathode material and cells in 2024.

The customs ledger starts later

The clean HS17 lithium-ion accumulator series runs from 2017 to 2024. It mixes cells, modules and packs; upstream chemical codes include non-battery grades and uses. Trade therefore remains context, not a factory-location measure.
