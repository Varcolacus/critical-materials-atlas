# Aerospace evidence ledger

What each source supports. Machine-readable values and full URLs are in
`out/aerospace_chain.json`, generated by `record_aerospace.py`.

## 1. Rhenium (superalloy element)

- USGS MCS 2026 (Rhenium): world mine+recovery production ~81,000 kg in 2025;
  Chile ~55% of world **mine** production (porphyry-copper molybdenum by-product).
- End uses: ~80% superalloys (high-temperature turbine components), ~15%
  petroleum-reforming catalysts.
- Secondary (recycled) rhenium led by the United States and Germany.
- Not read from trade: rhenium has no clean HS6 customs line.

## 2. Titanium sponge (structure)

- USGS MCS 2025 (Titanium): China ~71% of world sponge production in 2024;
  Japan ~17% and Russia ~13% (2022 shares); Kazakhstan and Ukraine smaller.
- Argus (2024): aerospace-approved sponge is a narrow, allied subset; most
  Chinese sponge is not qualified for critical aerospace parts.
- USGS: the United States has no domestic sponge production and imports it, ~80%
  from Japan, ~13% Saudi Arabia, ~9% Kazakhstan.
- Trade context (HS 810820) is shown but does not distinguish grade.

## 3. Single-crystal blade casting (capability)

- No public annual country-production series exists. Described qualitatively as a
  capability concentrated among specialist casters and OEM foundries in the US,
  UK, France and Japan, with China developing capability. Company control, not
  country tonnage, is the correct lens.

## 4. Jet engines (assembly)

- Secondary market analyses (Mordor Intelligence; Simple Flying, 2024): overall
  commercial engine share ≈ GE Aerospace 55% (incl. CFM JV with Safran), Pratt &
  Whitney 26%, Rolls-Royce 18%; narrow-body CFM >60% / P&W ~35%; market ~$81 bn
  in 2024. Shares are approximate and vary by segment and metric.

## Trade context

- CEPII BACI V202601 (UN Comtrade), HS 810820 (Ti unwrought/sponge), 750210
  (Ni unwrought), 841112 (turbojets >25 kN), 841122 (turboprops >1,100 kW),
  841191 (engine parts), 2002–2024. Context only; captive transfers, qualification
  and single-crystal castings are outside customs data.
