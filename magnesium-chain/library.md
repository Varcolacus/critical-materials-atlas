# Magnesium chain — research library

China makes roughly 87% of the world's primary magnesium, mostly via the coal-fired Pidgeon process — and a 2021 energy-curb episode left European aluminium and auto supply chains weeks from running out. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Magnesium Metal](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-magnesium-metal.pdf) — USGS (2026). China's production share, world supply/demand.
- [BGS, World Mineral Statistics — magnesium metal production](https://www2.bgs.ac.uk/mineralsuk/statistics/worldStatistics.html) — BGS (2024). Long-run production series by country.
- [European Commission, Study on the Critical Raw Materials for the EU 2023](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/raw-materials/areas-specific-interest/critical-raw-materials_en) — European Commission (2023). Criticality assessment including magnesium.
- [CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. Harmonised bilateral trade database used for the atlas's own flows.

## Government & agency reports
- [Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 (main volume)](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/mcs2026) — USGS (2026). Cross-commodity reserves/production reference.
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 — Magnesium Metal](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025-magnesium-metal.pdf) — USGS (2025). Prior-year figures for comparison; notes the sole US primary smelter's 2025 bankruptcy filing.
- [USGS Minerals Yearbook 2022 — Magnesium Metal](https://pubs.usgs.gov/myb/vol1/2022/myb1-2022-magnesium-metal.pdf) — USGS (2022). Detailed annual industry structure and end-use breakdown.
- [Eurofer — "Joint statement on magnesium supply shortage"](https://www.eurofer.eu/publications/position-papers/joint-statement-on-magnesium-supply-shortage) — European Steel Association (2021). Cross-industry warning during the China energy-curb shock.
- [European Aluminium — position paper on the imminent magnesium shortage](https://european-aluminium.eu/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/2021-09-27-position-paper_impact-of-imminent-short-1.pdf) — European Aluminium (2021). EU ~95% import dependence on China; sector impact assessment.
- [ACEA — "Magnesium supply shortage: cross-industry coalition and workers warn against catastrophic impact"](https://www.acea.auto/news/magnesium-supply-shortage-cross-industry-coalition-and-workers-warn-against-catastrophic-impact/) — European Automobile Manufacturers' Association (2021). Automotive-sector warning during the 2021 shock.
- [USITC — "Magnesium Price Spike: A Flash in the Pan?"](https://www.usitc.gov/publications/332/working_papers/magnesium_wp_compliant_.pdf) — US International Trade Commission working paper. Economic analysis of a prior magnesium price spike.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [Life cycle assessment of primary magnesium production using the Pidgeon process in China](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11367-009-0101-9) — International Journal of Life Cycle Assessment / Springer (2009). Cradle-to-gate footprint (~42 kg CO2e/kg Mg ingot).
- [Research on the Process, Energy Consumption and Carbon Emissions of Different Magnesium Refining Processes](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10179423/) — PMC (2023). Comparison of Pidgeon vs. alternative reduction routes.
- [Prospective life cycle assessment of an electric vehicle equipped with a model magnesium battery](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13705-024-00475-y) — Energy, Sustainability and Society / Springer (2024). Forward-looking magnesium-battery LCA, adjacent to the Pidgeon-process literature.

## Gaps & good next searches
- No dedicated academic criticality-index paper specifically scoring magnesium was found (as exists for lithium/cobalt) — search "magnesium criticality assessment Resources Policy" directly.
- Post-2022 Western smelter restart status (US Utah operator's 2025 bankruptcy, EU/US reshoring plans) is only lightly covered — search "US primary magnesium smelter restart 2026" and "West Virginia / Nevada magnesium project" for the latest.
- No IEA-specific magnesium chapter was located (IEA's critical-minerals coverage is lithium/cobalt/nickel/copper/rare-earth focused) — worth confirming magnesium's absence/inclusion in the newest Global Critical Minerals Outlook.
- Desulphurising-steel and die-casting end-use quantification is thin — search USGS Minerals Yearbook end-use tables for magnesium.
