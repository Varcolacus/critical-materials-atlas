# Silver chain — research library

Silver is mostly a by-product of lead, zinc, copper and gold mining, so supply cannot respond to its own price — and solar demand is running ahead of it. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Silver](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-silver.pdf) — USGS (2026). Annual world production, reserves and by-product supply data for silver.
- [World Silver Survey](https://www.silverinstitute.org/all-world-silver-surveys/) — The Silver Institute (2025). The industry's benchmark annual supply/demand/fabrication report.
- [Solar PV Global Supply Chains](https://www.iea.org/reports/solar-pv-global-supply-chains) — IEA (2022). Maps the PV manufacturing chain, including silver paste use in cells.
- [CEPII BACI V202601](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII, based on UN Comtrade (2026). Harmonised bilateral trade-flow database used for the trade layer.

## Government & agency reports
- [Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2026](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2026) — IEA (2026). Annual assessment of critical mineral supply/demand, useful context for by-product metals.
- [Methodology and technical input for the 2021 review and revision of the US Critical Minerals List](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/ofr20211045) — Nassar & Fortier, USGS Open-File Report 2021-1045 (2021). Explains how USGS screens metals (including by-product ones) for criticality.
- [Commodity Markets Outlook, April 2026](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/bitstreams/a384bcad-abc5-4b9b-9c1d-35f374581024/download) — World Bank (2026). Cross-commodity price and supply outlook covering precious/industrial metals.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [Evaluation of long-term silver supply shortage for c-Si PV under different technological scenarios](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/nrm.12176) — Li & Adachi, Natural Resource Modeling (2019). Models whether by-product silver supply can keep pace with PV cell demand.
- [A critical review of leaching pathways for silver recovery from EoL photovoltaic modules and prospects for flotation](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/08827508.2025.2569684) — Saffarian, Galvin & Firouzi, Mineral Processing and Extractive Metallurgy (2026). Recycling routes for silver from retired solar panels.
- [Resource scarcity challenges of photovoltaics in Europe 2050](https://pubs.rsc.org/se/article-abstract/doi/10.1039/D5SE01467K/1288238) — Kanz, Wulf & Zapp, Sustainable Energy & Fuels (2026). Long-run material-scarcity modelling for PV, including silver.
- [Supply chain analysis and material demand forecast in strategic technologies and sectors in the EU – A foresight study](https://publications.jrc.ec.europa.eu/repository/handle/JRC132889) — Carrara, Bobba, Blagoeva et al., JRC (2023). Widely-cited EU foresight study on material demand across clean-tech chains including silver.
- [Critical Minerals and the Global Supply Chain](https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-resource-112624-125817) — Chepeliev, Bekkers, Iyer, Qiu et al., Annual Review of Resource Economics (2026). Economics-literature survey of critical mineral supply chains.

## Databases & data sources
- [UN Comtrade Plus](https://comtradeplus.un.org/) — United Nations. Official international merchandise trade statistics platform underlying BACI.
- [Commodity Markets Outlook data files](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/bitstreams/76ad7738-f039-45cc-971a-2d5c6cf3a356/download) — World Bank (2026). Underlying price/production data behind the Outlook report.

## Gaps & good next searches
- Silver is not on the EU's own Critical Raw Materials list (it fails the "economic importance" screen despite supply concentration risk) — worth searching directly for academic critiques of that omission.
- Independent market-intelligence coverage (Metals Focus, CRU, Fastmarkets, Benchmark Mineral Intelligence) was not reachable this session — search these directly for 2025/2026 price and by-product-supply commentary.
- USGS's National Minerals Information Center silver statistics page (historical time series, not just the annual MCS PDF) is worth fetching directly: usgs.gov National Minerals Information Center, Silver.
- A dedicated material-flow-analysis (MFA) paper quantifying what share of world silver mine supply is truly by-product vs. primary-silver-mine output would strengthen the chain's core claim — search Resources, Conservation and Recycling or Journal of Industrial Ecology.
