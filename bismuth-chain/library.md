# Bismuth chain — research library

The non-toxic heavy metal that replaces lead — and China makes and refines roughly 80% of it, as a by-product of lead and tungsten smelting. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Bismuth](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-bismuth.pdf) — USGS (2026). Annual production, reserves, US import reliance.
- [BGS World Mineral Statistics — bismuth](https://www2.bgs.ac.uk/mineralsuk/statistics/worldStatistics.html) — British Geological Survey (2024). Long-run mine-production series.
- [European Commission, Critical Raw Materials 2023 — bismuth](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/raw-materials/areas-specific-interest/critical-raw-materials_en) — European Commission (2023). EU criticality assessment.
- [CEPII BACI V202601](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII, based on UN Comtrade (2026). Bilateral trade database used for the chain's trade panel.

## Government & agency reports
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries — Bismuth, 2020](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2020/mcs2020-bismuth.pdf) / [2021](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2021/mcs2021-bismuth.pdf) / [2022](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2022/mcs2022-bismuth.pdf) / [2024](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2024/mcs2024-bismuth.pdf) — USGS. Year-by-year annual summaries showing China's ~80% share and US import dependence trend.
- [2010 Minerals Yearbook — Bismuth](https://d9-wret.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com/assets/palladium/production/mineral-pubs/bismuth/myb1-2010-bismu.pdf) — USGS. Detailed annual chapter: by-product recovery, world review, US primary-lead-smelter closure context.
- [BISMUTH — CRM 2020 Factsheet](https://scrreen.eu/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/BISMUTH_CRM_2020_Factsheets_critical_Final.pdf) — SCRREEN2 (EU Horizon project) (2020). EU criticality factsheet: supply risk, economic importance, substitution.
- [Bismuth | CRM Alliance](https://www.crmalliance.eu/bismuth) — CRM Alliance (EU). Policy-facing profile of bismuth as an EU critical raw material.
- [Production of mineral commodities and geospatial map of the mineral industries and related infrastructure of China](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/ofr20261018/full) — USGS Open-File Report (2026). China's mineral-industry infrastructure, relevant to bismuth's China concentration.
- [Assessment of critical minerals supply chain for the United States in perspective of trade restriction by foreign countries](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0301420725002685) — ScienceDirect. US-focused analysis of trade-restriction exposure, incl. bismuth's 2024 Chinese export controls.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [Bismuth: Economic geology and value chains](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0169136822000300) — ScienceDirect (Ore Geology Reviews, 2022). Deposit geology and full value-chain academic review of bismuth.
- [Thermodynamic and Microstructural Analysis of Lead-Free Machining Aluminium Alloys with Indium and Bismuth Additions](https://doi.org/10.3390/ma16186241) — *Materials* (MDPI), also on [PMC](https://ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC10532454). Peer-reviewed study of bismuth as a lead-free machinability additive.
- [Comparative study on the machinability of lead-free brass](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0959652617303128) — ScienceDirect (Journal of Cleaner Production). Academic comparison of bismuth vs other lead-free brass additives.
- [Bismuth Alloys — an overview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/topics/earth-and-planetary-sciences/bismuth-alloys) — ScienceDirect Topics. Reference summary of bismuth alloy chemistry and fusible-alloy uses.
- [Free-Cutting Aluminium Alloys with Tin as Substitution for Lead](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00501-008-0390-5) — *BHM Berg- und Hüttenmännische Monatshefte* (Springer). Comparative lead-substitution metallurgy (tin vs bismuth).
