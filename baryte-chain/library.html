Research Library · Baryte

Baryte / drilling-mud chain

A curated reference library of public sources for deeper study — gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source and carries an abstract (or factual summary) plus the data it rests on. A research-support layer of the Critical Materials Atlas.

You cannot drill an oil or gas well without baryte: ground fine, its exceptional density weights the mud that holds back downhole pressure so the well does not blow out. Yet it is mined across India, China, Morocco, Kazakhstan and the US and ground near where wells are drilled. There is no chokepoint to find — the mine is the most diversified stage, and the risk is only that an essential input is invisible until it is short.

Ordered to follow the chain: ① Baryte ore (mined — India / China / Morocco) → ② ground to API drilling-grade powder → ③ the drilling mud & the well (plus barium chemicals, filler and medical uses) — then the cross-cutting frameworks and trade data that sit across every stage.

① Baryte ore — the mine and where it forms 7

The raw input, and the most diversified stage: India, China, Morocco, Kazakhstan and the US — no single country dominates.
  • USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Barite · USGS (2026) Annual two-page synopsis: production, reserves, import sources, salient statistics for barite. Abstract. USGS commodity summary: in 2025 three companies mined barite at four Nevada operations (data withheld); ~2.3 Mt was sold by US crushers/grinders. Typically more than 90% of US barite is used as a weighting agent in oil- and gas-drilling fluids. Net import reliance exceeds 75% (India 39%, China 21%, Morocco 19%, Mexico 14%); no large-scale drilling substitute exists. Data. USGS annual producer canvass (much withheld), reported import/export trade, world mine-production and reserves estimates, and rig-count indicators.
  • BGS World Mineral Statistics — barytes · British Geological Survey (2024) Free searchable world production and trade statistics database, used for the chain's history chart. Summary. BGS's World Mineral Statistics is a searchable database of annual world mine-production and trade figures by commodity and country, including barytes. The atlas draws the baryte mine-production time series from this series (alongside USGS) to build the diversified-mine history chart. Data. BGS-compiled national mine-production and trade returns (multi-decade annual series) — a database, not a paper.
  • World's barite resources as critical raw material · Mining Science and Technology (Russia) (2023) Academic treatment of global barite resources framed through the critical-raw-material lens. Abstract. Boyarko & Bolsunovskaya trace barite production from ~0.3 Mt/yr in the 1920s to 8.0–9.6 Mt/yr by 2010, with ~550 Mt cumulatively produced (1920–2020) and ~740 Mt of exploitable resources. Critical importing economies represent 38.8% of world GDP; new deposits in Iran, Kazakhstan and Pakistan and marine sediments could reduce criticality. Data. Synthesis of long-run world production, trade and reserve/resource statistics, plus importer/exporter GDP-share analysis — no new experimental dataset.
  • Barite Deposits of Türkiye: A Review · MDPI Minerals (2025) Review of Turkish barite deposit genesis (magmatism-associated vs. passive-margin-hosted). Abstract. Türkiye's barite deposits fall into two groups by tectonic setting: magmatism-associated (e.g. Kızılcaören carbonatite-related F+Ba+REE+Th; the Cretaceous Pontide-arc Kirazören and Karacaören) and passive-margin-hosted concordant/discordant veins in Palaeozoic lithologies of the Taurides and Arabian Platform. The Palaeozoic belt is the country's main resource, producing ~300,000 t/yr. Data. Review of deposit geology plus Pb and Sr isotope data across Turkish barite districts.
  • The Ballynoe Stratiform Barite Deposit, Silvermines, County Tipperary, Ireland · MDPI Minerals (2024) Case study of an Irish-type exhalative barite deposit and its formation mechanism. Abstract. The Ballynoe barite deposit is a conformable Lower Carboniferous horizon whose geometry follows footwall palaeotopography. Precipitated as a cryptocrystalline mud, later stylolitised and recrystallised, it shows compelling evidence of early local exhalation — pyritised worm tubes and haematised filaments of apparent microbial origin — and strontium with a seawater 87Sr/86Sr signature. Data. Field geology, sedimentary/petrographic textures, palaeontology and strontium-isotope data from the deposit.
  • Barite Replacement as a Key Factor in the Genesis of Sediment-Hosted Zn-Pb±Ba and Barite-Sulfide Deposits (Iran) · MDPI Minerals (2024) Ore-fluid and isotope study of barite genesis in Iran's 350+ sediment-hosted Zn-Pb-Ba deposits. Abstract. Iran hosts 350+ Precambrian–Cenozoic sediment-hosted Zn-Pb±Ba and barite-sulfide deposits (SHMS/SEDEX, Irish-type, MVT) in which barite is common — as disseminations, banded zebra textures, veins and massive lenses. In many giant Irish-type and stratabound deposits, sulfide ore replaces an early barite envelope, so barite replacement is a key control on genesis. Data. Petrographic textures plus sulfur- and strontium-isotope signatures across Iranian sediment-hosted deposits.
  • Origin and occurrence of gem-quality, skarn-hosted barite from Jebel Ouichane near Nador, Morocco · Scientific Reports (2021) Deposit-genesis study specific to Morocco, one of the chain's three leading producers. Abstract. Light-blue gem-quality barite from Jebel Ouichane occurs in iron-ore skarns in Jurassic–Cretaceous limestones. Fluid-inclusion and oxygen/sulfur-isotope data indicate slow, surface-reaction-controlled precipitation below 100 °C from low-salinity fluids mixing diluted meteoric water with magmatic-hydrothermal fluids, with Ba and Sr from altered carbonate and silicate minerals. Data. Crystallography, fluid-inclusion microthermometry, and δ¹⁸O / sulfur-isotope geochemistry of the Nador barite.

② Grinding to drilling-grade powder 2

Where the ore becomes the product: milled to a controlled-density, API-grade powder near where wells are drilled.
  • American Petroleum Institute — drilling-fluid (mud) specifications · API (2024) Industry specifications governing drilling-grade barite density and purity. Summary. The American Petroleum Institute publishes the drilling-fluid material specifications (e.g. API 13A) that define barite grades by specific gravity and purity — including the 4.20-SG standard and the 4.10-SG alternate issued in 2010 as high-grade reserves tightened. These specs set what counts as drilling-grade powder. Data. API consensus material specifications — engineering standards, not a dataset. (Landing page only; specific standard documents sit behind API's publications store.)
  • Fastmarkets — launch of three monthly barite price assessments · Fastmarkets (2023) Price-reporting agency's barite grade-specific monthly assessments for the drilling market. Summary. From 31 Aug 2023 Fastmarkets began three monthly barite assessments — FOB China, Chennai (India) and Morocco — for API-grade SG-4.10 unground lump, as western buyers switched from the scarcer 4.20 grade. The three producers were the world's top barite producers in 2022. Data. Fastmarkets spot price assessments (monthly, 1,000 t minimum) — market-reported prices, not government data.

③ Drilling mud & the well — the dominant use 9

Where nearly all baryte goes: weighting the mud that controls downhole pressure — and why nothing cheap replaces it.
  • Barite — A Case Study of Import Reliance on an Essential Material for Oil and Gas Exploration and Development Drilling (SIR 2014-5230) · USGS Scientific Investigations Report (2015) Detailed USGS case study of barite as a weighting agent and the US import-reliance picture. Abstract. Bleiwas & Miller frame barite as an import-reliant essential: its high specific gravity makes it the only mineral used in bulk to weight water-based drilling mud, primarily to prevent explosive releases. In 2012 US mine output (~670,000 t) met only ~20% of drilling demand — a third of 1980 output; imports were led by China (77% of imports), India, Morocco and Mexico. Data. USGS production, import and reserve/resource statistics with drilling-industry end-use data (2012 baseline, historical back to 1980).
  • Effect of Different Weighting Agents on Drilling Fluids and Filter Cake Properties in Sandstone Formations · ACS Omega (2021) Compares barite against alternative weighting agents on rheology and filter-cake performance. Abstract. Water-based drilling fluids were weighted with barite, micromax, ilmenite and hematite at equal concentration, then measured for rheology (Fann viscometer, 80 °F) and filtration (200 °F, 300 psi) over sandstone. Particle size and mineralogy of each agent were characterised; the study quantifies how each weighting material changes fluid and filter-cake behaviour. Data. Laboratory measurements — particle-size/mineralogy, rheological and filtration tests on water-based mud samples.
  • A Combined Barite–Ilmenite Weighting Material to Prevent Barite Sag in Water-Based Drilling Fluid · Materials (Basel) (2019) Technical study on blending barite with ilmenite to solve the "barite sag" settling problem. Abstract. Barite sag — weighting particles settling out and causing density swings — threatens well control in HPHT drilling. Blending 25/50/75 wt% ilmenite with barite in water-based fluid raised density while holding pH and rheology; 50 wt% ilmenite cut the sag factor to 0.51 (static) and 0.33 (dynamic) with no filtration penalty, an effective, cost-efficient fix. Data. Static and dynamic laboratory sag, density, pH, rheology and filtration tests on barite–ilmenite blends.
  • Delaminated iron ore (hematite-barite) as alternative weighting agent to barite in petroleum drilling fluids · Ain Shams Engineering Journal (2020) Investigates hematite-barite blends as a substitution route, underscoring how hard true substitution is. Abstract. Motivated by the cost of barite-based mud, the study tests delaminated iron ore (hematite-barite) as a novel high-reserve weighting material. Mud samples loaded with the delaminated ore reached the same 9.6–18.7 lb/gal density range as barite and showed comparable rheology, mud-cake thickness and fluid-loss behaviour, indicating a workable alternative in specific settings. Data. Laboratory mud formulations across a density range, with rheology, mud-cake and fluid-loss measurements plus a mechanism study.
  • The Crucial Role of Barite in the Global Oil and Gas Industry · Trident Energy International Industry-side explainer on barite's function and supply geography. Summary. Explainer: barite's 4.2–4.5 specific gravity makes it the weighting agent that controls downhole pressure, prevents blowouts and stabilises the wellbore. Production concentrates in a handful of regions — China largest, then India, Morocco and Mexico — leaving major consumers such as the US import-reliant. Data. No primary data — descriptive industry commentary on barite function and producer geography.
  • Focus on: Barite — a Crucial Critical Mineral Resource · Mining Digital Trade-press overview of barite's supply-chain complexity and geopolitical exposure. Summary. Trade-press overview: ~80% of barite consumption is oil-and-gas drilling; China's share fell from 44% (2012) to 36% (2022) as India, Morocco and Kazakhstan grew and the US works to revive domestic output. Frames barite risk as concentration, environmental rules and resource nationalism, not a single chokepoint. Data. Secondary reporting citing production-share figures and industry commentary — no primary dataset.
  • Barite Market Tight as China Supply Decreases · Rigzone Market reporting on a China-driven barite tightness episode affecting drilling operators. Summary. Trade-press report on an episode of barite market tightness driven by falling Chinese supply and its effect on drilling operators. (Page not retrievable on this pass — redirect loop; noted from the headline and the atlas's prior citation only.) Data. Trade-press market reporting — no primary dataset.
  • Schlumberger to address barite shortage · Energy News Bulletin Reporting on an oilfield-services response to a barite supply shortage. Summary. Report on an oilfield-services (Schlumberger) response to a barite supply shortage. (Page behind an access wall — HTTP 403, not retrievable this pass; noted from the headline and the atlas's prior citation only.) Data. Trade-press reporting — no primary dataset.
  • Barite Market 2026 — Prices, Producers, Supply Outlook · Critical Minerals HQ Current market snapshot: China production vs domestic use, export surplus, price outlook. Summary. Market snapshot: China's ~9.26 Mt output against ~1.4 Mt domestic use leaves a large exportable surplus, shaping global barite production and export dynamics. (URL returned HTTP 404 on this pass — the page appears to have moved; figures noted from the atlas's prior citation.) Data. Market-analysis snapshot — production/use/price estimates, no primary dataset.

Across the chain — criticality frameworks & policy 4

The list methodologies and industry bodies that classify and represent the whole chain.
  • Critical Mineral Resources: National Policy and Critical Minerals List (R47982) · Congressional Research Service (2024) Legislative/policy background on the U.S. critical minerals list process, under which barite is listed. Summary. CRS report reviewing US minerals policy, the Energy Act of 2020 definition of a critical mineral, and USGS list development (2022: 50 minerals; 2025: 60) for congressional supply-chain considerations — the policy frame in which barite is classified critical on import reliance. Data. Secondary synthesis of USGS lists/methodology, statute and production/import-reliance data; no new primary dataset.
  • Study on the Critical Raw Materials for the EU 2023 — Final Report · European Commission (2023) The EU's formal CRM criticality assessment underlying its 2023 list, which includes baryte. Summary. The EU's fifth technical criticality assessment, feeding the Critical Raw Materials Act. It screens candidate raw materials against Economic Importance and Supply Risk thresholds and defines the CRM and Strategic Raw Materials lists; baryte is among the 2023 critical raw materials. Data. EU criticality methodology (Economic Importance × Supply Risk) using production, trade, substitution and recycling data.
  • Barytes Association — About us · Barytes Association Industry association representing barytes/barite producers and processors. Summary. Founded October 2000, the Barytes Association now has 25+ member companies — barytes miners and major processors — together representing about half of world barytes production and 80%+ of marketed sales. It liaises with Euromines and IMA-North America on legislative and environmental matters. Data. No primary data — an industry-association description of membership and remit.
  • IMA-Europe — Industrial Minerals Association Europe · IMA-Europe Umbrella body for European industrial-minerals sector associations. Summary. IMA-Europe is an umbrella organisation of 15 mineral-specific associations (calcium carbonates, bentonite, borates, silica, talc and others), representing 500+ companies across 28 countries and engaging EU raw-materials, health, safety and environmental policy. Baryte is represented separately, via the Barytes Association, not as an IMA-Europe commodity group. Data. No primary data — sector-association description.

CEPII database: BACI provides reconciled bilateral trade flows for ~200 countries across ~5,000 HS6 products, 1995–2024, derived from UN Comtrade by removing CIF costs and reliability-weighting reporter discrepancies. Provenance for the salient statistics used across the chain. Data. USGS annual mineral-commodity statistics (production, imports, reserves, use) across all commodities.
