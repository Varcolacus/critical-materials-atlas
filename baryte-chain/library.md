# Baryte / drilling-mud chain — research library

You can't drill an oil or gas well without this invisible mineral: baryte (barium sulfate) weights drilling mud so wells don't blow out, is mined diversely across India, China and Morocco, and has no cheap substitute — essential but not concentrated, the "diffuse" case. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Barite](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-barite.pdf) — USGS (2026). Annual two-page synopsis: production, reserves, import sources, salient statistics.
- [BGS World Mineral Statistics — barytes](https://www2.bgs.ac.uk/mineralsuk/statistics/worldStatistics.html) — British Geological Survey (2024). World mine production data series used for the chain's history chart.
- [American Petroleum Institute — drilling-fluid (mud) specifications](https://www.api.org/) — API (2024). Industry specifications governing drilling-grade barite density/purity.
- [CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII (2026). Harmonized bilateral trade database used for the chain's trade panel (HS 251110/251120).

## Government & agency reports
- [Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/mcs2026) — USGS (2026). Full-volume MCS covering 90+ commodities, published Feb 2026; barite two-pager is one chapter.
- [Barite Statistics and Information](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/barite-statistics-and-information) — USGS National Minerals Information Center. Landing page for historical barite production/price/trade statistics and past MCS editions.
- [Barite — a case study of import reliance on an essential material for oil and gas exploration and development drilling (SIR 2014-5230)](https://pubs.usgs.gov/sir/2014/5230/pdf/sir2014-5230.pdf) — USGS Scientific Investigations Report (2014). Detailed case study: barite used as weighting agent in ~95% of US wells drilled, ~90% of US barite imports go to oil & gas, China/India/Morocco/Mexico import shares, US mine-production decline since 1980.
- [Critical Mineral Resources: National Policy and Critical Minerals List (R47982)](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R47982) — Congressional Research Service (2024). US critical-minerals-list policy context in which barite is classified as critical due to import reliance.
- [Study on the Critical Raw Materials for the EU 2023 — Final Report](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/publications/study-critical-raw-materials-eu-2023-final-report_en) — European Commission (2023). Underlying study for the EU's 2023 list of 34 critical raw materials, which includes baryte.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [World's barite resources as critical raw material](https://mst.misis.ru/jour/article/view/572) — Boyarko, Mining Science and Technology (Russia) (2023). Academic treatment of global barite resources framed through the critical-raw-material lens.
- [Barite Deposits of Türkiye: A Review](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-163X/15/7/692) — MDPI Minerals (2025). Review of Turkish barite deposit genesis (magmatism-associated vs. passive-margin-hosted).
- [The Ballynoe Stratiform Barite Deposit, Silvermines, County Tipperary, Ireland](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-163X/14/5/498) — MDPI Minerals (2024). Case study of an Irish-type exhalative barite deposit and its formation mechanism.
- [Barite Replacement as a Key Factor in the Genesis of Sediment-Hosted Zn-Pb±Ba and Barite-Sulfide Deposits (Iran)](https://www.mdpi.com/2075-163X/14/7/671) — MDPI Minerals (2024). Ore-fluid and isotope study of barite genesis in Iran's 350+ sediment-hosted Zn-Pb-Ba deposits.
- [Origin and occurrence of gem-quality, skarn-hosted barite from Jebel Ouichane near Nador, Morocco](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC8119950/) — PMC (open access). Deposit-genesis study specific to Morocco, one of the chain's three leading producers.
- [Effect of Different Weighting Agents on Drilling Fluids and Filter Cake Properties in Sandstone Formations](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acsomega.1c02129) — ACS Omega. Compares barite against alternative weighting agents; barite retains best rheological/filter-cake performance.
- [A Combined Barite–Ilmenite Weighting Material to Prevent Barite Sag in Water-Based Drilling Fluid](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6631356/) — PMC (open access). Technical study on blending barite with ilmenite to solve the "barite sag" settling problem.
- [Delaminated iron ore (hematite-barite) as alternative weighting agent to barite in petroleum drilling fluids](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S209044792030054X) — ScienceDirect. Investigates hematite-barite blends as a substitution route, underscoring how hard true substitution is.
