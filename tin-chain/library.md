# Tin / solder chain — research library

About half of world tin demand is solder, the joint in every circuit board — and supply is concentrated in China and Indonesia, with a conflict-linked (3TG) and politically fragile swing feed from Myanmar's Wa State and the DR Congo. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Tin](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-tin.pdf) — USGS (2026). Two-page global mine/refine production, reserves and trends synopsis for tin.
- [International Tin Association — tin use survey](https://www.internationaltin.org/tin-use-survey/) — ITA (2025). Annual end-use survey; source for the "~half is solder" figure.
- [BGS World Mineral Statistics — tin mine production](https://www2.bgs.ac.uk/mineralsuk/statistics/worldStatistics.html) — British Geological Survey (2024). Country-level mine production time series.
- [CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. Harmonised bilateral trade database used for the chain's trade layer.

## Government & agency reports
- [Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 (full volume)](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026.pdf) — USGS (2026). Full report covering all ~90 mineral commodities, including tin, with production/reserves tables.
- [Tin Statistics and Information](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/tin-statistics-and-information) — USGS National Minerals Information Center. Landing page for historical tin statistics, yearbooks and commodity summaries.
- [RMIS — More on Critical Raw Materials](https://rmis.jrc.ec.europa.eu/eu-critical-raw-materials) — EU Joint Research Centre. Portal for the EU's Critical Raw Materials criticality assessments and methodology (tin has been assessed in past CRM cycles).
- [Study on the critical raw materials for the EU 2023 — Final report](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/371695621_Study_on_the_critical_raw_materials_for_the_EU_2023_-_Final_report) — European Commission / DG GROW (2023). The EU's tri-annual criticality study underlying the CRM list.
- [DG ENTR — Report on Critical Raw Materials for the EU](https://rmis.jrc.ec.europa.eu/uploads/crm-report-on-critical-raw-materials_en.pdf) — European Commission (DG Enterprise & Industry). Earlier foundational EU CRM criticality report.
- [JRC identifies key opportunities for critical raw material recovery](https://joint-research-centre.ec.europa.eu/jrc-news-and-updates/jrc-identifies-key-opportunities-critical-raw-material-recovery-2026-05-29_en) — EU Joint Research Centre (2026). Circular-economy recovery potential for CRMs from battery, vehicle and e-waste streams.
- [USGS Fact Sheet 2015-3022 (3TG conflict minerals processing facilities)](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/fs20153022) — USGS. USGS work identifying tantalum/tin/tungsten/gold processing facilities in response to conflict-minerals legislation.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [International Supply Chain of Tin Resources in China: Evaluation and Countermeasures](https://www.engineering.org.cn/sscae/EN/10.15302/J-SSCAE-2025.08.013) — Strategic Study of CAE (2025). Tin-specific paper evaluating supply-chain security across resource supply, trade circulation and recycling dimensions.
- [The problem of conflict minerals: A review of current approaches and a web 3.0 inspired road ahead](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0301420722005074) — ScienceDirect (Resources Policy) (2022). Review of due-diligence approaches to 3TG conflict minerals including tin.
- [The security of critical mineral supply chains](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s13563-022-00340-4) — Mineral Economics, Springer Nature (2022). Framework for assessing supply security across critical minerals.
- [Critical minerals and industrial policy: a network-based approach to supply chain risk](https://academic.oup.com/oxrep/article/42/1/193/8750860) — Oxford Review of Economic Policy. Network index of criticality built from trade and production-network data.
- [Challenges in Balancing Sustainability, Competitiveness and Geopolitics in Critical Mineral Supply Chains](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/bse.70213) — Maltais, Business Strategy and the Environment (2026). Geopolitical/sustainability trade-offs across critical mineral chains.
- [Resilience of critical transition minerals supply chain in the context of strategic rivalry](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/02646811.2025.2495920) — Taylor & Francis (2025). Policy/regulatory implications of supply-chain resilience under great-power rivalry.
- [Critical minerals lists for low-carbon transitions: Reviewing their structure, objectives, and limitations](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2214629625003330) — ScienceDirect (2025). Comparative review of how criticality lists (which include tin) are constructed.
- [Critical Minerals in an Age of Geopolitical Rivalry: Stockpiling, Refining Constraints, and the Limits of Friend-Shoring](https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.5860082) — Saadaoui, Smyth, Vespignani, Wang, SSRN working paper. Refining-concentration risk (China) relevant to tin's smelting bottleneck.

## Databases & data sources
- [USGS National Minerals Information Center — Tin Statistics and Information](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/tin-statistics-and-information) — USGS. Long-run US and world tin production/consumption/trade statistics.
- [BGS World Mineral Statistics](https://www2.bgs.ac.uk/mineralsuk/statistics/worldStatistics.html) — British Geological Survey. Free searchable global mineral production database, including tin.
- [CEPII BACI](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. Harmonised, cleaned bilateral trade database derived from UN Comtrade (tin ores 2609, unwrought tin 800110 used in this chain).
- [RMIS — Raw Materials Information System](https://rmis.jrc.ec.europa.eu/eu-critical-raw-materials) — EU JRC. EU-side raw-materials data and criticality-assessment portal.

## Industry & trade associations
- [International Tin Association (ITA)](https://www.internationaltin.org/) — ITA. Leading tin industry body (St Albans, UK); publishes the Tin Desk market intelligence, the annual use survey, and strategic reports (e.g. "TIN2030").
- [ITSCI — Tin Supply Chain Monitoring Initiative](https://www.itsci.org/) — ITSCI. Traceability and due-diligence program for tin, tantalum and tungsten from conflict-affected central Africa (DRC, Rwanda, Burundi, Uganda), aligned with OECD due-diligence guidance.
