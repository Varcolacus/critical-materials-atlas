# Lead chain — research library

The most-recycled metal runs in a loop, not a chokepoint: ~60% of lead supply is recycled, overwhelmingly from lead-acid batteries, in a loop that closes locally near demand — the real risk is toxicity from informal recycling, not scarcity. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Lead](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-lead.pdf) — USGS (2026). Latest production/recycling/use data.
- [International Lead and Zinc Study Group — supply & use](https://www.ilzsg.org/) — ILZSG (2025). Intergovernmental statistics body for lead and zinc.
- [WHO — lead poisoning and informal battery recycling](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/lead-poisoning-and-health) — WHO (2023). Health-risk fact sheet.
- [CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. Trade data underlying the atlas.

## Government & agency reports
- [Lead Statistics and Information](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/lead-statistics-and-information) — USGS National Minerals Information Center. Portal to historical lead data.
- [2026 USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries Web Tool](https://www.usgs.gov/tools/2026-usgs-mineral-commodity-summaries-web-tool) — USGS. Interactive access to the 2026 MCS dataset.
- [Critical Mineral Resources: National Policy and Critical Minerals List](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R47982) — Congressional Research Service. US policy context for critical/non-critical mineral classification (lead is notably NOT on the US critical list, reinforcing the chain's "no chokepoint" argument).

## Academic papers & reviews
- [A critical review on secondary lead recycling technology and its prospect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1364032116002811) — Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews (ScienceDirect; also on [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/299495881_A_critical_review_on_secondary_lead_recycling_technology_and_its_prospect)). Technology review of secondary lead production.
- [Path to the sustainable development of China's secondary lead industry: An overview of the current status of waste lead-acid battery recycling](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0195925523003554) — ScienceDirect (Resources, Conservation & Recycling family). China-focused secondary-lead industry analysis.
- [Sustainability evaluation of secondary lead production from spent lead acid batteries recycling](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/329060328_Sustainability_evaluation_of_secondary_lead_production_from_spent_lead_acid_batteries_recycling) — ResearchGate. Sustainability/LCA-style evaluation.
- [Mass lead intoxication from informal used lead-acid battery recycling in Dakar, Senegal](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20019903/) — PubMed (full text at [PMC2790507](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC2790507/)). Landmark case study on informal-recycling mass poisoning.
- [Blood Lead Levels in Children Living Near an Informal Lead Battery Recycling Workshop in Patna, Bihar](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC7058140/) — PMC/NCBI. India case study.
- [Child Exposure to Lead in the Vicinities of Informal Used Lead-Acid Battery Recycling Operations in Nairobi Slums, Kenya](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC6221501/) — PMC/NCBI. Kenya case study.
- [Lead Exposures from Car Batteries — A Global Problem](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC2799476/) — PMC/NCBI. Global-scope review of ULAB-driven lead exposure.
