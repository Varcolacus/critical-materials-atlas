# Electricity-grid chain — research library

The ore is fine — the transformer is the bottleneck: grids run on abundant copper, aluminium and steel, but the real constraint is equipment lead time — large power transformers can take up to four years to procure. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [IEA, Electricity Grids and Secure Energy Transitions](https://www.iea.org/reports/electricity-grids-and-secure-energy-transitions/executive-summary) — IEA (2023). The report that established the "80 million km by 2040" framing.
- [IEA, Building the Future Transmission Grid](https://www.iea.org/reports/building-the-future-transmission-grid) — IEA (2025). Supply-chain and lead-time survey of grid equipment.
- [US DOE, Large Power Transformer Resilience Report](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2024-10/EXEC-2022-001242%20-%20Large%20Power%20Transformer%20Resilience%20Report%20signed%20by%20Secretary%20Granholm%20on%207-10-24.pdf) — US DOE (2024). Transformer supply-chain resilience assessment.
- [US DOE, Advanced Conductor Report](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2024-08/Advanced%20Conductor%20Report%20December%202023.pdf) — US DOE (2023). Conductor technology and capacity report.
- [CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. Trade data underlying the atlas.

## Government & agency reports
- [Executive summary — Building the Future Transmission Grid](https://www.iea.org/reports/building-the-future-transmission-grid/executive-summary) — IEA (2025). Procurement now 2–3 years for cables, up to 4 years for large power transformers — twice 2021 levels.
- [Rising component prices and supply chain pressures are hindering the development of transmission grid infrastructure](https://www.iea.org/news/rising-component-prices-and-supply-chain-pressures-are-hindering-the-development-of-transmission-grid-infrastructure) — IEA news release. Cable costs nearly doubled since 2019; transformer prices up ~75%.
- [Grids — Electricity 2026](https://www.iea.org/reports/electricity-2026/grids) — IEA (2026). Latest annual grid-investment and queue assessment (over 2,500 GW stalled worldwide).
- [Lack of ambition and attention risks making electricity grids the weak link in clean energy transitions](https://www.iea.org/news/lack-of-ambition-and-attention-risks-making-electricity-grids-the-weak-link-in-clean-energy-transitions) — IEA news release. Companion release to the 80-million-km grid report.
- [Electricity Grids and Secure Energy Transitions (OECD/IEA PDF)](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2023/10/electricity-grids-and-secure-energy-transitions_9559dcb0/455dd4fb-en.pdf) — OECD/IEA (2023). Full report text.
- [Queued Up: Characteristics of Power Plants Seeking Transmission Interconnection](https://emp.lbl.gov/queues) — Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory. US interconnection-queue dataset and annual report.
- [Grid Connection Barriers To New-Build Power Plants In the United States](https://emp.lbl.gov/news/grid-connection-barriers-new-build-power-plants-united-states) — LBNL Energy Markets & Planning. Analysis of US interconnection delay drivers.
