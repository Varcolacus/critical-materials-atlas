# Research notes

## Narrative correction

The grid is not one commodity story. Bulk metal availability, specialised electrical steel, conductor design, cable plants, transformer factories, project permitting and investment can constrain different stages. A country can mine or refine metal without being able to manufacture the relevant equipment.

## Interpretive rules

- Treat the USGS histories as economy-wide production context, never as grid demand.
- Treat the IEA investment values as three published anchors with different statuses.
- Preserve “add or refurbish”: it is not a new-line-only target.
- Keep overhead conductor design separate from underground and submarine cable design.
- Treat DOE transformer detail as US evidence unless the IEA supplies the global statement.
- Do not translate trade shares into factory capacity or dependency scores.

## Customs baskets

- GOES: HS 722511 + 722611.
- Bare aluminium stranded conductors: HS 761410 + 761490.
- Liquid-dielectric transformers: HS 850421 + 850422 + 850423.
- Insulated conductors above 1 kV: HS 854460.

Every basket has an explicit scope warning in the output JSON and page.
