# Research notes and boundaries

The central finding is that useful heat is a system outcome. A machine’s materials matter, but deployment and performance also require compatible refrigerant rules, standards, buildings, grids, installers, commissioning and operating economics.

- The EHPA 2005–2022 line uses the EU-21 scope printed in its 2023 report. Later 2024 and 2025 snapshots cover 19 and 21 countries respectively and are not appended to that series.
- IEA global sales combine region-specific definitions. Reversible air conditioners used for heating are counted differently across markets.
- Factory capacity is reported in GW/year of thermal output. It cannot be compared directly with unit sales or installed stock.
- More than 70% domestic sourcing of final units can coexist with concentrated upstream rotary-compressor production.
- HS 841861 explicitly excludes machines of HS 8415. HS 841581 captures reversible air-conditioning equipment, while compressor and heat-exchanger headings are mixed-use.
- Copper, primary aluminium, rare-earth and fluorspar histories cover their whole economies. They do not measure heat-pump demand, component-grade supply, refrigerant output or recycling.
- Refrigerant transitions change safety classification, pressure, product design, standards, tools and technician requirements; they are not simple material substitutions.
