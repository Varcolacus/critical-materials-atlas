The machine is only one part of delivered heat

A heat pump combines a compressor, refrigerant circuit, heat exchangers, motor, controls and installation system. Its real performance then depends on climate, delivery temperature, building demand, commissioning, electricity prices and skilled maintenance.

annual sales ≠ installed stock ≠ factory GW ≠ useful heat ≠ recorded trade

The correction. Heat-pump deployment is not governed by critical minerals alone. Compressor supply, refrigerant rules, product standards, installers, building compatibility and the electricity-to-fuel price ratio can become the binding constraints.

“Heat pump” covers several physical systems

The accounting problem. Reversible air conditioners used for primary heating are heat pumps, but regions count them differently. Unit totals can therefore change when statistical coverage changes—even if the physical market does not.

The component chain is more revealing than a mineral list

Final assembly is local; a key upstream component is concentrated

Manufacturing capacity by region, 2024

Shares of about 145 GW/year nameplate manufacturing capacity. Thermal-output capacity is not unit production.

Two geographies coexist. The IEA finds more than 70% of building heat pumps sold in every major market were sourced domestically in 2024, while rotary compressors were mostly produced in China. Final-unit origin does not reveal component origin.

The refrigerant transition changes the product

Refrigerants are not drop-in commodities. Global-warming potential, flammability, pressure, efficiency, charge size, standards and technician competence determine which fluid a product and installation can safely use.

Trade categories split the physical market

BACI provides 2002–2024 monetary context. Dedicated heat pumps outside the air-conditioning heading and reversible air-conditioning heat pumps must be viewed separately; compressors and heat exchangers serve many other applications.

Do not infer installation market share from customs exports. Domestic production is important, product codes divide heat pumps by customs architecture, and the installed system contains local labour and balance-of-plant that never crosses a border with the unit.
