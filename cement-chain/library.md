# Cement chain — research library

The most-used material on Earth has no chokepoint — limestone and kilns are everywhere and cement is too cheap and heavy to ship far, so the only binding constraint is carbon: cement alone is about 8% of global CO2. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Cement](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-cement.pdf) — USGS (2026). Annual US/world production and reserves.
- [IEA, Cement — tracking & net-zero roadmap](https://www.iea.org/energy-system/industry/cement) — IEA (2023). Sector tracking page: emissions, technology pathways.
- [Global Cement and Concrete Association — Concrete Future roadmap](https://gccassociation.org/concretefuture/) — GCCA (2024). Industry's own 2050 net-zero roadmap.
- [CEPII BACI V202601](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII, based on UN Comtrade (2026). Bilateral trade database used for the chain's trade panel.

## Government & agency reports
- [Cement Statistics and Information](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/cement-statistics-and-information) — USGS National Minerals Information Center. World cement supply/demand data, linked to full Mineral Commodity Summaries (1996–2026) and Minerals Yearbook archives.
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2022 — Cement](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2022/mcs2022-cement.pdf) — USGS (2022). A specific prior-year annual summary for cross-year comparison.
- [Background Facts and Issues Concerning Cement and Cement Data (OF-2005-1152)](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/cement-statistics-and-information) — USGS Open-File Report 2005-1152 (linked from the statistics page above). Detailed methodological background on how USGS compiles cement data.
- [Trends in global CO2 emissions: 2014 Report](http://www.pbl.nl/sites/default/files/cms/publicaties/PBL_2014_Trends_in_global_CO2_emisions_2014_1490_0.pdf) — PBL Netherlands Environmental Assessment Agency & European Commission Joint Research Centre (2014). Government/EU-agency emissions accounting that sizes cement's CO2 share.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [The sponge effect and carbon emission mitigation potentials of the global cement cycle](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-17583-w) — Cao et al., *Nature Communications* (2020). Peer-reviewed global material-flow and carbon-cycle analysis of cement.

## Databases & data sources
- [Cement Statistics and Information](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/cement-statistics-and-information) — USGS. Primary production/trade data portal (monthly Mineral Industry Surveys + annual summaries, 1965–2026).

## Industry & trade associations
- [Global Cement and Concrete Association — Concrete Future](https://gccassociation.org/concretefuture/) — GCCA (already core). Represents ~80% of the world's concrete industry outside China; full roadmap PDF downloads (English/Spanish/Mandarin) linked from the page.

## Analysis, think-tanks & reporting
- [Making Concrete Change: Innovation in Low-carbon Cement and Concrete](https://reader.chathamhouse.org/making-concrete-change-innovation-low-carbon-cement-and-concrete) — Chatham House (2018). Widely-cited think-tank report on cement decarbonization pathways.
