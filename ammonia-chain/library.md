# Ammonia / nitrogen-fertilizer chain — research library

Half the world eats because we pull nitrogen from the air with natural gas — and the chain's real chokepoint is the gas price, not a mineral. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Nitrogen (Fixed)–Ammonia](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-nitrogen.pdf) — USGS (2026). Two-page commodity synopsis: production, reserves, trade, events/trends for nitrogen/ammonia.
- [IEA, Ammonia Technology Roadmap](https://www.iea.org/reports/ammonia-technology-roadmap) — IEA (2021). The core technical roadmap for decarbonising Haber-Bosch (grey/blue/green/turquoise ammonia).
- [FAO, World fertilizer trends and outlook](https://www.fao.org/documents/card/en/c/cc0088en) — FAO (2022). Global fertilizer supply/demand/trade outlook including nitrogen.
- [CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. Bilateral trade database used for the chain's ammonia (281410) and urea (310210) trade figures.

## Government & agency reports
- [USGS Nitrogen Statistics and Information](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/nitrogen-statistics-and-information) — USGS, National Minerals Information Center. Ongoing nitrogen/ammonia production and trade statistics hub.
- [Mineral Commodity Summaries](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/mineral-commodity-summaries) — USGS. Annual full-report landing page (all commodities, including nitrogen), earliest comprehensive source of prior-year global production data.
- [Understanding the resilience of fertiliser markets to shocks](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2024/06/understanding-the-resilience-of-fertiliser-markets-to-shocks_c8c4806f/43664170-en.pdf) — OECD (2024). Analysis of how fertilizer markets absorbed the 2022 gas-price and Russia/Belarus export shocks.
- [Ensuring availability and affordability of fertilisers — COM(2022) 590](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX%3A52022DC0590) — European Commission (2022). EU policy response to the 2022 gas-driven ammonia plant curtailments.
- [Global Fertilizer Market Challenged by Russia's Invasion of Ukraine](https://www.ers.usda.gov/amber-waves/2023/september/global-fertilizer-market-challenged-by-russia-s-invasion-of-ukraine) — USDA Economic Research Service (2023). US government analysis of the war's effect on global fertilizer prices and trade.
- [Food Outlook — Fertilizer market update, June 2025](https://openknowledge.fao.org/server/api/core/bitstreams/0c79f9df-b2eb-43cb-9b84-977efea5b197/content) — FAO (2025). Latest FAO fertilizer market special feature, post-2022-shock recovery data.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [Integrated techno-economic and greenhouse-gas assessment of the global ammonia supply chain](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlepdf/2026/ee/d5ee05571g) — Energy & Environmental Science (2025/26). Compares grey/blue/green/pink/yellow ammonia routes and costs across 63 countries.
- [Unraveling the effect of variable natural gas feedstock on an industrial ammonia process](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0098135422002885) — Computers & Chemical Engineering / ScienceDirect (2022). How natural-gas composition variability affects industrial ammonia synthesis.
- [Green ammonia supply chain and associated market structure: an analysis based on transaction cost economics](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.19498) — arXiv (2023). Economic-structure analysis of the emerging green-ammonia supply chain.
- [Blue Ammonia and the Supply Chain: Pioneering Sustainability Assessment for a Greener Future](https://doi.org/10.3390/en18051137) — Energies / MDPI (2025). Life-cycle sustainability assessment of blue ammonia (natural gas + carbon capture) from feedstock to shipping.
- [Life cycle assessment of ammonia synthesis in China](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11367-021-02010-z) — International Journal of Life Cycle Assessment / Springer (2021). Cradle-to-gate LCA of coal- vs gas-based ammonia routes.
- [The nexus of geopolitics, decarbonization, and food security gives rise to distinct challenges across fertilizer supply chains](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2590332224006031) — ScienceDirect (2024). Academic framing of fertilizer-chain concentration risk across geopolitics, climate policy and food security.
- [Ammonia Production from Clean Hydrogen and the Implications for Global Natural Gas Demand](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/15/2/1623) — Sustainability / MDPI (2023). Models how far electrolytic-hydrogen ammonia could displace gas-based Haber-Bosch demand.

## Gaps & good next searches
- No BGS (British Geological Survey) equivalent was found for nitrogen/ammonia — nitrogen has no ore, so BGS's mineral-statistics remit largely doesn't cover it; worth checking BGS's occasional gas/energy-criticality notes instead.
- No EU JRC Critical Raw Materials entry — nitrogen/ammonia is not on the EU CRM list (energy commodity, not a mineral), so JRC coverage would have to come via its gas/energy-security work rather than its CRM Foundation series.
- Coal-based ammonia in China (the main non-gas route) is thin here beyond one LCA paper; search "China coal-to-ammonia capacity" and NDRC/CNCIA sources for a fuller picture of the non-gas half of global production.
- Ammonia-as-shipping-fuel and hydrogen-carrier demand growth is only lightly covered; search "IMO ammonia bunker fuel" and "ammonia hydrogen carrier shipping" for the demand-side branch flagged in the chain's cards.
- Benchmark Mineral Intelligence / Fastmarkets-style paid market pricing services were not surfaced by open search — their public commentary (if any) would need a targeted site search.
