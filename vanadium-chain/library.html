Research Library · Vanadium

Vanadium chain

A curated reference library of public sources for deeper study — gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source and carries an abstract (or factual summary) plus the data it rests on. A research-support layer of the Critical Materials Atlas.

A pinch of vanadium micro-alloys steel — a tiny dose for a big strength gain — and most of it is recovered as a by-product of steel itself, which is why China's mine share rose from ~37% to ~64%. Its second act is redox-flow batteries for long-duration grid storage. The chokepoint rides on steel, not a unique deposit.

Ordered to follow the chain: ① Source (vanadium-bearing magnetite / steel slag — a by-product, China ~64%) → ② Ferro-vanadium (~90% of vanadium micro-alloys steel) → ③ Vanadium-redox-flow batteries (long-duration grid storage) — then the cross-cutting criticality frameworks, market analysis and trade data.

The 2026 PDF returned HTTP 403 on two passes — not retrievable; scope taken from the USGS series and the atlas's existing read. Data.   • BGS — World Mineral Statistics (vanadium) · British Geological Survey (2024) Historical world mine-production series by country and commodity, including vanadium. Scope. The statistics landing page returned HTTP 404 this pass; the series is already extracted into the atlas's own data. Data. BGS compilation of national production returns and official statistics, published annually.
  • USGS National Minerals Information Center — Vanadium Statistics and Information · USGS Hub page indexing every USGS vanadium publication — monthly surveys, annual summaries, yearbooks and historical statistics. Data. Index/portal to the USGS vanadium statistical series — no standalone dataset of its own.

② Ferro-vanadium — the steel micro-alloy 1

Where the metal becomes the alloy: a fraction-of-a-percent dose greatly raises steel strength — rebar, pipe, tools.
  • Vanitec · Vanitec The global vanadium industry association; technical library on vanadium in HSLA steel and flow batteries. Summary. Vanitec, the self-described only global vanadium organisation, promotes vanadium use and stewardship. Its technical focus is high-strength low-alloy (HSLA) steel — construction, pipelines, bridges, anti-seismic rebar — and vanadium flow batteries; it runs a technical library and V-Strength / V-Technology newsletters. Data. Industry-association technical library and commentary — no independent statistical dataset.

The retrieved page resolved to Electricity-2026 landing content; the substantive storage / flow-battery text was not in it. Data.   • Review of vanadium and its redox flow batteries for renewable energy storage · ICE Energy (2024) Review of vanadium and its redox flow batteries for renewable-energy storage. Abstract. Review of vanadium's role in renewable-energy storage via redox flow batteries: it surveys RFB advantages over alternatives for large-scale stationary storage and argues vanadium systems are positioned to lead a significant share of the stationary-storage market in the coming decades. Data. Literature review of VRFB technology and markets — synthesis, no primary dataset.
