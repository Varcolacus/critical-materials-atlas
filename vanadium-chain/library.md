# Vanadium chain — research library

About 90% of vanadium micro-alloys steel (a small dose, a big strength gain), while China's mine share rose from ~37% to ~64% as it recovers vanadium as a by-product of its own steel industry — and vanadium-redox-flow batteries are its emerging second act in long-duration grid storage. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

Note on method: the WebSearch tool was unavailable for this session (budget exhausted), so this library was built via WebFetch — direct verification of known agency/industry URLs, plus Google Scholar and Bing fetched directly — rather than the usual WebSearch sweep. A few categories are thinner than usual as a result (see Gaps below).

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Vanadium](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-vanadium.pdf) — USGS (2026). Annual mine-production and reserves data by country, the atlas's primary source line.
- [IEA, Grid-scale storage & long-duration technologies](https://www.iea.org/energy-system/electricity/grid-scale-storage) — IEA (2024). Long-duration storage technology landscape, including flow batteries.
- [BGS, World Mineral Statistics — vanadium](https://www2.bgs.ac.uk/mineralsuk/statistics/worldStatistics.html) — British Geological Survey (2024). Historical mine-production series used for the 2000→2024 China/South Africa/Russia chart.
- [CEPII BACI, V202601 (based on UN Comtrade)](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII (2026). Harmonised bilateral trade dataset behind the ferro-vanadium/oxide trade figures.

## Government & agency reports
- [USGS National Minerals Information Center — Vanadium Statistics and Information](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/vanadium-statistics-and-information) — USGS. Hub page for monthly Mineral Industry Surveys, annual Mineral Commodity Summaries/Minerals Yearbooks back to 1994, and critical-minerals/recycling special publications on vanadium.
- [IEA, Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2024](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2024) — IEA (2024). Flagship annual critical-minerals demand/supply outlook; vanadium sits among the 37 minerals in its data explorer even though the headline chapters focus on six others.
- [EU JRC Raw Materials Information System (RMIS)](https://rmis.jrc.ec.europa.eu/) — European Commission Joint Research Centre. Portal for EU raw-material and country profiles, criticality and security-of-supply analysis; a vanadium-specific profile page exists on the site but its direct URL could not be located in this pass (see Gaps).

## Academic papers & reviews
- [A Fuzzy AHP Decision-Support Framework for Stage-Specific Mineral Criticality Assessment: Evidence from Australian Vanadium](https://www.researchsquare.com/article/rs-10323399/latest) — Mammadli, Barakos, Chang, Mehraliyeva (2026, preprint). Stage-specific criticality methodology applied directly to a vanadium case study.
- [Review of vanadium and its redox flow batteries for renewable energy storage](https://www.emerald.com/jener/article/177/1/3/1221872) — Odebiyi, Lasisi, Farotimi et al., journal (2024). Review of VRFB chemistry and role in renewable-energy storage.
- [An overview of the design and optimized operation of vanadium redox flow batteries for durations in the range of 4–24 hours](https://www.mdpi.com/2313-0105/9/4/221) — Viswanathan, Crawford, Thomsen et al., Batteries (MDPI) (2023). Engineering review of VRFB design for long-duration storage use cases.
- [Cleaner and efficient vanadium extraction from vanadium slag: optimal adaptation mechanism between calcification roasting and reductive leaching processes](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0959652625019481) — Li, An, Jiang et al., Journal of Cleaner Production (2025). Processing route for recovering vanadium from steel slag — the by-product coupling to China's steel industry that anchors the chain's chokepoint.
- [High efficient vanadium extraction from vanadium slag using an enhanced acid leaching-coprecipitation process](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1383586622018743) — Hu, Zhang, Liu et al., Separation and Purification Technology (2023). Another slag-leaching extraction study, same by-product theme.
- [Review and development of the methodology and data used to produce the UK Criticality Assessment](https://nora.nerc.ac.uk/) — Josso, Lusty, Gunn, Shaw, Singh, Horn et al. (NERC/BGS) (2023). Methodology paper behind the UK's national mineral-criticality assessment, which covers vanadium.
