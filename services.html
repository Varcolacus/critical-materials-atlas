Work with us

Know your exposure before the export control lands.

A single country’s decision — a ban, an outage, a licensing rule — can squeeze a material your whole product line depends on, at a stage the customs ledger can’t even see. We find that stage in your supply chain, tell you who could backfill and who couldn’t, and keep watching what tightens. Independent, public-data, and built on the same tested engine behind this atlas.

What we do

Three ways in, built to run in sequence: understand the exposure, stress it, then keep it monitored.

Start here

Exposure map

We take your critical inputs and trace each one from mine to refinery to the border, and locate the real chokepoint — usually a refining or processing stage one country holds, not the mine everyone watches.

Deliverable: a per-input exposure brief — where you depend on a single country or a single facility, and how concentrated each stage really is.
Then

Supplier stress-test

We model a shock to each chokepoint — an export ban, a plant outage — and ask the harder question: could the rest of the world actually backfill it, and would the spare capacity sit anywhere near you?

Deliverable: a shock-by-shock reallocation test on your inputs, separating “annoying” from “structurally uncoverable.”
Ongoing

Watch service

Supply risk never stops moving. We keep a continuous watch on your inputs — new export controls, shifting concentration, the reconciled nowcast — and flag what changed before the official data lands.

Deliverable: a recurring briefing + alerts on the materials that matter to you.

Why us, not a generalist consultancy

We see what official stats miss

A reconciliation engine rebuilds bilateral trade from both sides of every flow — recovering the refiner-and-re-export reality that a single customs series hides. Most supply-chain reports stop at “who exports.” That’s the wrong end of the chain.

The method is public and tested

Every claim traces to an open script, ten automated guards, and three rounds of adversarial review that caught and published real errors. You can check the work — and so can your board. What you can trust →

Findings end in a decision

We don’t hand you a concentration statistic. We tell you what kind of chokepoint it is — and therefore whether it yields to a new supplier, to recycling, or only to a stockpile. A finding that doesn’t answer “so what do I do” isn’t finished.

How an engagement works

  1. Scoping call. You name the inputs that keep you up at night; we say plainly what the public data can and can’t settle for each.
  2. Exposure map + stress-test. A fixed-scope study on those inputs, delivered as a brief your procurement and risk teams can act on.
  3. Optional watch service. If the exposure is live, we keep monitoring it — so the next export-control headline isn’t the first you hear of it.
