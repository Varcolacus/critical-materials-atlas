# Defence evidence ledger

- **Gallium / germanium** — USGS MCS 2025: China ~99% refined gallium, ~60% refined
  germanium; both under Chinese export licensing from 2023.
- **Antimony** — USGS MCS 2025: China ~48% of world mined antimony (world ~83 kt,
  China ~40 kt in 2024); cited in 200+ munition types. Global Trade Alert / Fastmarkets:
  Sept-2024 export controls → shipments ~−97%, prices ~+200%.
- **Tungsten** — ~80% China mine share (USGS); armour-piercing and carbide end-use.
- **Rare-earth magnets** — ~90% China downstream (EU CRM 2023); precision-guidance actuators.
- **Export-control timeline** — CSIS (Dec 2024): Ga/Ge (2023) → antimony + superhard
  (Aug–Sep 2024) → outright US ban on Ga/Ge/Sb/superhard (Dec 2024), later eased.
- **Response** — USGS net-import reliance: US has no primary antimony or gallium
  production; strategic stockpiles limited.
- **Trade context** — CEPII BACI V202601, HS 811292 / 811010 / 810194 / 850511 / 810320,
  2002–2024. Context only; military end-use and captive procurement are outside it.
