# Defence / military-materials chain — research library

The West's weapons run on materials a rival controls: radar, night vision, precision-guided munitions and the shells themselves depend on a short list of materials — gallium, germanium, antimony, tungsten, rare-earth magnets — whose mining and refining is concentrated in China, and which China has already export-controlled or banned outright to the United States (2023–2024). A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 — Gallium](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025-gallium.pdf) — USGS (2025). Official US production/reserves/trade data for gallium, source of the ~99% refined-China share.
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 — Germanium](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025-germanium.pdf) — USGS (2025). Official germanium supply data underlying the IR-optics/night-vision material link.
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 — Antimony](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025-antimony.pdf) — USGS (2025). Official antimony mine-production data, source of the 48% China mine-share figure.
- [CSIS, China Imposes Its Most Stringent Critical Minerals Export Restrictions Yet](https://www.csis.org/analysis/china-imposes-its-most-stringent-critical-minerals-export-restrictions-yet-amidst) — CSIS (2024). Analysis of the December 2024 gallium/germanium/antimony export ban to the US.
- [Global Trade Alert, China export-control measures for antimony](https://globaltradealert.org/state-act/88204-china-government-announces-export-control-measures-for-antimony-and-related-items) — Global Trade Alert (2024). Primary policy-tracking record of the August 2024 antimony export controls.
- [Fastmarkets, Uncertainty lingers over antimony exports following US-China truce](https://www.fastmarkets.com/insights/uncertainty-lingers-over-antimony-exports-following-us-china-truce/) — Fastmarkets (2025). Market reporting on antimony price/volume response and the truce.
- [European Commission, Study on the Critical Raw Materials for the EU 2023](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/raw-materials/areas-specific-interest/critical-raw-materials_en) — European Commission (2023). EU's own critical-materials assessment, cross-checked for the chain's material list.
- [CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. Harmonised bilateral trade database used for the chain's trade table.

## Government & agency reports
- [CRS R47833, Emergency Access to Strategic and Critical Materials: The National Defense Stockpile](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R47833) — Congressional Research Service. Background on the legal authority and mechanics of the US National Defense Stockpile.
- [GAO-24-106959, National Defense Stockpile: Actions Needed to Improve DOD's Efforts to Prepare for Emergencies](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-24-106959) — US GAO (2024). Audit finding gaps in DOD's stockpile preparedness for supply disruptions.
- [National Defense Magazine, GCI: Critical Mineral Stockpiling Presents Opportunities](https://www.nationaldefensemagazine.org/articles/2026/3/6/gci-critical-mineral-stockpiling-presents-opportunities) — National Defense Magazine (2026). Coverage of the Pentagon's 2025 intent to procure up to $1B in stockpile materials (scandium, tungsten, graphite, samarium, dysprosium, terbium).
- [National Defense Magazine, Defense Logistics Agency Considers Dipping into Strategic Mineral Stockpiles](https://www.nationaldefensemagazine.org/articles/2025/6/20/defense-logistics-agency-considers-dipping-into-strategic-mineral-stockpiles) — National Defense Magazine (2025). Reporting on DLA stockpile-drawdown deliberations amid supply pressure.
- [Inside Government Contracts, Federal Push for Critical Minerals Stockpiling: 2025 in Review and Outlook for 2026](https://www.insidegovernmentcontracts.com/2026/02/federal-push-for-critical-minerals-stockpiling-2025-in-review-and-outlook-for-2026/) — Inside Government Contracts (2026). Year-in-review of US federal critical-minerals stockpiling policy.
- [CSET (Georgetown), Ministry of Commerce Notice 2024 No. 46](https://cset.georgetown.edu/publication/china-rare-earth-export-ban/) — Center for Security and Emerging Technology (2024). Translation/analysis of China's formal notice tightening dual-use export controls to the US.

## Analysis, think-tanks & reporting
- [CSIS, Rare Earth Export Restrictions One Year Later](https://www.csis.org/analysis/rare-earth-export-restrictions-one-year-later) — CSIS. One-year retrospective on China's rare-earth export restrictions and their effect on downstream industries.
- [CSIS, The Consequences of China's New Rare Earths Export Restrictions](https://www.csis.org/analysis/consequences-chinas-new-rare-earths-export-restrictions) — CSIS. Analysis of the April 2025 heavy-rare-earth and magnet export restrictions.
- [CSIS, China's New Rare Earth and Magnet Restrictions Threaten U.S. Defense Supply Chains](https://www.csis.org/analysis/chinas-new-rare-earth-and-magnet-restrictions-threaten-us-defense-supply-chains) — CSIS. Defense-specific framing of the rare-earth/magnet restrictions, incl. F-35 (900+ lb REEs) and submarine (9,200 lb) figures.
- [CSIS, Minerals at War: Strategic Resources and the Foundations of the U.S. Defense Industrial Base](https://www.csis.org/analysis/minerals-war-strategic-resources-and-foundations-us-defense-industrial-base) — CSIS. Broader framing of strategic minerals as the foundation of the US defense industrial base.
- [IEA, With new export controls on critical minerals, supply concentration risks become reality](https://www.iea.org/commentaries/with-new-export-controls-on-critical-minerals-supply-concentration-risks-become-reality) — IEA commentary. IEA's own read on how export controls turn theoretical supply-concentration risk into realised disruption.
- [MINING.COM, Pentagon moves to build $1 billion critical minerals stockpile to counter China](https://www.mining.com/pentagon-moves-to-build-1-billion-critical-minerals-stockpile-to-counter-china-report/) — Mining.com. Trade-press report on the Pentagon's stockpiling drive.
- [Modern War Institute (West Point), Minerals, Magnets, and Military Capability: China's Rare Earth Weaponization Should Be a Wake-Up Call](https://mwi.westpoint.edu/minerals-magnets-and-military-capability-chinas-rare-earth-weaponization-should-be-a-wake-up-call/) — Modern War Institute. Military-academy analysis framing rare-earth export control as demonstrated weaponization.
- [The Diplomat, China's Mineral Export Ban Strikes at the US Defense Industrial Base](https://thediplomat.com/2024/12/chinas-mineral-export-ban-strikes-at-the-us-defense-industrial-base/) — The Diplomat (2024). Regional-affairs framing of the December 2024 ban's direct defense-industrial impact.
