# Defence-chain interpretation notes

## The claim
For defence, a critical-minerals list read at the mine understates the problem: the
same materials are (a) China-dominated upstream and (b) already used as export levers.
The risk is the gap between a long material list and a short Western buffer.

## Claims rejected
- **Per-weapon material shares.** Not attempted — no public bill of materials ties a
  tonnage of antimony or gallium to a specific missile. Material→capability is mapped
  qualitatively and labelled as such.
- **"Trade shows who arms whom."** Rejected. Customs codes miss military end-use and
  captive defence procurement; gallium/germanium share one basket (811292). Trade is
  shown only as availability context.
- **Mixing stages silently.** Shares are labelled by the stage that binds each material
  (refined for Ga/Ge, mine for Sb/W, downstream for magnets); they are not summed.

## Rules reused
Define the transformation first; production/capacity for "who makes it"; policy events +
price/volume response to measure leverage; net-import reliance + stockpile for the buffer;
show gaps rather than invent series.
