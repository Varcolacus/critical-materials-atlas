# IEA Energy Technology Perspectives 2017 — HELD, BUT LICENCE-BLOCKED FOR OUR USE

Files: `ETP2017_buildings_summary.xlsx`, `ETP2017_industry_summary.xlsx`,
`ETP2017_transport_summary.xlsx`. Downloaded free from the IEA, June 2018 vintage.

## READ THIS BEFORE USING ANY OF IT

The download page carries a clause that is decisive for this project, quoted:

> "You will need to enter into a Licence Agreement with the IEA and **pay a fee if you wish to use
> the IEA's data, including scenario data, in any type of modelling for the purpose of creating
> derived data or derived products**, and any services to distribute or display such derived
> products."

Creating derived data and derived products is precisely what the atlas does. Every page is a derived
product of its inputs. So this dataset is **free to download and not free to use the way we would
use it** — modelling with it, or publishing anything computed from it, needs a paid IEA licence
agreement (`compliance@iea.org`).

**Status: held for reference only. Not ingested, not modelled with, nothing derived from it
published.** The files are kept so the decision is inspectable and so nobody re-downloads them to
ask the same question again.

## The wider lesson, worth carrying to every future download

"Free" and "usable" are different tests, and IEA products vary:

| Licence seen so far | Download | Use as our input | Republish raw |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CC BY 4.0** (Critical Minerals Dataset, Energy & AI annex) | yes | yes | yes, with attribution |
| **Terms of Use for Non-CC Material** (Value Added DB, EEI Highlights) | yes | yes | **no** |
| **ETP-style: fee for derived products** (this) | yes | **no, not without a paid agreement** | no |

The third row is the trap: nothing on the download button distinguishes it from the first two. The
clause only appears in the page body. **Check the licence text, not the price, before building
anything on an IEA dataset.**

## Also worth noting
ETP 2017 is a 2018-vintage scenario set, superseded several times over. Even with a licence it would
be a poor input today.
