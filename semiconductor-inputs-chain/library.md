# Semiconductor-inputs chain — research library

Before the chip, the chemicals — advanced photoresist is ~90% Japan, plus process gases, CMP slurries and EUV pellicles: thin, qualified-supplier chokepoints upstream of the fab. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [SEMI — semiconductor materials market](https://www.semi.org/en/products-services/market-data/materials) — SEMI (2024). Market data on wafer-fab materials (photoresist, gases, CMP slurries, wet chemicals).
- [CSIS — Japan–Korea trade dispute (2019 photoresist controls)](https://www.csis.org/analysis/japan-korea-trade-dispute) — CSIS (2019). Analysis of Japan's export curbs on photoresist and two fab chemicals to Korea.
- [IEA — The State of Clean Technology Manufacturing](https://www.iea.org/reports/the-state-of-clean-technology-manufacturing/analysis) — IEA (2023). Manufacturing-capacity concentration relevant to fab inputs and specialty gases.
- [CEPII BACI V202601](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. Bilateral HS6 trade (photographic chemical preparations 370790 as a coarse resist proxy).

## Government & agency reports
- [BIS / US Dept of Commerce — Semiconductor supply-chain assessments](https://www.bis.gov/) — US Dept of Commerce, Bureau of Industry and Security. Government supply-chain risk assessments for semiconductors and their material inputs.
- [European Commission — Critical Raw Materials](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/raw-materials/areas-specific-interest/critical-raw-materials_en) — European Commission (2023). CRM assessment covering several fab-gas and material inputs.
- [The White House — Building Resilient Supply Chains (100-day review, semiconductors)](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/100-day-supply-chain-review-report.pdf) — Executive Office of the President (2021). US review including semiconductor materials and chemicals.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [Effective Availability in Global Semiconductor Trade: Assessing US Supply Chain Risks and Policy Response](https://www.scirp.net/pdf/me_7204130.pdf) — Yu & Yu (2025). Trade-based assessment of semiconductor supply-chain risk and policy.
- [Partners in Chips: Pathways for Korea–Japan Cooperation in Semiconductors](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7164499) — Choi, Kyung & Kim, SSRN (2026). Directly relevant to the photoresist/fab-chemical dependency between Japan and Korea.
- [Changes in Relations between Japan and Korea in the IT Industry](https://catalog.lib.kyushu-u.ac.jp/opac_download_md/4772426/19_p049.pdf) — Yoshioka, Kyushu University (2022). Analysis of the 2019 Japan–Korea materials-export dispute.
- [Competing at the upstream of innovation: the US–China balance in critical minerals](https://dair.nps.edu/handle/123456789/5404) — Picarsic & de La Bruyère, Naval Postgraduate School (2025). Upstream (materials/chemicals) leverage in the chip race.

## Databases & data sources
- [SEMI market data](https://www.semi.org/en/products-services/market-data) — SEMI. Industry statistics on materials, equipment and fab capacity.
- [UN Comtrade](https://comtradeplus.un.org/) — UN. Trade in photographic/photoresist chemical preparations and specialty gases by HS code.
- [CEPII BACI](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. Mirror-reconciled HS6 trade flows.

## Industry & trade associations
- [SEMI](https://www.semi.org/) — SEMI. The global industry association for semiconductor materials and equipment supply chains.
- [Semiconductor Industry Association (SIA)](https://www.semiconductors.org/) — SIA. US industry body; supply-chain reports and policy analysis.

## Analysis, think-tanks & reporting
- [CSET (Georgetown) — semiconductor supply chain](https://cset.georgetown.edu/publications/) — Center for Security and Emerging Technology. Deep analyses of the chip supply chain, including materials/chemicals chokepoints.
- [CSIS — Critical Minerals / semiconductor supply chains](https://www.csis.org/programs/critical-minerals-security-program) — CSIS. Ongoing analysis of chip and materials supply-chain security.
