# Tungsten chain — research library

You can't machine metal without tungsten — China has mined ~80% for decades and added it to its export-control list in Feb 2025. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Tungsten](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-tungsten.pdf) — USGS (2026). Annual world mine production, reserves, uses and prices; China ~80% of mine output.
- [BGS World Mineral Statistics — tungsten](https://www2.bgs.ac.uk/mineralsuk/statistics/worldStatistics.html) — BGS (2024). Long-run mine-production series by country.
- [International Tungsten Industry Association (ITIA)](https://www.itia.info/) — ITIA (2024). Uses, supply and the APT/carbide value chain.
- [CEPII BACI V202601](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. Harmonised bilateral HS6 trade flows (ore 261100, APT 284180, unwrought 810194).

## Government & agency reports
- [USGS — Tungsten Statistics and Information](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/tungsten-statistics-and-information) — USGS. Hub for annual and historical tungsten statistics and commodity summaries.
- [USGS — About the 2025 List of Critical Minerals](https://www.usgs.gov/programs/mineral-resources-program/science/about-2025-list-critical-minerals) — USGS (2025). Methodology (trade-disruption/GDP-loss) behind the US critical-minerals list, which includes tungsten.
- [European Commission — Critical Raw Materials](https://single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu/sectors/raw-materials/areas-specific-interest/critical-raw-materials_en) — European Commission (2023). EU CRM list and assessment; tungsten is a listed CRM.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [Criticality of non-fuel minerals: a review of major approaches and analyses](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/abs/10.1021/es200563g) — Erdmann & Graedel, Environmental Science & Technology (2011). The foundational review of criticality methodologies (tungsten a recurring case).
- [A dynamic perspective of the geopolitical supply risk of metals](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S095965261630590X) — Habib, Hamelin & Wenzel, Journal of Cleaner Production (2016). Time-series geopolitical supply-risk model covering tungsten.
- [Methodology and technical input for the 2021 review and revision of the US Critical Minerals List](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/ofr20211045) — Nassar & Fortier, USGS Open-File Report (2021). The quantitative basis for listing tungsten and peers.
- [From Tungsten to Lithium: Eight Decades of Critical Raw Materials Frameworks…](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/18/15/7972) — Ruiz-Navas et al., Sustainability / MDPI (2026). History of EU/US criticality frameworks, anchored on tungsten.

## Databases & data sources
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries series](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/) — USGS. Annual by-commodity production/reserves/trade data.
- [BGS World Mineral Statistics](https://www2.bgs.ac.uk/mineralsuk/statistics/worldStatistics.html) — BGS. Long-run mine-production database.
- [UN Comtrade](https://comtradeplus.un.org/) — UN. Bilateral trade in tungsten ores, APT and unwrought tungsten by HS code.
- [CEPII BACI](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII. Mirror-reconciled HS6 trade flows built from Comtrade.

## Industry & trade associations
- [International Tungsten Industry Association (ITIA)](https://www.itia.info/) — ITIA. The primary industry body for tungsten uses, recycling and the carbide value chain.

## Analysis, think-tanks & reporting
- [CSIS — Critical Minerals Security Program](https://www.csis.org/programs/critical-minerals-security-program) — CSIS. Ongoing analysis of minerals security, export controls and diplomacy relevant to tungsten.
