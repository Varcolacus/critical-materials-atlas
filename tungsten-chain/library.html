Research Library · Tungsten

Tungsten / carbide & defence chain

A curated reference library of public sources for deeper study — gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source and carries an abstract (or factual summary) plus the data it rests on. A research-support layer of the Critical Materials Atlas.

Tungsten carbide makes the cutting tools, drill bits and wear parts that machine almost everything else — and, in dense heavy alloys, the armour-piercing penetrators that make it a defence metal. Yet the supply narrows at the very first step. Here the chokepoint IS the mine — China has held about 80% for decades — and the processing behind it.

Ordered to follow the chain: ① Mine (scheelite / wolframite ore, China ~80%) → ② APT (ammonium paratungstate, the traded intermediate) → ③ tungsten carbide / metal powder → ④ cutting tools & penetrators — then the cross-cutting criticality frameworks and trade data that sit across every stage. The February-2025 export-control lever runs through all four.

① Mine — ore & the decades-long ~80% 3

The raw input — scheelite and wolframite ore, ~80% China and stable for decades.
  • USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Tungsten · USGS (2026) Annual world tungsten mine production, reserves, uses and prices by country. Abstract. The MCS 2026 tungsten PDF returned HTTP 403 on two passes — not retrieved directly this pass. The chapter is USGS's annual tungsten reference: world mine production and reserves by country (China the dominant producer), cemented carbides as the leading use, plus US net import reliance and prices. Data. USGS annual producer canvass and reported trade/price data; corroborated by the USGS tungsten statistics hub (below).
  • BGS World Mineral Statistics · British Geological Survey (2024) Long-run by-country mine-production series for tungsten and 70+ commodities. Summary. BGS database of world mineral production for more than 70 commodities by country, spanning 1913 to the present (latest edition 2020–2024). Compiled from government departments, statistical offices, commodity authorities and company reports through a global contact network; available as PDF, Excel and a JSON API. Data. Compiled national production returns, cross-checked through international specialist groups — the long-run tungsten mine series behind the history chart.
  • USGS — Tungsten Statistics and Information · USGS USGS hub for annual and historical tungsten statistics and commodity summaries. Summary. USGS National Minerals Information Center hub for tungsten: Mineral Commodity Summaries (from 1996), Minerals Yearbooks (from 1994) and historical statistics back to 1932, tracking supply, demand and material flows. States cemented carbides (hardmetals) are the leading wear-resistant use. Data. USGS / legacy Bureau of Mines mineral statistics, in PDF and Excel series.

② Processing to tools — APT, carbide & defence 1

Where ore becomes the traded intermediate and the tool: APT, tungsten carbide, and the defence end-use.
  • International Tungsten Industry Association (ITIA) · ITIA (2024) Primary industry body for tungsten uses, supply, recycling and the carbide value chain. Summary. Not-for-profit tungsten trade association spanning the whole value chain — miners, converters, consumers, recyclers, traders. Covers applications (automotive, aerospace, mining, construction, electronics, defence), the tungsten-carbide intermediate chain, recycling/circularity, and health/environment; publishes industry data and guidance. Data. Member-reported industry information and association publications — no independent primary dataset.

③ From dominance to lever — export controls 1

A decades-old dominance turned into an active policy instrument in February 2025.
  • CSIS Critical Minerals Security Program · CSIS Ongoing think-tank research hub on minerals security, export controls and diplomacy. Summary. CSIS initiative on US critical-minerals supply-chain vulnerability, countering Chinese dominance through domestic capacity, allied partnerships and export-security analysis. Produces commentary, country reports, a minerals-diplomacy explainer series, newsletters and podcasts (directed by Baskaran & Horvath). Data. Policy analysis and commentary — no primary dataset.

Across the chain — criticality frameworks & policy 6

List methodologies and criticality studies that score tungsten across the EU and US frameworks.
  • About the 2025 List of Critical Minerals · USGS (2025) Methodology behind the US critical-minerals list, which includes tungsten. Summary. USGS page on the final 2025 critical-minerals list — 60 minerals (2022's 50 plus 10). Built from an economic model over 84 commodities, 400+ industries and 1,200+ trade-disruption scenarios, risk-weighting likelihood × impact. Tungsten is listed among the highest-risk commodities. Data. USGS economic supply-disruption model on 2023 MCS / Minerals Yearbook data; 1,200+ scenarios plus single-point-of-failure analysis.
  • Critical Raw Materials · European Commission (2023) The EU CRM list and assessment; tungsten is a listed critical raw material. Summary. European Commission CRM page: the 2023 list names 34 critical raw materials, scored on Economic Importance × Supply Risk at the supply-chain bottleneck (extraction or processing). Tungsten is a confirmed EU CRM; the CRM Act adds strategic-raw-material targets and supply-diversification goals. Data. EU criticality methodology (Economic Importance × Supply Risk) over candidate materials using production, trade, substitution and governance data.
  • Criticality of Non-Fuel Minerals: A Review of Major Approaches and Analyses · Erdmann & Graedel, Environmental Science & Technology (2011) Foundational review of criticality methodologies (tungsten a recurring case). Abstract. The article page returned HTTP 403 and the abstract is elided by the publisher — not retrieved. By title and citation record: a review comparing the major mineral-criticality approaches and their supply-risk and vulnerability indicators — one of the field's foundational methodology surveys. Data. Literature review / methodological comparison — no primary dataset.
  • A dynamic perspective of the geopolitical supply risk of metals · Habib, Hamelin & Wenzel, Journal of Cleaner Production (2016) Time-series geopolitical supply-risk model covering tungsten. Abstract. ScienceDirect / Elsevier returned HTTP 403 — abstract not retrieved. By title: develops a dynamic, time-series measure of geopolitical supply risk for metals, combining production concentration with country-governance indicators to track how risk evolves over time. Data. Production-concentration (HHI-type) and country-governance indicators over a time series — no new experimental dataset.
  • Methodology and Technical Input for the 2021 Review and Revision of the U.S. Critical Minerals List · Nassar & Fortier, USGS Open-File Report (2021) The quantitative basis for listing tungsten and its peers. Abstract. pubs.usgs.gov returned HTTP 403 on retry — not retrieved. By title: the USGS Open-File Report giving the methodology and technical input for the 2021 review of the US Critical Minerals List — the quantitative supply-risk indicators used to screen commodities including tungsten. Data. USGS supply-risk indicators (production concentration, import reliance, trade) — a methodology report, not a dataset.
  • From Tungsten to Lithium: Eight Decades of Critical Raw Materials Frameworks · Ruiz-Navas et al., Sustainability / MDPI (2026) History of EU/US criticality frameworks, anchored on tungsten. Abstract. MDPI returned HTTP 403 on two passes — abstract not retrieved. By title: an eight-decade review of EU and US critical-raw-materials frameworks, using tungsten as the anchor case to trace how criticality assessment evolved "from tungsten to lithium." Data. Historical review of policy and framework documents — no primary dataset.

Across the chain — trade & commodity data 3

The datasets the chain's own figures are built from.
  • CEPII BACI V202601, based on UN Comtrade · CEPII Harmonised bilateral HS6 trade flows (ore 261100, tungstates/APT 284180, unwrought 810194). Summary. CEPII database: reconciled bilateral trade flows for ~200 countries across ~5,000 HS6 products, 1995–2024, derived from UN Comtrade by converting CIF imports to FOB and reliability-weighting reporter discrepancies into one figure per flow (version 202601, January 2026). Data. UN Comtrade declarations, HS6 bilateral, mirror-reconciled (CIF/FOB, reporter-reliability weighting) — the ore / APT / unwrought-tungsten lines in the trade table.
  • USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries series · USGS Annual by-commodity production/reserves/trade data (tungsten and related commodities). Summary. The MCS 2026 index returned HTTP 403 this pass. USGS's annual Mineral Commodity Summaries: one downloadable chapter per commodity plus the full report and appendixes — the reference series for domestic and world mineral production, supply and use. Data. USGS annual mineral-commodity statistics (production, imports, reserves, use) — the production engine behind the mine-share figures.
  • UN Comtrade · United Nations Official UN bilateral trade statistics in tungsten ores, APT and unwrought tungsten by HS code. Summary. The Comtrade portal serves a JavaScript app shell — content not retrievable by fetch. UN Comtrade is the United Nations' repository of official international trade statistics — reporter-declared imports and exports by HS commodity and partner, the raw declarations that BACI reconciles. Data. Member-state customs declarations by HS code and partner — the un-reconciled source behind BACI.

Gaps & good next searches

  • The analysis / reporting layer is thin: this library leans on canonical agency and database pages plus peer-reviewed criticality papers, not trade-press. Next: "tungsten APT carbide supply chain Resources Policy", "tungsten export control 2025 CSIS / Benchmark Mineral Intelligence", "ammonium paratungstate market Fastmarkets".
  • APT and carbide processing capacity has no clean public country-level series — the processing chokepoint (§1) is asserted from ITIA/USGS qualitative statements, not a capacity dataset. A hardmetal/APT capacity source would harden the "processing sits there too" claim.
  • Tungsten's HS codes (261100 ore, 284180 tungstates/APT, 810194 unwrought) capture ore and intermediates but not finished carbide tools or the tungsten in munitions — the most concentrated end of the chain is invisible in trade.
  • Tungsten-specific criticality studies (as opposed to general reviews) deserve a dedicated pass: Resources Policy and Resources, Conservation & Recycling for "tungsten criticality substitution".
  • A tungsten recycling / hard-metal scrap review would quantify how much secondary supply offsets the mine — currently only qualitative in the record.
