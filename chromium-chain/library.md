# Chromium chain — research library

There is no stainless steel without chromium — and the ferrochrome smelting step, ~40% electricity by cost, has migrated to cheap power in Kazakhstan and China even as the chromite ore itself stays concentrated in southern Africa. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Chromium](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-chromium.pdf) — USGS (2026). Annual chromite mine and ferrochrome production, reserves.
- [BGS World Mineral Statistics — chromium (chromite)](https://www2.bgs.ac.uk/mineralsuk/statistics/worldStatistics.html) — British Geological Survey (2024). Long-run mine-production series.
- International Chromium Development Association — uses and supply — cited as `https://www.icdachromium.com/` in the chain's own sources, but that domain no longer resolves to the association (see Gaps below).
- [CEPII BACI V202601](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII, based on UN Comtrade (2026). Bilateral trade database used for the chain's trade panel.

## Government & agency reports
- [Chromium Statistics and Information](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/chromium-statistics-and-information) — USGS National Minerals Information Center. Chromite/ferrochromium supply, demand and strategic-mineral framing; links to Mineral Commodity Summaries (1996–2026), Minerals Yearbook (1994–2023) and a dedicated Chromium Life Cycle Study.
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025 — Chromium](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025-chromium.pdf) — USGS (2025). Prior-year annual summary for year-over-year comparison.
- [Substance Information — Chromium(VI) compounds](https://echa.europa.eu/substance-information/-/substanceinfo/100.014.189) — European Chemicals Agency (ECHA). EU regulatory database entry on hexavalent chromium, relevant to plating/toxicity regulation.
- [Scientific Opinion on Dietary Reference Values for Chromium](https://doi.org/10.2903/j.efsa.2014.3845) — European Food Safety Authority, *EFSA Journal* (2014). EU agency scientific opinion on chromium as a dietary trace element (context for chromium's biological role vs. industrial toxicity).

## Academic papers & reviews
- [Chromium and genomic stability](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mrfmmm.2011.12.002) — *Mutation Research/Fundamental and Molecular Mechanisms of Mutagenesis* (2012). Peer-reviewed review of hexavalent chromium toxicology, relevant to plating and smelting exposure.
