# Cobalt chain — research library

Cobalt has a mine problem in one country and a refining problem in another: roughly three-quarters is mined in the DR Congo (with a real artisanal-mining and child-labour dimension) and roughly three-quarters is refined in China, wherever it was dug. A curated reference library for deeper study — public sources, gathered August 2026. Each entry links to a real source.

## Core references (already cited in the chain)
- [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026 — Cobalt](https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026-cobalt.pdf) — USGS (2026). Annual mine production, reserves, US/world shares.
- [IEA, Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2025](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2025/executive-summary) — IEA (2025). Refining-concentration and demand-outlook analysis.
- [BGS World Mineral Statistics — cobalt mine production](https://www2.bgs.ac.uk/mineralsuk/statistics/worldStatistics.html) — British Geological Survey (2024). Long-run mine-production series by country.
- [CEPII BACI V202601](https://www.cepii.fr/CEPII/en/bdd_modele/bdd_modele_item.asp?id=37) — CEPII, based on UN Comtrade (2026). Bilateral trade database used for the chain's trade panel.

## Government & agency reports
- [Cobalt Statistics and Information](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/cobalt-statistics-and-information) — USGS National Minerals Information Center. Global supply/demand/material-flow data; links to a dedicated Critical Minerals Review 2021, "Cobalt Deposits in the United States," and "Ni-Co Laterite Deposits of the World."
- [Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2025 — Executive summary](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2025/executive-summary) — IEA (2025, already core). Confirms refining concentration worsened 2020–2024 (top-3 refiners' share 82%→86%) and notes the DRC's Feb-2025 four-month cobalt-export suspension.

## Academic papers & reviews
- [China's domestic and foreign influence in the global cobalt supply chain](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.resourpol.2019.03.015) — *Resources Policy* (2019). Peer-reviewed analysis of China's refining dominance — directly on this chain's second chokepoint.
- [A non-academic perspective on the future of lithium-based batteries](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-35933-2) — *Nature Communications* (2023). Peer-reviewed outlook on battery chemistry trends, relevant to the LFP/low-cobalt "engineered down" trend the chain describes.

## Databases & data sources
- [Cobalt Institute — Cobalt Data Room](https://www.cobaltinstitute.org/) — Cobalt Institute. Industry production, demand and recycling data, incl. the figure that DRC supplies ~76% of mined cobalt and Indonesia ~12%.
- [Cobalt Statistics and Information](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/cobalt-statistics-and-information) — USGS. Primary production/reserves/trade data portal (monthly + annual series).

## Industry & trade associations
- [Cobalt Institute](https://www.cobaltinstitute.org/) — Cobalt Institute. Global industry association covering the full cobalt value chain; sections on industrial and artisanal/small-scale mining, responsible-sourcing standards, and a Cobalt Learning Group.

## Analysis, think-tanks & reporting
- [Cobalt mining for lithium ion batteries has a high human cost](https://www.washingtonpost.com/graphics/business/batteries/congo-cobalt-mining-for-lithium-ion-battery/) — The Washington Post (2016). In-depth investigative reporting on DRC artisanal cobalt mining conditions.
- [Child labour behind smart phone and electric car batteries](https://www.amnesty.org/en/latest/news/2016/01/child-labour-behind-smart-phone-and-electric-car-batteries/) — Amnesty International (2016). Human-rights investigation into DRC cobalt supply chains and battery-maker due diligence.
